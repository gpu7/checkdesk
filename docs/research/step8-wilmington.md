## Method

- **USPS:** official USPS Location Finder API (`tools.usps.com`, `requestType` po). Official Wilmington ZIPs came from ZIP-Codes.com search.asp (the `de-wilmington` city-page slug is not a collision). Search title says "25 ZIP Codes in Wilmington, DE"; the city page and search.asp have the same 25 unique codes, all listed Wilmington (10 Standard, 3 P.O. Box, 12 Unique). Neighborhood slugs `de-elsmere`, `de-newport`, `de-bellefonte`, `de-arden`, `de-ardencroft`, `de-ardentown`, `de-greenville`, `de-talleyville`, `de-stanton`, `de-marshallton`, and `de-edgemoor` collided with the Wilmington city page; those ZIPs came from search.asp. Pass 1: 5 and 15 miles around 77 ZIPs (154 queries, 279 unique records, 9 printed Wilmington). Pass 2 added 1, 2, 3, 10, and 20 mile widenings around 43 official and edge ZIPs (215 queries, 16 added records, no new printed-Wilmington offices). Total 295 records (`step8-browser/wilmington-usps-locator-2026-11-15.jsonl`). Official location pages were fetched for all 295 records (all HTTP 200 on the first try); each page's embedded `poDetail` matched the API record. Full notes: `step8-browser/wilmington-usps-2026-11-15.md`.
- **Walmart:** not collected.
- **City limits:** U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, address and coordinates endpoints, Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties, matching Wilmington city GEOID 1077580 only. All 295 addresses and official locator map points on both benchmarks (`step8-browser/wilmington-usps-geocoder-2026-11-15.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used. No map-point fallbacks. Rodney Sq unit STE 1 was dropped; the geocoder matched 500 DELAWARE AVE, still in the city. Seven printed-Wilmington offices geocode outside the city (unincorporated New Castle County, Greenville CDP, or Edgemoor CDP) and are held.
- Fees reuse existing rows in `data/providers/fees.csv`. Coordinates for USPS rows are in the notes column (lat/lng columns stay blank).
