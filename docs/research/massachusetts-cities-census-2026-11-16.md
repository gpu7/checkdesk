# Massachusetts: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, and Maryland. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. Massachusetts is a New England state: it is partitioned into minor civil divisions (SUMLEV 061), and many municipalities are towns that have no SUMLEV 162 row. This ranking uses **only SUMLEV 162 incorporated places**, the same method as Connecticut and Maine. Massachusetts towns that are not also incorporated places are excluded; see Quirks. The order is not ambiguous (see Quirks and the cutoff note below). There are no SUMLEV 170 or 172 consolidated-city rows in this state file, and none of the five is a consolidated government. Hours on the city pages are Eastern Time for all five (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: Massachusetts after Maryland. Do not start Michigan from this run.

## Quirks

- **Use SUMLEV 162 incorporated places only, not the town (MCD) ranking.** Massachusetts is partitioned into minor civil divisions (SUMLEV 061; 351 rows in this file): 58 cities and 293 towns. Towns are incorporated municipalities in Massachusetts law, but those 293 towns have **no** SUMLEV 162 row in this Census vintage. Those towns are excluded from this ranking, the same way Connecticut and Maine towns that are not also incorporated places were excluded. The largest such towns are Plymouth town (67,846, in Plymouth County) and Brookline town (63,414, in Norfolk County), then Arlington town (46,563, in Middlesex County). All of them are smaller than Lowell (119,971) and smaller than Brockton (106,134), so even a combined city-and-town list would have the same top five. Towns without a SUMLEV 162 row stay out of scope. This does not make the ranking ambiguous.
- **City and coextensive county subdivision.** Each of the 58 cities is also represented as a county subdivision. For all five places in this ranking, the file carries a SUMLEV 162 “X city” row (FUNCSTAT A), a SUMLEV 061 “X city” row (FUNCSTAT F) with the same population, and a SUMLEV 071 place-part row (FUNCSTAT A) equal to the whole-place total, in one county. Ranking and city-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 place, not the MCD row. Check Desk pages use the city name (Boston, not “Boston city” as a legal-style label in running text except where the Census NAME is quoted). Do not double-count the city and the MCD.
- **“Town city” legal names.** Of the 58 SUMLEV 162 rows, 45 are published as “X city” and 13 as “X Town city” (Agawam, Amherst, Barnstable, Braintree, Bridgewater, Franklin, North Attleborough, Palmer, Randolph, Southbridge, West Springfield, Weymouth, and Winthrop). Those 13 adopted a city form of government and kept “Town” in the legal name. The largest is Weymouth Town city (59,759), 21st in this ranking and well outside the five. None of the five uses that naming.
- **Only 58 incorporated places, all cities, all FUNCSTAT A.** The official Excel table has 58 place rows, matching the CSV. There are no boroughs, villages, or “city (balance)” rows in this file.
- **No SUMLEV 170 or 172 rows.** The Massachusetts Vintage 2025 state file has none. None of the five is a consolidated city-county government.
- **Cambridge and Lowell are separate places in the same county.** Both are in this five, both in Middlesex County. Do not merge the pair, and do not fold other Middlesex cities into either list.
- **West Springfield is not Springfield.** West Springfield Town city is a separate SUMLEV 162 place in Hampden County and stays off the Springfield list.
- **County governments and FUNCSTAT N.** Nine of the 14 SUMLEV 050 county rows are FUNCSTAT N (a nonfunctioning legal entity), including all four home counties of this five. That is a county-government status in this vintage; it does not change the incorporated-place ranking or the city-limits checks, which use the SUMLEV 162 place.
- **CDPs are excluded.** This SUB-IP / sub-est file is incorporated places only; no CDP names appear. Those CDPs are out of scope, consistent with earlier states.
- **Whole place, not county parts.** Ranking uses the SUMLEV 162 whole-place total, consistent with earlier states. Every SUMLEV 162 row has COUNTY = 000. Each of the five has a single SUMLEV 157 part equal to the whole-place total, in one county, as noted in the table.

## Time zones

All of Massachusetts is in the eastern standard time zone under **49 CFR 71.4**. That section states that the eastern zone “includes that part of the United States that is west of 67°30″ W. longitude and east of the boundary line described in § 71.5, and includes all of the State of Maine, but does not include any part of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.” The Eastern–Central boundary in **49 CFR 71.5** is described through Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee, and Georgia to the Florida–Georgia line and does not enter Massachusetts. None of the five cities sits on a zone boundary. Therefore Boston, Worcester, Springfield, Cambridge, and Lowell are all labeled **Eastern Time**.
