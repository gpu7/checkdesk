# New York: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-17 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, and New Mexico. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. This ranking uses **only SUMLEV 162 incorporated places**. Census-designated places (CDPs) are not in this file and are excluded. **New York towns are county subdivisions (SUMLEV 061), not incorporated places, and must not be ranked or listed**, even when they are larger than Syracuse (see Quirks). Offices that print New York, Buffalo, Yonkers, Rochester, or Syracuse but geocode in a town, village, other city, CDP, or any other place that is not the target city must be held. The five borough names and Queens neighborhood names are not a pending exception: the neighborhood-named office rule is **not approved**, so offices whose printed city is Brooklyn, Bronx, Staten Island, Flushing, Jamaica, or another name that is not the place name must be held even when they geocode inside New York city. List only offices whose printed city matches the place name (for New York city, `New York`). The order is not ambiguous (see Quirks and the cutoff note below). There are no SUMLEV 170 or 172 consolidated-city rows in this state file, and the CONCIT field is `00000` on every row. New York city is a single SUMLEV 162 incorporated place (GEOID 3651000) with five county parts. Hours on the city pages are Eastern Time for all five (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: New York after New Mexico. Do not start North Carolina from this run.

## Quirks

- **Towns are not SUMLEV 162 places.** New York towns are county subdivisions (SUMLEV 061). This state file has 1,009 SUMLEV 061 rows and no town-suffixed SUMLEV 162 rows. Eight towns are larger than Syracuse city and are still excluded: Hempstead town (795,734, Nassau County), Brookhaven town (496,410, Suffolk), Islip town (340,867, Suffolk), Oyster Bay town (301,115, Nassau), North Hempstead town (238,982, Nassau), Babylon town (219,766, Suffolk), Huntington town (205,891, Suffolk), and Ramapo town (161,564, Rockland). Hempstead village (59,819) is a different SUMLEV 162 place and is also outside this five. Amherst town (130,442, Erie County) and Smithtown town (116,847, Suffolk) are also excluded as towns. Offices that geocode in those towns, or in the Balance of a county, must be held.
- **New York city is one place, not five.** There are no SUMLEV 170 or 172 rows and no non-zero CONCIT values. Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole-place GEOID 3651000. The five borough counties are legal counties with FUNCSTAT C, and each is coextensive with one New York city part. They are not separate incorporated places in this ranking.
- **Printed city for New York is `New York` only.** The pending rule decision on neighborhood-named offices is not approved. Offices that print Brooklyn, Bronx, Staten Island, Flushing, Jamaica, Astoria, Manhattan, New York City, or any other string that is not `New York` are held even if every Census geocode leg falls inside GEOID 3651000. Report the held count by printed name. Buffalo, Yonkers, Rochester, and Syracuse use the ordinary printed-city match (`BUFFALO`, `YONKERS`, `ROCHESTER`, `SYRACUSE`) in New York state.
- **Other incorporated places in the home counties stay off these lists.** The five boroughs have no other incorporated places. Erie County also has Lackawanna city, Tonawanda city, and the villages of Kenmore, Depew, Lancaster, Hamburg, East Aurora, Williamsville, Springville, Sloan, Orchard Park, Akron, Alden, Blasdell, Angola, North Collins, Gowanda (part), and Farnham, plus the Balance of Erie County (553,422). Westchester County also has New Rochelle city, Mount Vernon city, White Plains city, Peekskill city, Rye city, and the villages of Port Chester, Harrison, Ossining, Mamaroneck, Scarsdale, Tarrytown, Dobbs Ferry, Sleepy Hollow, Mount Kisco, Rye Brook, Hastings-on-Hudson, Croton-on-Hudson, Pelham, Briarcliff Manor, Pleasantville, Tuckahoe, Larchmont, Irvington, Bronxville, Pelham Manor, Elmsford, Ardsley, and Buchanan, plus the Balance of Westchester County (269,142). Monroe County also has the villages of Brockport, East Rochester, Hilton, Webster, Fairport, Spencerport, Honeoye Falls, Churchville, Scottsville, and Pittsford, plus the Balance of Monroe County (501,686). Onondaga County also has the villages of Baldwinsville, North Syracuse, Solvay, Manlius, Fayetteville, Minoa, East Syracuse, Skaneateles, Liverpool, Marcellus, Camillus, Jordan, Elbridge, Tully, and Fabius, plus the Balance of Onondaga County (274,492).
- **Out-of-state offices.** Home counties of this five are directly adjacent, including water boundaries in Census `county_adjacency.txt`, to Bergen, Hudson, Middlesex, Monmouth, and Union Counties, NJ, and to Fairfield County, CT. Erie County, NY, is not itself adjacent to Pennsylvania in that file; in-state adjacent Cattaraugus and Chautauqua Counties do border McKean, Warren, and Erie Counties, PA, and those further Pennsylvania counties are not added to the harvest. In-state adjacent Nassau and Putnam also border Fairfield County, CT, and Rockland also borders Bergen and Passaic Counties, NJ; those further out-of-state counties are not added beyond the home-county adjacencies already listed. Vermont and Massachusetts do not touch a home county of this five. Offices in NJ, CT, PA, VT, and MA must not be listed on a New York city page.
- **No CDPs and no unincorporated listings.** CDPs are not in this file. Hold any office whose printed city and geocode disagree, including a neighborhood-named or borough-named office, a CDP, or an unincorporated area.

## Time zones

All five cities are in the Eastern time zone. 49 CFR 71.4 (Eastern zone) includes that part of the United States west of 67°30′ W. longitude and east of the boundary line described in § 71.5, and includes all of the State of Maine, but does not include any part of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico. Section 71.5 (boundary line between eastern and central zones) describes that boundary through Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee, and onward; it does not mention New York and does not enter the state. Hours on the New York, Buffalo, Yonkers, Rochester, and Syracuse pages are labeled Eastern Time.

## Name collisions

New York, Buffalo, Yonkers, Rochester, and Syracuse should be treated as the New York Census places above (GEOID 36 + PLACE FIPS). **Rochester, New York, is not Rochester, Minnesota, and is not Rochester, New Hampshire.** The live sitemap already has `/rochester/` (Rochester, Minnesota) and `/rochester-nh/` (Rochester, New Hampshire), so the New York slug is `/rochester-ny/`. Do not edit the existing Minnesota or New Hampshire Rochester pages except the home and `/cities/` meta and list lines that add each new New York city. `/new-york/`, `/buffalo/`, `/yonkers/`, and `/syracuse/` were free on the live sitemap (545 URLs) at ranking time and are unsuffixed. New York is not a borough name, not Jersey City, and not Newark. Buffalo, New York, is not Lackawanna, Tonawanda, or a later Buffalo in another state; the unsuffixed slug is taken by this Buffalo. Yonkers is not Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, or The Bronx. Syracuse is not a village in Onondaga County. If a later state ships a colliding city name, that later city would take a state-suffix slug; do not change these New York slugs then, and do not edit any existing non-New-York page in this run except the home and `/cities/` meta and list lines that add each new New York city.
