# Kansas: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, and Iowa. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. Kansas uses incorporated places (SUMLEV 162) and excludes CDPs. For Kansas City, ranking and city-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 place row “Kansas City city” (GEOID 2036000, 157,805), not Wyandotte County as a whole (170,597; FUNCSTAT C). Census does not publish a “Kansas City city (balance)” label or a SUMLEV 170 consolidated-city row for this government; the method is the same as Augusta (balance GEOID 1304204) and Indianapolis (balance GEOID 1836003): use the place remainder, not the county total. Hours on the city pages are Central Time for all five (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: Kansas after Iowa. Do not start Kentucky from this run.

## Quirks

Kansas has two FUNCSTAT C counties in this file: Wyandotte County and Greeley County. The file’s summary levels are 040 (state), 050 (county), 061 (county subdivision), 071 (county subdivision remainder / place part), 157 (place-county part), 162 (incorporated place), 170 (consolidated city), and 172 (consolidated-city part). The only SUMLEV 170 / 172 rows are for Greeley County unified government (1,089), its balance (380), and Tribune city. There is no SUMLEV 170 row for Kansas City or Wyandotte County.

**Kansas City, KS: use the SUMLEV 162 place, not the county.** Ranking and city-limits checks use “Kansas City city” (GEOID 2036000, 157,805), the incorporated remainder of the Unified Government of Wyandotte County and Kansas City, Kansas. That is the same method used for Augusta (SUMLEV 162 balance GEOID 1304204) and Indianapolis (SUMLEV 162 balance GEOID 1836003): do not use the FUNCSTAT C county total (170,597) and do not fold in the other incorporated places that remain in the county (Bonner Springs, Edwardsville, and Lake Quivira). Unlike Augusta and Indianapolis, Census does not label this row “(balance)” and does not publish a companion SUMLEV 170 consolidated-city total for Kansas City. The SUMLEV 162 row is FUNCSTAT A.

**Greeley County is a different, small unified government.** The SUMLEV 170 “Greeley County unified government” row is 1,089. The SUMLEV 162 / 172 balance is 380 (PLACE 28412, FUNCSTAT F). Tribune city (709; FUNCSTAT N on the SUMLEV 162 row) and Horace city (93) are the other places in that county. Greeley County (GEOID 20071) is in far western Kansas and is not near this five. It is also not Greeley city (281; PLACE 28350) in Anderson County, and not Greeley, Colorado, which already has the `/greeley/` page on Check Desk.

**Whole place, not county parts.** Ranking uses the SUMLEV 162 whole-place total for every Kansas place. All five of this run are single-county. City-limits checks for the five use only each city’s own GEOID (2079000, 2053775, 2036000, 2052575, 2071000).

**CDPs are excluded.** This SUB-IP / sub-est file is incorporated places only; no CDP names appear. Nearby CDPs must not be folded into a city list, consistent with earlier states. The Hawaii CDP exception does not apply here.

**Legal types.** Of the 625 SUMLEV 162 rows, 624 are cities (the NAME of each ends in “ city”) and one is a unified-government balance (Greeley County unified government (balance), FUNCSTAT F). Tribune city is the only FUNCSTAT N SUMLEV 162 row. All five of the ranking are incorporated cities with FUNCSTAT A. Kansas does not use village or town as a Census legal type in this file.

**Slug collision for Kansas City only.** Existing Check Desk location slugs were checked on 2026-11-16. None of `/wichita/`, `/overland-park/`, `/kansas-city/`, `/kansas-city-ks/`, `/olathe/`, or `/topeka/` is in use. Kansas City, Missouri, is a later state in this plan and would collide with a bare `/kansas-city/` slug, so Kansas City, Kansas, uses `/kansas-city-ks/` (precedent: `/aurora-il/`). The other four do not need a state-suffix slug. Lawrence is outside this five; if it is shipped later it should be checked for collisions with other Lawrences.

**Name collisions and nearby places that must not be folded in.** Wichita is not Derby, Haysville, Bel Aire, Park City, Maize, Valley Center, Goddard, Mulvane, Eastborough, Andover, or Augusta, Kansas (Butler County; not Augusta, Georgia). ## Time zones

Most of Kansas is in the Central Time zone. Under **49 CFR 71.6**, the Central zone includes that part of the United States west of the boundary in **49 CFR 71.5** and east of the boundary in **49 CFR 71.7**. The Central–Mountain boundary in Kansas is described in **49 CFR 71.7(d) (Kansas-Colorado)**, from the official 2025 eCFR / 49 CFR Subtitle A (10-1-25 Edition) text:

> From the junction of the west line of Hitchcock County, Nebraska, with the Nebraska-Kansas boundary westerly along that boundary to the northwest corner of the State of Kansas; thence southerly along Kansas-Colorado boundary to the north line of Sherman County, Kansas; thence easterly along the north line of Sherman County to the east line of Sherman County; thence southerly along the east line of Sherman County to the north line of Logan County; thence westerly along the north line of Logan County to the east line of Wallace County; thence southerly along the east line of Wallace County to the north line Wichita County; thence westerly along the north line of Wichita County; thence westerly along the north line of Wichita County to the east line of Greeley County; thence southerly along the east lines of Greeley County and Hamilton Counties; thence westerly along the south line of Hamilton County to the Kansas-Colorado boundary; thence southerly along the Kansas Colorado boundary to the junction of that boundary with the north boundary of the State of Oklahoma.

Under **49 CFR 71.8**, the Mountain zone is west of that line. That puts four western Kansas counties in Mountain Time: **Sherman, Wallace, Greeley, and Hamilton**. Cheyenne County (north of Sherman), Stanton and Morton Counties (south of Hamilton), and every other Kansas county, including Sedgwick, Johnson, Wyandotte, and Shawnee, lie east of the line and are in Central Time. Under **49 CFR 71.7(g)**, municipalities on this boundary line are in the Mountain zone (the Murdo, S. Dak., exception is not in Kansas). None of the five cities in this run is in Sherman, Wallace, Greeley, or Hamilton County, and none sits on that boundary. All five are therefore labeled **Central Time**.
