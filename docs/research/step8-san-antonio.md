Official map points also fall in San Antonio city on both files. The original San Antonio-printed rows are unchanged.

Added office (printed city in parentheses): Fort Sam Houston (JBSA FT SAM HOUSTON). The printed city is forced to JBSA FT SAM HOUSTON, not title-cased as Jbsa Ft Sam Houston. The office is on Joint Base San Antonio-Fort Sam Houston, which may limit public access.
