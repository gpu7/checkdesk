# Maryland: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, and Maine. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. Maryland uses incorporated places (SUMLEV 162) and excludes CDPs and unincorporated areas; the no-CDP rule applies. Maryland has many large CDPs (including Columbia, Germantown, and Silver Spring) that are not in this SUB-IP / sub-est file and stay out of scope. Baltimore city is an independent city: ranking and city-limits checks use its SUMLEV 162 place row, which equals the SUMLEV 050 county-equivalent row and the one SUMLEV 157 part. There are no SUMLEV 170 or 172 consolidated-city rows in this state file, and none of the five is a consolidated government. Hours on the city pages are Eastern Time for all five (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: Maryland after Maine. Do not start Massachusetts from this run.

## Quirks

- **Baltimore city is an independent city, not a consolidated government.** Ranking and city-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 place row, not the SUMLEV 050 county-equivalent row, though the two have the same population. Census does not publish a “Baltimore city (balance)” label and does not publish a SUMLEV 170 row for this government (the Maryland file has no SUMLEV 170 rows). Baltimore County is a different county and stays off the Baltimore list.
- **CDPs are excluded.** This SUB-IP / sub-est file is incorporated places only; no CDP names appear. Columbia, Germantown, Silver Spring, Waldorf, Ellicott City, Glen Burnie, Bethesda, Towson, and the other large Maryland CDPs are out of scope, consistent with earlier states. The Hawaii CDP exception was one-time only.
- **Maryland towns and villages in this file are incorporated places.** Unlike Maine and Connecticut, where many towns are only minor civil divisions and have no SUMLEV 162 row, Maryland’s 123 towns and 5 villages in this vintage are SUMLEV 162 rows and are part of the incorporated-place universe. They are still outside this five. All 157 SUMLEV 162 rows are FUNCSTAT A.
- **No SUMLEV 170 or 172 rows.** The Maryland Vintage 2025 state file has none. None of the five is a consolidated city-county government.
- **No SUMLEV 061 or 071 rows.** This state file has no county-subdivision inventory. Ranking uses the SUMLEV 162 place.
- **Gaithersburg and Rockville are separate places.** Both are in this five and both are in Montgomery County. Do not merge them.
- **Mount Airy town spans two counties.** The SUMLEV 162 total is 9,857, with parts in Carroll County (6,299) and Frederick County (3,558). It is not in this five.
- **Whole place, not county parts.** Ranking uses the SUMLEV 162 whole-place total, consistent with earlier states. Every SUMLEV 162 row has COUNTY = 000. Each of the five has a single SUMLEV 157 part equal to the whole-place total, in one county, as noted in the table.
- **“Balance of … County” rows are not places.** The SUMLEV 157 rows with PLACE 99990 and FUNCSTAT S (Balance of Montgomery County 897,552, Balance of Prince George's County 711,456, Balance of Frederick County 172,049) are statistical remainders and are out of scope.
- **Westminster is not in this five.** Westminster city, Maryland (20,589; GEOID 2483100) is 13th in this ranking, in Carroll County. Check Desk already has `/westminster/` for Westminster, Colorado. If Westminster, Maryland is ever added later, it would need a state-suffix slug (`/westminster-md/`). Do not edit `/westminster/` in this run.

## Time zones

All of Maryland is in the eastern standard time zone under **49 CFR 71.4**. That section states that the eastern zone “includes that part of the United States that is west of 67°30″ W. longitude and east of the boundary line described in § 71.5, and includes all of the State of Maine, but does not include any part of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.” The Eastern–Central boundary in **49 CFR 71.5** is described through Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee, and Georgia to the Florida–Georgia line and does not enter Maryland. None of the five cities sits on a zone boundary. Therefore Baltimore, Frederick, Gaithersburg, Rockville, and Bowie are all labeled **Eastern Time**.

Local copy of the official part 71 XML: `/workspace/step8md/CFR-2025-title49-vol1-part71.xml` (govinfo, CFR 2025 title 49 vol. 1, dated 2025-10-01).

## Name collisions

Baltimore, Frederick, Gaithersburg, Rockville, and Bowie should be treated as the Maryland Census places above (GEOID 24 + PLACE FIPS). Baltimore, Maryland, is not Baltimore, Ohio (no existing Check Desk `/baltimore/` page; the Maryland slug is `/baltimore/`). Frederick, Maryland, is not Frederick, Colorado (no existing `/frederick/` page). Gaithersburg has no existing `/gaithersburg/` page. Rockville, Maryland, is not Rockville, Connecticut or another Rockville (no existing `/rockville/` page). Bowie, Maryland, is not Bowie, Texas (no existing `/bowie/` page). Westminster, Maryland, is not in this five and is not Westminster, Colorado (`/westminster/`). Hagerstown, Annapolis, College Park, and Salisbury are also outside this five. Columbia, Germantown, and Silver Spring are CDPs, not incorporated places, and stay out of scope.
