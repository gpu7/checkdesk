## Method

- **USPS:** official USPS Location Finder API (`tools.usps.com`, `requestType` po). Official Hartford ZIPs came from ZIP-Codes.com search.asp (the `ct-hartford` city-page slug is not a collision). Search title says "52 ZIP Codes in Hartford, CT"; the search table has 50 unique codes and the city page has 51 unique; the union is 52. Of the search rows, 34 are listed Hartford. The other 16 are listed as West Hartford, East Hartford, Wethersfield, Newington, or New Hartford and are not treated as official Hartford printed-city ZIPs, but they were included in pass 1. The city page has two codes not in the search table (06183 Unique, 06199 P.O. Box), treated as official Hartford ZIPs. The West Hartford slug `ct-west-hartford` collided with the Hartford city page; West Hartford ZIPs came from search.asp. Neighborhood slugs `ct-blue-hills`, `ct-unity-plaza`, `ct-barry-square`, and `ct-central` also collided with the Hartford city page. Pass 1: 5 and 15 miles around 107 ZIPs (214 queries, 166 unique records, 8 printed Hartford). Pass 2 added 1, 2, 3, 10, and 20 mile widenings around 75 official and edge ZIPs (375 queries, 16 added records, no new printed-Hartford offices). Total 182 records (`step8-browser/hartford-usps-locator-2026-11-15.jsonl`). Official location pages were fetched for all 182 records (all HTTP 200 on the first try); each page's embedded `poDetail` matched the API record. Full notes: `step8-browser/hartford-usps-2026-11-15.md`.
- **Walmart:** not collected.
- **City limits:** U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, address and coordinates endpoints, Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties, matching Hartford city GEOID 0937000 only. All 182 addresses and official locator map points on both benchmarks (`step8-browser/hartford-usps-geocoder-2026-11-15.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used. Old State House (80 State House Sq) needed the map-point fallback after street-name retries; shortening to State St snapped to a different street and was rejected. The official map point is in Hartford city on both files. Hartford Post Office official ZIP 06101 vs 2020 match ZIP 06120 is the same street and still in the city (not a fallback).
- Fees reuse existing rows in `data/providers/fees.csv`. Coordinates for USPS rows are in the notes column (lat/lng columns stay blank).
