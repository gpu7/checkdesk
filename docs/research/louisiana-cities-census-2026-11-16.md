# Louisiana: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, and Kentucky. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. Louisiana uses incorporated places (SUMLEV 162) and excludes CDPs. New Orleans is coextensive with Orleans Parish. Baton Rouge and Lafayette are the municipal (SUMLEV 162) place rows of partially consolidated city-parish governments; ranking and city-limits checks use those place rows, not the parish totals, the same way Augusta, Indianapolis, and Louisville used their SUMLEV 162 place or balance rows rather than the county or consolidated-city total. Hours on the city pages are Central Time for all five (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: Louisiana after Kentucky. Do not start Maine from this run.

The state total (SUMLEV 040) in the same CSV is **4,618,189**. Louisiana has 64 parishes (SUMLEV 050). Sixty-one are FUNCSTAT A. The two FUNCSTAT B parishes are East Baton Rouge Parish (456,180; GEOID 22033) and Lafayette Parish (257,949; GEOID 22055), each a government that is partially consolidated with its principal city but still contains other incorporated places. The one FUNCSTAT C parish is Orleans Parish (362,154; GEOID 22071), which is coextensive with New Orleans city. This state file has no SUMLEV 170 or 172 consolidated-city rows.

Census functional-status codes used below are from the Census Bureau list *Functional Status Codes and Definitions* (https://www.census.gov/library/reference/code-lists/functional-status-codes.html): **B** is an active government that is partially consolidated with another government but with separate officials providing primary general-purpose functions; **C** is an active government consolidated with another government with a single set of officials. ## Quirks

**New Orleans: use the SUMLEV 162 place, which equals Orleans Parish.** Ranking and city-limits checks use “New Orleans city” (GEOID 2255000, 362,154, FUNCSTAT A). That equals the FUNCSTAT C parish total. This is the same method used for Lexington (Lexington-Fayette urban county equals Fayette County), Columbus, Georgia (Columbus city equals Muscogee County), and Macon (Macon-Bibb County equals Bibb County): when the consolidated government fills the whole county or parish and Census publishes no leftover municipalities and no balance label, use the SUMLEV 162 place row, not a larger county-style total, because the two figures are the same. City-limits checks use only GEOID 2255000. An office that prints New Orleans but geocodes inside Jefferson, St. Bernard, Plaquemines, or St. Tammany Parish, including Kenner or a Jefferson Parish CDP with a New Orleans mailing address, must be held.

**Baton Rouge: use the SUMLEV 162 city, not the parish.** Ranking and city-limits checks use “Baton Rouge city” (GEOID 2205000, 222,795, FUNCSTAT B). Do not use the East Baton Rouge Parish total (456,180) and do not fold in St. George, Central, Zachary, Baker, or the unincorporated parish remainder. Unlike Louisville, Indianapolis, and Augusta, Census does not publish a “Baton Rouge (balance)” SUMLEV 162 row or a SUMLEV 170 consolidated-city total; the comparable figure is the municipal place itself. FUNCSTAT B here marks a partial city-parish consolidation that still has separate remaining municipalities. City-limits checks use only GEOID 2205000. An office that prints Baton Rouge but geocodes inside St. George, Central, Zachary, Baker, or unincorporated East Baton Rouge Parish must be held.

**Lafayette: use the SUMLEV 162 city, not the parish, and use a state-suffix slug.** Ranking and city-limits checks use “Lafayette city” (GEOID 2240735, 123,311, FUNCSTAT B), not the Lafayette Parish total (257,949). Do not fold in Youngsville, Broussard, Carencro, Scott, Duson, or the unincorporated parish remainder. As with Baton Rouge, Census does not publish a balance label or a SUMLEV 170 row. City-limits checks use only GEOID 2240735. An office that prints Lafayette but geocodes inside one of those other municipalities or in unincorporated Lafayette Parish must be held. The Check Desk slug is `/lafayette-la/`. Do not touch Lafayette, Colorado (`/lafayette/`).

**Shreveport spans two parishes.** The whole-place GEOID 2270000 (175,902) is the sum of the Caddo part (173,180) and the Bossier part (2,722). City-limits checks use that whole-place GEOID. An office that prints Shreveport but geocodes inside Bossier City or another Caddo or Bossier municipality, or in unincorporated area outside the Shreveport place, must be held.

**No SUMLEV 170 or 172 rows.** The Louisiana Vintage 2025 state file has none. Houma city (31,735; GEOID 2236255; FUNCSTAT N) is a nonfunctioning legal entity in Terrebonne Parish and is 11th in this ranking; it is not in this five.

## Time zones

All of Louisiana is in the central standard time zone under **49 CFR 71.6(a)**. That zone is the part of the United States west of the Eastern–Central boundary in **49 CFR 71.5** and east of the Central–Mountain boundary in **49 CFR 71.7**. Section 71.5 runs through Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee, and Florida. Section 71.7 runs through the Dakotas, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma, and Texas. Louisiana is not named on either boundary line, and none of the five cities sits on a zone boundary. Therefore New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Shreveport, Lafayette, and Lake Charles are all labeled **Central Time**.

Local copy of the official part 71 XML: `/workspace/step8la/CFR-2025-title49-vol1-part71.xml` (govinfo, CFR 2025 title 49 vol. 1, dated 2025-10-01).

## Name collisions

New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Shreveport, Lafayette, and Lake Charles should be treated as the Louisiana Census places above (GEOID 22 + PLACE FIPS). Lafayette, Louisiana, is not Lafayette, Colorado (`/lafayette/`). Lake Charles, Louisiana, is not Lakewood, Colorado (`/lakewood/`). Iowa town, Louisiana, is in Calcasieu Parish and is not in this five, and it is not the state of Iowa. Central city and St. George city, both in East Baton Rouge Parish, are not in this five. Kenner and Bossier City are also outside this five.

Ship order: New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Shreveport, Lafayette, Lake Charles. Post Offices only. Hours labeled Central Time for all five. Only Lafayette needs a state-suffix slug (`/lafayette-la/`).
