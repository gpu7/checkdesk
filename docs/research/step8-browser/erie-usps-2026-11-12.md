# Erie, CO: USPS locator research (2026-11-12, America/Denver)

Research pass (copied here for the Erie build; see `docs/research/step8-erie.md`). No business was contacted. No browser was used. last_verified: 2026-11-12.

## Method

- **Locator:** official USPS Location Finder API (`https://tools.usps.com/UspsToolsRestServices/rest/POLocator/findLocations`, `requestType` po), queried with curl, the same way as Windsor and Brighton. Searches at 5 and 15 miles around 80516 (Erie), 80504, 80501, and 80503 (Longmont), 80026 (Lafayette), 80027 (Louisville/Superior), 80023, 80020, and 80021 (Broomfield), 80602 (Brighton/Thornton), 80514 (Dacono), 80530 (Frederick), 80621 (Fort Lupton), 80234 (Northglenn/Westminster), and 80031 (Westminster) returned **73 unique Post Office records**. All 30 queries returned JSON. Raw records: `erie-usps-locator-2026-11-12.jsonl` (this folder).
- **Official pages:** `https://tools.usps.com/locations/details/<id>` fetched with curl at about 3:25 PM MT. 1362695 Erie: HTTP 200. 1546616 Erie Carrier Annex: HTTP 200. Both loaded on the first try . Hours and services come from the official locator record that these pages display. Retail hours means the `BUSINESS` hours block only.
- **Town limits:** U.S. Census Geocoder address endpoint, Public_AR_Current / Current_Current (Incorporated Places, CDPs, and Counties layers), checked for all 73 records (`erie-usps-geocoder-2026-11-12.jsonl`). Every official USPS locator map point was also checked against the current benchmark. For both Erie-printed addresses I ran one-line lookups against both the Census 2020 file and the current file. Map-point and one-line results: `erie-geocoder-fallback-2026-11-12.jsonl`.
