# Fort Collins, CO money-services store research

Checked 2026-11-11 using official King Soopers and Walmart public store pages only. No sign-in, contact, or purchase actions were performed.

## Scope and page coverage

Opened 28 official store detail pages: 11 King Soopers and 17 Walmart Supercenters. The King Soopers locator returned 19 results over 3 pages; Fort Collins, Windsor, Loveland, and Greeley results were opened (the Greeley results were within about 25 miles). Walmart Store Finder returned 17 stores within 50 miles of Fort Collins ZIP 80524; all 17 result pages were opened. They were all Supercenters; no Neighborhood Markets appeared. No fuel centers were added separately.

## Printed Fort Collins stores (7)

- King Soopers: North College Marketplace; CEDARWOOD; Midtown Marketplace; Timberline; JFK Parkway.
- Walmart: Fort Collins E Magnolia St Supercenter (#2729); Fort Collins S Mason St Supercenter (#3177).

## Nearby / out-of-city printed addresses

- King Soopers: Windsor (Windsor); 29th Street Loveland and Eagle Drive Loveland (Loveland); North Gate Village, Westlake, and University Square (Greeley).
- Walmart Supercenters: Timnath (1); Loveland (2); Greeley (2); Longmont (2); Cheyenne, WY (2); Lafayette (1); Brighton (2); Westminster (1); Broomfield (2).
- The fully paginated King Soopers results also contained more distant stores in Longmont, Firestone, Frederick, Boulder, and Erie; no details were opened for those results. No Fort Collins-area King Soopers result printed Timnath or Wellington.

## Money-services findings

King Soopers pages list “Money Orders” and “Money Services”; Midtown Marketplace also lists “Check Cashing” and “Western Union”. No opened page explicitly named bill pay or prepaid reload. Walmart pages list generic “Money Services” and “Financial Services” labels only; all four specific service flags are false for those rows, and Western Union/MoneyGram is not mentioned.

## Blocked or incomplete pages and leads

Walmart briefly showed a “Robot or human?” press-and-hold challenge while opening a store page; the longer hold cleared it and the requested pages then loaded. One King Soopers detail page initially showed only the shell and loaded on direct retry. No targeted store page remained incomplete. No other-brand leads were identified; no ACE, payday, title-loan, or other-brand pages were opened.
