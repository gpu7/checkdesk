# Mesa, AZ: USPS locator research (2026-11-14, America/Denver)

Research only until the city pages are shipped. No business was contacted. No browser was used. last_verified: 2026-11-14. Official detail pages that were fetched are marked verification_status `verified_official_locator`. Hours below are **Arizona Time** (Mountain Standard Time). Arizona does not observe daylight saving. City-limits wording: Mesa is an **incorporated city** in Maricopa County (Census "Mesa city" GEOID 0446000). The county is Maricopa County (GEOID 04013). Mesa is not a consolidated city-county. A USPS printed city of Mesa is treated as agreeing with that Census place name only when the address and official map point also fall inside Mesa city. Offices printed Mesa whose address or map point falls outside the city are holds. Offices printed with other place names are left off, or held if they geocode inside Mesa city. Map-point fallback applies only when the printed city already agrees and the official map point is in Mesa city.

## Method

- **Locator:** official USPS Location Finder API (`https://tools.usps.com/UspsToolsRestServices/rest/POLocator/findLocations`, `requestType` po), queried with curl the same way as Phoenix and Tucson. Official Mesa, AZ ZIP list: https://www.zip-codes.com/city/az-mesa.asp (USPS data last updated November 1, 2026). The official table lists 23 codes. The 19 Valley codes were queried: 85201–85216, 85274, 85275, and 85277 (85211, 85214, 85216, 85274, 85275, and 85277 are P.O. Box codes). The same Mesa page also lists four far Navajo/Hopi codes (86033, 86042, 86045, and 86514); those were treated as a listing quirk, the same way as in the Phoenix research, and were not queried as Mesa nearby ZIPs. Nearby official ZIPs not already on that Valley list, queried in pass 1: Chandler 85224, 85225, and 85226; Gilbert 85233, 85234, 85295, and 85296; Tempe 85281 and 85282; Apache Junction 85120; Queen Creek 85142; Phoenix-edge 85008, 85018, 85040, and 85044; and Higley 85236. Pass 1 (`sweep.py`): 35 ZIPs, 70 queries at 5 and 15 miles, all JSON, **54 unique Post Office records**, 0 parse failures, the same eight Mesa-printed offices already known from the Phoenix Valley sweep. Pass 2: 1, 2, 3, 10, and 20 mile widenings around edge Mesa and fringe ZIPs 85207, 85208, 85209, 85212, 85215, 85205, 85120, and 85142 (40 queries, **1 added record**: Coolidge, printed Coolidge, far from Mesa city). Total **55 records** (`usps_all.json`). Official pages and both-file geocodes for all 55 were already on file from the Phoenix and Tucson research (all HTTP 200, `match_api`, coordinates agree) and were copied into `/workspace/step8mes/`. No additional Mesa-printed office appeared.
- **Official pages:** `https://tools.usps.com/locations/details/<id>` for all 55 records. All returned **HTTP 200** and each page's embedded `poDetail` matched the API record on name, address, phone, BUSINESS hours, and map coordinates (`pages.json`). Retail hours = the BUSINESS block only. Money-order service is taken from the office's own `poDetail` services list.
- **City limits:** all 55 addresses and all 55 official locator map points against Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties (`usps_geo_all.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used.
