# Colorado Springs Walmart and King Soopers official-page facts

**Checked:** 2026-11-11. Public official store-finder and store-detail pages only; no sign-in or store contact.

## Coverage and counts

- **King Soopers:** the requested store search returned **16 grocery results on 2 pages**; both result pages were opened and all 16 official store pages were checked. **12** pages print Colorado Springs as the address city and **4** print another city. Colorado Springs does **not** have zero King Soopers in this search. No fuel-center-only listings appeared among the grocery results; several grocery pages separately list fuel centers.
- **Walmart:** the official finder returned **22 results within its stated 50-mile search radius** in one loaded result set (no additional results page appeared): **17 Supercenters** and **5 Neighborhood Markets**. All 22 official store-detail pages were opened. **5** Supercenter pages print Colorado Springs as the city; the other 12 Supercenters print a different city. Colorado Springs does **not** have zero Supercenters in this finder result set.
- **CSV rows:** 38 total — 16 King Soopers grocery results, 17 Walmart Supercenters, and 5 Neighborhood Markets retained as `excluded_neighborhood_market`.

Classification follows the city printed in the official-page address, except Neighborhood Markets are explicitly classified as excluded. The Walmart finder exposes results as a 50-mile set, so out-of-city stores are retained as returned, even where the distance may be more than an ordinary reading of “very near.”

## Store-page money-service labels

- King Soopers pages explicitly name **Money Orders** on 12 of 16 pages; they name **Check Cashing** only on Flying Horse. The generic **Money Services** label appears on 15 of 16 pages. No King Soopers page opened explicitly named Bill Pay or Prepaid Reload. Flying Horse also lists Western Union; it is recorded in notes rather than inferred into a requested service column.
- Walmart Supercenter pages name the generic **Money Services** label; the opened pages did not explicitly name check cashing, money orders, bill pay, or prepaid reload. Neighborhood Market pages did not name Money Services on the opened pages. No chain-level service claims were used.

## Notes and blockers

- **No unresolved blockers or truncated results.** Walmart displayed a “Robot or human?” press-and-hold verification on one store page; the hold succeeded and research continued. Computer was used only for that native press-and-hold challenge.
- The King Soopers **Monument** search result/page heading has an official address whose printed city is **Colorado Springs**; it is classified `in_city` per the printed address and noted in the CSV.
- The King Soopers results suggested City Market on the Gold Hill Woodland Park and Canon City cards. No City Market details or roster were collected.
- For King Soopers, store IDs are the official ID suffixes in the opened store-page URLs. The Walmart pages print their store numbers.
- Hours in `hours_raw` record the official pages' regular store schedule. King Soopers rows also include pharmacy hours (and fuel-center hours when explicitly shown); Walmart rows include the page's sensory-friendly window and holiday-hours line alongside regular hours.
