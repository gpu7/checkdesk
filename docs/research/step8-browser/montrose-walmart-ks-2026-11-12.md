## Coverage notes

- Walmart's city-only finder query for “Montrose, CO” returned zero matches; the ZIP 81401 and 81403 finder searches and the official Montrose directory were used instead.
- Walmart finder results reviewed were those displayed within 50 miles; farther tails were not enumerated. No city-limit geocode was performed.
- King Soopers locator showed four results (one results page) for each ZIP search; all were City Market. The direct Montrose directory URL was unusable as noted above.
- No Safeway or other unexpected grocery brand appeared in the reviewed results. No locator result surfaced for Olathe, Ridgway, Ouray, Grand Junction, or an unincorporated Montrose County listing. No City Market page was opened, and City Market was not treated as an approved or newly added brand.
