# Phoenix, AZ: USPS locator research (2026-11-14, America/Denver)

Research only. Arizona's five-city Census list still needs Richard's approval before any Phoenix pages, PR, or deploy. No business was contacted. No browser was used. last_verified: 2026-11-14. Official detail pages that were fetched are marked verification_status `verified_official_locator`. Hours below are **Arizona Time** (Mountain Standard Time). Arizona does not observe daylight saving; Navajo Nation DST does not apply in Phoenix. City-limits wording: Phoenix is an **incorporated city** in Maricopa County (Census "Phoenix city" GEOID 0455000). The county is Maricopa County (GEOID 04013). Phoenix is not a consolidated city-county. A USPS printed city of Phoenix is treated as agreeing with that Census place name only when the address and official map point also fall inside Phoenix city. Offices printed Phoenix whose address or map point falls outside the city (in a CDP, another city, or unincorporated county land) are holds. Offices printed with other place names are left off, or held if they geocode inside Phoenix city.

Nearby official ZIPs from ZIP-Codes.com city pages and from search.asp for Guadalupe (85283) and Queen Creek (85140, 85142, 85143, 85144), not already on the Phoenix city list: Glendale, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa (Valley codes only; the Mesa city page also lists far Navajo/Hopi ZIPs 86033, 86042, 86045, and 86514, which were not queried as Mesa "nearby" ZIPs), Chandler, Gilbert, Peoria, Avondale, Tolleson, Paradise Valley, Litchfield Park, Goodyear, El Mirage, Surprise, Youngtown, Cave Creek, Carefree, Buckeye, Gila Bend, Sun City, Sun City West, Laveen (85339; Laveen has its own city page and is not on the Phoenix 71), Apache Junction, and Queen Creek / San Tan Valley. 186 ZIPs, 372 queries, all JSON, **85 unique Post Office records**, 0 parse failures. Dateland 85333 returned 0 locations at 5, 15, and 20 miles; a 50-mile query returned Tacna and Wellton only — no Dateland-printed office appeared. Total **119 records** (`usps_all.json`). Some 5-mile queries returned more records than the same ZIP at 15 miles (for example 85085: 32 at 5 miles, 18 at 15 miles); the union was kept. As in Fairbanks, a very small radius can also return a default nearest list, which only widens coverage.
- **Official pages:** `https://tools.usps.com/locations/details/<id>` fetched with curl about 11:22 AM MT for all 119 records. All returned **HTTP 200 on the first try** (`http.txt`, `d<id>.html`). Each page embeds the official `poDetail` record; `pages.py` parsed it and confirmed name, address, phone, BUSINESS hours, and official map coordinates match the API record for all 119 (`pages.json`). "Retail hours" = the `BUSINESS` block only (not lobby, PO Box, pickup, holiday, bulk-mail, or APC). Money-order service is taken from the office's own `poDetail` `services` list (MONEYORDERSDOM and/or MONEYORDERSINQ). Those code strings also appear elsewhere on some detail pages inside other locations' service lists; those hits were not treated as tags for the subject office.
- **City limits:** all 119 addresses through the Census Geocoder address endpoint and all 119 official locator map points, each against both Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties (`usps_geo_all.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used. Raw records are under `/workspace/step8phx/`.
