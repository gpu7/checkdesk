## Method

- **USPS:** official USPS Location Finder API (`tools.usps.com`, `requestType` po). Official Waterbury ZIPs came from ZIP-Codes.com search.asp (the `ct-waterbury` city-page slug is not a collision). Search title says "17 ZIP Codes in Waterbury, CT"; the city page and search.asp have the same 17 unique codes. Of those, 15 are listed Waterbury (7 Standard, 8 P.O. Box). The other 2 are listed as Prospect 06712 and Wolcott 06716 and are not treated as official Waterbury printed-city ZIPs, but they were included in pass 1. Neighborhood slugs `ct-east-end` and `ct-plaza` collided with the Waterbury city page. The Wolcott slug `ct-wolcott` also collided with the Waterbury city page; Wolcott ZIPs came from search.asp (title "2 ZIP Codes in Wolcott, CT"). Pass 1: 5 and 15 miles around 53 ZIPs (106 queries, 159 unique records, 5 printed Waterbury). Pass 2 added 1, 2, 3, 10, and 20 mile widenings around 40 official and edge ZIPs (200 queries, 25 added records, no new printed-Waterbury offices). Total 184 records (`step8-browser/waterbury-usps-locator-2026-11-15.jsonl`). Official location pages were fetched for all 184 records (all HTTP 200 on the first try); each page's embedded `poDetail` matched the API record. Full notes: `step8-browser/waterbury-usps-2026-11-15.md`.
- **Walmart:** not collected.
- **City limits:** U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, address and coordinates endpoints, Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties, matching Waterbury city GEOID 0980000 only. All 184 addresses and official locator map points on both benchmarks (`step8-browser/waterbury-usps-geocoder-2026-11-15.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used. No map-point fallbacks. Waterbury Post Office official ZIP 06701 vs both files matching 06702 is the same street and still in the city (not a fallback). Lakewood official 797 LAKEWOOD AVE, ZIP 06703 vs both files matching 797 LAKEWOOD RD, ZIP 06704 is the same street and still in the city (not a fallback). East End unit STE 6 was dropped; the geocoder matched 159 MANOR AVE, still in the city.
- Fees reuse existing rows in `data/providers/fees.csv`. Coordinates for USPS rows are in the notes column (lat/lng columns stay blank).
