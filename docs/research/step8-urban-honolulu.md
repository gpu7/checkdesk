# Urban Honolulu, Hawaii USPS research (2026-11-16)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). First Hawaii community in the one-time Hawaii CDP exception approved by Richard (answer to t50s373). Post Offices only. Hours on the Urban Honolulu pages are Hawaii Time (HST). Community limits are the Urban Honolulu Census Designated Place (CDP), GEOID 1571550, in Honolulu County (GEOID 15003). 2020 Census population 350,964. Urban Honolulu is a Census community, not an incorporated city; Hawaii has no incorporated places recognized by the Census Bureau. The official USPS printed city is Honolulu. This is Urban Honolulu, Hawaii, not a Honolulu in another state.
