# Austin, TX — community-named office added 2026-11-19

The Del Valle Post Office is now listed on the Austin pages, under the rule approved 2026-11-19: the official page prints a community name, both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Austin city (GEOID 4805000), money-order tags are present, and the displayed city stays the official printed USPS city. Official map points also fall in Austin city on both files, in Travis County. The original Austin-printed rows are unchanged.

Added office (printed city in parentheses): Del Valle (Del Valle). Del Valle is a community inside Austin city, not a separately incorporated place.

The Austin list is now 15 Post Offices, all with money orders (six Open evenings, same closes as before; ten Open weekends, Saturday only, including Del Valle 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.). Hours stay Central Time. Mc Neil stays held because both Census address files did not place the address inside Austin city (map-point fallback is not used when the printed city disagrees). Five Austin-printed no-money-order offices also remain held.
