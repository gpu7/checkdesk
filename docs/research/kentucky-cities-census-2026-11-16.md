# Kentucky: five largest incorporated places (Census Vintage 2025)

Prepared 2026-11-16 (America/Denver). Same file and method as Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii (CDP exception was one-time only), Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, and Kansas. Standing instruction is to research and ship these five, largest first, one PR each, Post Offices only. Kentucky uses incorporated places (SUMLEV 162) and excludes CDPs. Louisville/Jefferson County metro government and Lexington-Fayette urban county are consolidated governments. Ranking and city-limits checks use their SUMLEV 162 place or balance rows, the same way Augusta used its balance (GEOID 1304204), Indianapolis used its balance (GEOID 1836003), and Kansas City, KS used the SUMLEV 162 place “Kansas City city” (GEOID 2036000): use the place remainder, not the county or consolidated-city total. Hours are labeled by city: Eastern Time for Louisville, Lexington, and Covington; Central Time for Bowling Green and Owensboro (see Time zones below). Grouping is alphabetical: Kentucky after Kansas. Do not start Louisiana from this run.

## Time zones

Kentucky is split between the Eastern and Central zones. Under **49 CFR 71.4**, the Eastern zone is the part of the United States east of the boundary in **49 CFR 71.5**. Under **49 CFR 71.6(a)**, the Central zone is west of that boundary and east of the boundary in **49 CFR 71.7**. The Kentucky segment of the Eastern–Central boundary is **49 CFR 71.5(c) (Kentucky)**, from the official 2025 eCFR / 49 CFR Subtitle A (10-1-25 Edition) text:

> From the junction of the east line of Perry County, Ind., with the Indiana-Kentucky boundary easterly along that boundary to the west line of Meade County, Ky.; thence southeasterly and southwesterly along the west lines of Meade and Hardin Counties to the southwest corner of Hardin County; thence along the south lines of Hardin and Larue Counties to the northwest corner of Taylor County; thence southeasterly along the west (southwest) lines of Taylor County and northeasterly along the east (southeast) line of Taylor County to the west line of Casey County; and thence southerly along the west and south lines of Casey and Pulaski Counties to the intersection with the western boundary of Wayne County; and then south along the western boundary of Wayne County to the Kentucky-Tennessee boundary.

Municipalities located on that boundary line are in the Central zone (**49 CFR 71.5(h)**). Jefferson, Fayette, and Kenton Counties lie east of this line and are in the Eastern zone. Daviess County lies west of the west line of Meade County along the Indiana-Kentucky boundary, and Warren County lies west and south of the Hardin–Larue segment; both are in the Central zone. None of the five cities in this run sits on the 71.5(c) boundary. Therefore:

- **Louisville** (Jefferson County), **Lexington** (Fayette County), and **Covington** (Kenton County) are labeled **Eastern Time**.
- **Bowling Green** (Warren County) and **Owensboro** (Daviess County) are labeled **Central Time**.
