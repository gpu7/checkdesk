## Method

- **USPS:** official USPS Location Finder API (`tools.usps.com`, `requestType` po). Official New Haven ZIPs came from ZIP-Codes.com search.asp (the `ct-new-haven` city-page slug is not a collision). City page and search.asp have the same 32 unique ZIPs, all in South Central CT. Of those, 26 are listed New Haven (5 Standard, 18 P.O. Box, 3 Unique). The other 6 in the official New Haven search are listed as other cities (East Haven 06512; Hamden 06514, 06517, 06518; Bethany 06524; Woodbridge 06525) and are not treated as official New Haven printed-city ZIPs, but they were included in pass 1. Neighborhood slugs `ct-hamden`, `ct-westville`, and `ct-fair-haven` collided with the New Haven city page. Seymour's slug collided with Oxford. Pass 1: 5 and 15 miles around 62 ZIPs (124 queries, 133 unique records, 6 printed New Haven). Pass 2 added 1, 2, 3, 10, and 20 mile widenings around 39 official and edge ZIPs (195 queries, 14 added records far from New Haven city, no new printed-New-Haven offices). Total 147 records (`step8-browser/new-haven-usps-locator-2026-11-15.jsonl`). Official location pages were fetched for all 147 records (all HTTP 200 on the first try); each page's embedded `poDetail` matched the API record. Full notes: `step8-browser/new-haven-usps-2026-11-15.md`.
- **Walmart:** not collected.
- **City limits:** U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, address and coordinates endpoints, Public_AR_Current / Current_Current and Public_AR_Census2020 / Census2020_Census2020, Incorporated Places + CDPs + Counties, matching New Haven city GEOID 0952000 only. All 147 addresses and official locator map points on both benchmarks (`step8-browser/new-haven-usps-geocoder-2026-11-15.jsonl`). OpenStreetMap was not used. No listed office needed the map-point fallback. All six printed-New-Haven offices have address and map-point matches in New Haven city on both Census files.
- Fees reuse existing rows in `data/providers/fees.csv`. Coordinates for USPS rows are in the notes column (lat/lng columns stay blank).
