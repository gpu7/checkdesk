Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. One Pearl City Post Office lists money orders on its official page, prints Pearl City, and geocodes inside the Pearl City CDP: the Pearl City Post Office at 950 Kamehameha Hwy. This is Pearl City, Hawaii, a Census community in Honolulu County, not a Pearl City in another state, and not Urban Honolulu, East Honolulu, or Waipahu. It is not labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 4:00 p.m. It publishes Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and is labeled Open weekends. It does not publish Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. It is listed on a documented map-point fallback: the current and 2020 Census address files did not match 950 Kamehameha Hwy after street, city, and ZIP retries, and both official map points fall inside Pearl City CDP. The printed city is Pearl City. No other office in the Hawaii locator sweep geocodes into Pearl City CDP. No Pearl City location names check cashing.
