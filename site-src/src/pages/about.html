About this site and how we check facts

Check Desk is an independent information directory. It is not a bank, money transmitter, check casher, or financial adviser.

How we check facts

  • We take every price, hour, and service detail from the provider's own official page, or from another clearly cited public source, and we label which is which.
  • Every figure links to its source and carries the date we last verified it.
  • If a provider does not publish a national price, we say exactly that. We do not estimate or average prices.
  • A chain policy is not the same as a verified store. Rows can be marked policy_only_call_ahead until the location itself is checked on an official locator or official store page. Call the location before you go.
  • We use public sources only. We do not contact businesses to collect data.

Not financial advice

This site is general information, not financial, legal, or tax advice, and not a recommendation to use or avoid any product, loan, or account. We do not promote payday loans, title loans, or other credit products.
