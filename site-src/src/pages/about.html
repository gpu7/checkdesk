About Check Desk

Check Desk is an independent information directory of places where you can cash a check, buy a money order, pay a bill in person, or reload a prepaid card. It is not a bank, money transmitter, check casher, or financial adviser, and it is not affiliated with the businesses it names.

Who we are

Check Desk is a small, independent site operated by Richard Casey. It publishes a factual, verified directory. It does not cash checks, sell money orders, pay bills, or reload cards, and it does not offer accounts or credit.

What we do

We list checked locations and the services their official pages name, with a last-verified date and a verification status on every listing. The directory is built only from public sources:

  • Official store locators and official brand pages
  • Official USPS location pages
  • The U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder, which we use to confirm that an address falls inside the city limits used on that city page

We use public sources only. We do not contact businesses to collect data.

Who this is for

The pages are written for unbanked and underbanked people, and for people who need a desk open after typical bank hours. When published hours support it, a location can be labeled Open evenings or Open weekends.

How we check facts

  • Every price, hour, and service detail comes from the provider's official page or another clearly cited public source, and we label which is which.
  • Every figure and every location row carries a last-verified date and a verification status.
  • If a provider does not publish a fee, we write "Not published, call ahead." We do not estimate or average prices.
  • A chain policy is not the same as a verified store. A location appears on a city page only after it has been checked on an official store locator, official store page, or official USPS location page. Call the location before you go.

How to read the stamps

  • Last verified is the date we last opened the official source, shown with a short status such as "Checked on the official store page" or "Checked on the official USPS location page," and a link to that source.
  • Published fee is the brand's official policy, not a store's till price. If the brand does not publish a dollar figure, the cell says "Not published, call ahead."
  • Open evenings means the published hours are open after 5 p.m. on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday. Open weekends means the published hours include Saturday or Sunday. Both labels come only from published hours. Store hours are not always money-desk hours, so call ahead.

What we do not list

Check Desk does not list payday lenders, title lenders, or stores whose chain also offers payday or title loans, including ACE Cash Express. The ACE Cash Express policy page covers official check-cashing wording only; it does not list stores or describe loans.

Paid placement and affiliates

At this time the site has no ads, no paid placements, and no affiliate links. Commissions do not influence what we publish because there are no commissions. If that ever changes, the disclosure page will be updated first, and any commercial link will be labeled on the page where it appears.

Not financial advice

Policies and fees change. Call the location before you go. Check Desk is an information directory, not financial advice. This site is general information, not financial, legal, or tax advice, and not a recommendation to use or avoid any product, loan, or account.
