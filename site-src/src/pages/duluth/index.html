Duluth check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

This page lists locations inside Duluth city limits, each checked on an official USPS location page. So far that is five Post Offices. It shows which services each page lists, the published hours, and what the brand publishes about fees.

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Five Duluth Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Duluth, Minnesota, and are inside Duluth city limits (the Census place Duluth city, GEOID 2717000, in St. Louis County): Civic Center at 515 W 1st St, Duluth at 2800 W Michigan St, Lakeside at 4427 E Superior St, Mount Royal at 200 Mount Royal Shopping Cir, and West Duluth at 215 N Central Ave Bldg 1. This is Duluth, Minnesota, in St. Louis County, not Duluth township, Hermantown, Proctor, or a Duluth in another state, and not St. Louis, Missouri. Minneapolis, St. Paul, and Rochester stay on separate lists. None is labeled Open evenings. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Duluth, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and Mount Royal, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Civic Center, Lakeside, and West Duluth do not publish Saturday or Sunday hours. All five match the current and 2020 Census address files in Duluth city, and all official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. One office prints Duluth, Minnesota, but the Census geocoder places it outside Duluth city, so it is not listed: Proctor at 217 2nd St, in Proctor city. Two offices print Duluth and geocode in Duluth city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: a no-hours Duluth record at 2800 W Michigan St, and Duluth MN SDC at the same address. No other office in the Minnesota locator sweep geocodes into Duluth city. No Duluth location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Duluth pages are Central Time.
