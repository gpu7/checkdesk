Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. 10 San Jose Post Offices below list money orders on their official pages and have addresses and official map points inside San Jose city limits. Nine print San Jose. One community-named office is also listed, because both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Jose city and the official page lists money orders: Alviso at 1525 Gold St, which prints Alviso. Displayed addresses keep the official printed USPS city. Only Willow Glen publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. Seven publish Saturday hours. None publishes Sunday hours. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Jose city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and either the official printed city is San Jose, or both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Jose city and money-order tags are present. The displayed city is the official printed USPS city. San Jose is an incorporated city inside Santa Clara County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a San Jose mailing city, print a neighborhood postal city, or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here. The Alviso Post Office is listed under the neighborhood-name rule.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.
