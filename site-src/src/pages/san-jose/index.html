How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Jose city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and either the official printed city is San Jose, or both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Jose city and money-order tags are present. The displayed city is the official printed USPS city. San Jose is an incorporated city inside Santa Clara County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a San Jose mailing city, print a neighborhood postal city, or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here. The Alviso Post Office is listed under the neighborhood-name rule.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Which San Jose Post Offices sell money orders?

All 10 San Jose locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Alviso (1525 Gold St, which prints Alviso), Saint James Park, Westgate, Robertsville, Hillview, Blossom Hill, Cambrian Park, Willow Glen, Berryessa, and Bayside. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The New Almaden Post Office also lists money orders, but it is held because the address and official map point fall outside San Jose city. Nine other San Jose-printed records inside the city do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside San Jose city limits or print a different city.

Which San Jose Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Only Willow Glen publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. (5:30 p.m.) and is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count. The other nine listed offices close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays. Eastridge and Oakridge Mall also publish later weekday hours, but they are not listed because their official pages do not list money orders.

Which San Jose Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Seven of the 10 listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Westgate, Robertsville, Hillview, Blossom Hill, Cambrian Park, Willow Glen, and Berryessa. Saint James Park, Bayside, and Alviso do not publish Saturday or Sunday retail hours. None of the 10 publishes Sunday hours.

Why is the Alviso Post Office listed if it prints Alviso?

Its official page prints Alviso, not San Jose. Both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Jose city (GEOID 0668000), official map points also fall there, and the official page lists money orders, so it is listed under the approved neighborhood-name rule. The displayed city stays the official printed USPS city. Alviso is a community inside San Jose city, not a separately incorporated place.

Why is the New Almaden Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints San Jose, and the Census geocoder matches 21300 ALMADEN RD, SAN JOSE, CA, 95120, but that match and the official USPS map point fall in unincorporated Santa Clara County, not inside San Jose city, on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose address and official map point are not in San Jose city is held for review and not listed.

Where can I cash a check in San Jose?

No San Jose location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in San Jose have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in San Jose?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
