Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Five Springfield Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Springfield, Massachusetts, and are inside Springfield city limits (the Census place Springfield city, GEOID 2567000, in Hampden County): Brightwood at 3065 Main St Ste 1, Colonial Station at 1149 Main St, Forest Park at 393 Belmont Ave, Mason Square at 914 State St, and Tower Square at 1500 Main St. This is Springfield, Massachusetts, in Hampden County, not Springfield, Illinois, Springfield, Missouri, Springfield, Vermont, or another Springfield, and not West Springfield, Longmeadow, Chicopee, or Holyoke. None is labeled Open evenings. Forest Park weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. The other listed offices close earlier. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Brightwood and Mason Square, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; and Forest Park, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Colonial Station and Tower Square do not publish weekend hours. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All five match the current and 2020 Census address files in Springfield city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Two offices at 1883 Main St print Springfield and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: the Springfield Post Office, which also has no published retail hours, and Springfield MA S&DC. Indian Orchard at 19 Oak St prints Indian Orchard, not Springfield, so it is not listed, even though all four Census legs fall in Springfield city. No other office in the Massachusetts locator sweep geocodes into Springfield city. No Springfield location names check cashing.
