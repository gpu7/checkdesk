For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All 6 listed Springfield Post Offices sell money orders. None is labeled Open evenings. Forest Park weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. The other listed offices close earlier. Four publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Brightwood, Mason Square, and Indian Orchard, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; and Forest Park, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Colonial Station and Tower Square do not publish weekend hours. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
