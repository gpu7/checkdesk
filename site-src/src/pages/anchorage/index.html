How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Municipality limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Anchorage municipality limits (the consolidated city-borough, checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Anchorage. Some addresses inside the municipality use a different mailing city, such as Eagle River, Chugiak, Girdwood, or JBER.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Anchorage Post Offices sell money orders?

All ten Anchorage Post Offices on this page have official USPS location pages that list money orders: Eastchester (800 Ingra St), the Anchorage Postal Store (320 W 5th Ave Ste 348), Sand Lake (2200 Strawberry Rd), Midtown (3721 B St), Muldoon (2420 Muldoon Rd), Lake Otis (5855 Lake Otis Pkwy), Russian Jack (801 Northway Dr), Huffman (1221 Huffman Park Dr), Spenard (1601 W Northern Lights Blvd), and the Anchorage Main Office (4141 Postmark Dr). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Which Anchorage Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Nine publish weekday retail hours past 5 p.m. (Alaska Time): Eastchester, Sand Lake, and Russian Jack close at 5:30 p.m.; Midtown, Muldoon, Lake Otis, Huffman, and Spenard close at 6 p.m.; and the Anchorage Main Office is open until 11 p.m. The Anchorage Postal Store closes at 2 p.m. on weekdays, so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Which Anchorage Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Five publish Saturday retail hours: Midtown and Muldoon (10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.), Russian Jack and Huffman (10 a.m. to 4 p.m.), and the Anchorage Main Office (9 a.m. to 11 p.m.). The Main Office also publishes Sunday hours from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastchester, the Postal Store, Sand Lake, Lake Otis, and Spenard are closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Why are the Eagle River, Chugiak, Girdwood, and Fort Richardson Post Offices not listed?

Each official page prints a city other than Anchorage (Eagle River, Chugiak, Girdwood, or JBER). The Census geocoder places those addresses or official map points inside Anchorage municipality, but the printed city and the geocode disagree, so they are held for review and not listed. Fort Richardson is also on a military installation, which may limit public access.
