How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Municipality limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Anchorage municipality limits (the consolidated city-borough, checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Neighborhood-named offices whose official pages print a community or installation name such as Eagle River, Girdwood, or JBER, rather than Anchorage, are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Anchorage municipality and money-order tags are present; the displayed city is the official printed USPS city. Chugiak stays held because the 2020 Census address file did not match.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Anchorage Post Offices sell money orders?

All thirteen Anchorage Post Offices on this page have official USPS location pages that list money orders. Ten print Anchorage: Eastchester (800 Ingra St), the Anchorage Postal Store (320 W 5th Ave Ste 348), Sand Lake (2200 Strawberry Rd), Midtown (3721 B St), Muldoon (2420 Muldoon Rd), Lake Otis (5855 Lake Otis Pkwy), Russian Jack (801 Northway Dr), Huffman (1221 Huffman Park Dr), Spenard (1601 W Northern Lights Blvd), and the Anchorage Main Office (4141 Postmark Dr). Three neighborhood-named offices are also listed: Eagle River (11471 Business Blvd, prints Eagle River), Girdwood (118 Lindblad Ave, prints Girdwood), and Fort Richardson (724 Quartermaster Rd, prints JBER). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Which Anchorage Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Eleven publish weekday retail hours past 5 p.m. (Alaska Time): Eastchester, Sand Lake, Russian Jack, Eagle River, and Fort Richardson close at 5:30 p.m.; Midtown, Muldoon, Lake Otis, Huffman, and Spenard close at 6 p.m.; and the Anchorage Main Office is open until 11 p.m. Girdwood weekday retail closes at 5 p.m., with a midday closure, which does not count as Open evenings. The Anchorage Postal Store closes at 2 p.m. on weekdays, so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Which Anchorage Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Seven publish Saturday retail hours: Midtown and Muldoon (10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.), Russian Jack and Huffman (10 a.m. to 4 p.m.), Eagle River (10 a.m. to 2 p.m.), Girdwood (10 a.m. to 12 p.m.), and the Anchorage Main Office (9 a.m. to 11 p.m.). The Main Office also publishes Sunday hours from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastchester, the Postal Store, Sand Lake, Lake Otis, Spenard, and Fort Richardson are closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Why is the Chugiak Post Office not listed?

The official page prints Chugiak rather than Anchorage. The current Census address file and both official map points place the office inside Anchorage municipality, but the 2020 address file did not match, so it stays held. Eagle River, Girdwood, and Fort Richardson are listed because both the current and 2020 address files place those addresses inside Anchorage municipality and the official pages list money orders. Displayed addresses keep the official printed USPS city. Fort Richardson is on Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson, which may limit public access.
