What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Alaska Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Alaska Time. Eastchester, Sand Lake, Lake Otis, and Spenard publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. and are closed on weekends, so they are labeled Open evenings only. Midtown, Muldoon, Russian Jack, and Huffman publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. and Saturday hours, so they carry both labels. The Anchorage Main Office publishes hours until 11 p.m. every day, including Sunday, so it also carries both labels. The Anchorage Postal Store closes at 2 p.m. on weekdays and is closed on weekends, so it is not on this page.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Municipality limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Anchorage municipality limits (the consolidated city-borough, checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Anchorage. Some addresses inside the municipality use a different mailing city, such as Eagle River, Chugiak, Girdwood, or JBER.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.
