What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Pacific Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Pacific Time. Of the 31 listed San Francisco Post Offices, seven publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Pine Street, Clayton Street, Golden Gate, Geary, Lakeshore Plaza, Bryant Street, and San Francisco P&DC Finance, which closes at 8:30 p.m. on weekdays. Twenty-two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5 p.m. weekday close does not count as Open evenings. All seven evening offices also have Saturday hours, so this page lists 22 offices. The nine listed offices with no evening and no weekend retail hours are Federal Building San Fran, Fox Plaza, Sutter Street, Brannan Street, Bernal Heights, Gateway San Francisco, Bayview, Presidio, and McLaren, so they are not on this page.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Francisco city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is San Francisco. San Francisco is a consolidated city-county, coextensive with San Francisco County. South San Francisco is a separate incorporated city in San Mateo County and is not listed here. Many nearby addresses sit in Daly City, Oakland, or another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Does this page show locations that are open now?

No. This page does not have live open or closed status. Labels come from hours published on official USPS location pages, last verified Nov 15, 2026. Check the published hours in the table and confirm with the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in San Francisco at night?

No location on this San Francisco list names check cashing on its official page yet, so none is listed for it at night. The Post Offices here list money orders, not general check cashing. See the San Francisco hub for every checked location.

Can I buy a money order in San Francisco on a weekend or at night?

San Francisco money-order locations on this list publish weekend hours, and publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. 22 of the 31 listed Post Offices publish Saturday retail hours, seven stay open past 5 p.m. on weekdays, and all 31 are closed Sunday [1]. See money orders in San Francisco.
