Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Francisco city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is San Francisco. San Francisco is a consolidated city-county, coextensive with San Francisco County. South San Francisco is a separate incorporated city in San Mateo County and is not listed here. Many nearby addresses sit in Daly City, Oakland, or another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Which San Francisco Post Offices sell money orders?

All 31 San Francisco locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Nine other San Francisco-printed records inside the city do not list money orders, so they are not listed here, including the West Portal Post Office, which does publish Saturday hours. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside San Francisco city limits or print a different city, including the two offices in South San Francisco.

Which San Francisco Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Seven listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Pine Street (6 p.m.), Clayton Street, Golden Gate, Geary, Lakeshore Plaza, and Bryant Street (all 5:30 p.m.), and San Francisco P&DC Finance (8:30 p.m.). A 5 p.m. close does not count. The other 24 listed offices close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays. West Portal is not listed, because its official page does not list money orders.

Which San Francisco Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Twenty-two of the 31 listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. The nine listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Federal Building San Fran, Fox Plaza, Sutter Street, Brannan Street, Bernal Heights, Gateway San Francisco, Bayview, Presidio, and McLaren. None of the 31 publishes Sunday hours. West Portal also publishes Saturday hours, but it is not listed because its official page does not list money orders.

Why are Diamond Heights, Lakeshore Plaza, Marina, Bryant Street, and Presidio listed if an address file did not match?

Each of those five official pages already prints San Francisco, and the official USPS map point falls inside San Francisco city on both the current and 2020 Census files. One or both Census address files did not match after street-name retries. Under the documented map-point fallback, an office is listed when the printed city already matches and the official map point is inside the city. For the Presidio office, retries that dropped the unit number snapped the geocoder to Presidio Avenue, a different street, so that snap was not treated as a match; the official map point still places the office in San Francisco city.

Is South San Francisco included?

No. South San Francisco is a separate incorporated city in San Mateo County. The two Post Offices there print South San Francisco and sit in South San Francisco city, so they are not listed under San Francisco.

Where can I cash a check in San Francisco?

No San Francisco location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in San Francisco have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in San Francisco?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
