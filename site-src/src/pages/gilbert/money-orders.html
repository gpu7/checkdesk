How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Gilbert town limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Gilbert. Gilbert is legally a town, an incorporated place inside Maricopa County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Gilbert mailing city or sit in another incorporated place, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Where can I get a money order near me in Gilbert?

This page lists checked Gilbert locations whose official USPS page lists money orders: two Post Offices. None is labeled Open evenings. USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1]. See the money-order guide.

Does this list show money-order desks that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. Published hours were last verified Nov 14, 2026. Confirm with the location before you go.

Are there 24-hour money-order desks in Gilbert?

None of the listed Gilbert money-order locations publishes 24-hour hours. Gilbert Post Offices on this list close between 4:30 p.m. and 5 p.m. on weekdays (with no Sunday hours). Confirm hours with the Post Office before you go.
