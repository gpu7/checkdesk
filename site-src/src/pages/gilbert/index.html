How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Gilbert town limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Gilbert. Gilbert is legally a town, an incorporated place inside Maricopa County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Gilbert mailing city or sit in another incorporated place, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Which Gilbert Post Offices sell money orders?

Both Gilbert locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: the Gilbert Post Office and the Val Vista Post Office. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Gilbert town limits, print a different city, or do not list money orders on the official page.

Which Gilbert Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Neither of the two listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so neither is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Gilbert Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Both listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Gilbert and Val Vista. Neither publishes Sunday retail hours.

Why is the Higley Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints Higley, not Gilbert. The Census Geocoder had no address match after street-name and city-name retries, and the official USPS map point falls inside Gilbert town on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose printed city is not Gilbert is held for review and not listed, even when the map point is inside the town. The map-point fallback applies only when the printed city already agrees.

Why are the Fiesta Annex and the Gilbert Roadrunner Annex not listed?

Both print Gilbert, and both have official map points inside Gilbert town, but their official pages do not list money orders and publish no retail hours, so they are not on this money-order directory.

Is Gilbert a city or a town?

Gilbert is legally a town. The Census place name is Gilbert town, an incorporated place in Maricopa County. This directory uses that place boundary.
