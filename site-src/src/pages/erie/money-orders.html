Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. The Erie Post Office is the only Erie location whose official page lists money orders. The two King Soopers stores in Erie show only a general "Money Services" label on their official store pages and do not name money orders, check cashing, or bill pay, so we list them on the town hub without services. King Soopers says its money-order cost "will vary by state" and does not publish a fee [3], so call ahead. No Walmart store prints an Erie address. Walmart publishes a chain-wide $1 maximum money-order fee [2] at stores that sell them.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the Town of Erie limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city, and some Erie stores use a Lafayette ZIP code.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
