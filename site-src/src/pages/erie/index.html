How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the Town of Erie limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city, and some Erie stores use a Lafayette ZIP code.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Erie Post Office sells money orders?

The Erie Post Office (150 Wells St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Erie Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Erie Post Office (150 Wells St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to noon. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Erie Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Erie Post Office (150 Wells St) publishes weekday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Do King Soopers stores in Erie sell money orders?

Their official store pages do not say. King Soopers Vista Ridge (1891 State Highway 7) and King Soopers Highway 287 and Arapahoe Road (2900 Arapahoe Rd) show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name money orders, so we list them on this hub without services. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Erie?

No Erie store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and both Erie King Soopers pages show a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Is the King Soopers on Arapahoe Road in Erie or Lafayette?

Erie. King Soopers Highway 287 and Arapahoe Road (2900 Arapahoe Rd) uses the 80026 ZIP code, which Lafayette also uses, but its official store page prints Erie and the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places the address inside the Town of Erie.

Why are Lafayette and Louisville locations not listed?

Lafayette, Louisville, Superior, Broomfield, and Longmont are separate cities and towns, and Dacono, Frederick, and Firestone are separate towns. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside the Town of Erie, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside the Town of Erie limits.
