Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six New Orleans Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print New Orleans, Louisiana, and geocode inside New Orleans city limits (the Census place New Orleans city, GEOID 2255000, which equals Orleans Parish): Bywater at 1521 Poland Ave, Carrollton at 3400 S Carrollton Ave, Lake Forest Station at 9600 Lake Forest Blvd, New Orleans Main Office Window Service at 701 Loyola Ave, Station A Algiers at 3901 Macarthur Blvd, and Uptown Station at 2000 Louisiana Ave. The official locator name for the Main Office Window Service office is N O MAIN OFC WINDOW SVE. This is New Orleans, Louisiana, in Orleans Parish, not Metairie, Kenner, Gretna, Elmwood, River Ridge, Harahan, or Westwego. The Census place fills Orleans Parish; there are no other incorporated places in the parish.
