For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All six listed New Orleans Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: New Orleans Main Office Window Service, until 7:00 p.m. All six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Carrollton, Station A Algiers, and Uptown Station, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; New Orleans Main Office Window Service, 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.; Bywater, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and Lake Forest Station, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.
