King Soopers

King Soopers offers bill pay at its Money Services desks in general: bring your bill stub and enough to cover the bill and the fee, paid in cash or debit [2]. It does not publish a dollar fee: Not published, call ahead. None of the Denver King Soopers store pages we checked lists bill pay, so they are not on this list. Call the store to ask. The billers named on King Soopers' national page include out-of-state names, so they are not a Denver list.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

How much does Walmart charge to pay a bill?

Walmart says fees start at $1 and vary by biller and speed. It does not publish a maximum [1].

Can I pay a bill in cash at Walmart?

Yes. Walmart's bill-pay page lists cash or debit, and says photo ID may be required for payments over $900 [1].
