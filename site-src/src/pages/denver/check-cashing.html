Where to cash a check in Denver

Short version. Walmart publishes a $4 maximum for pre-printed checks up to $1,000 and an $8 maximum above $1,000; two-party personal checks are limited to $200 with a $6 maximum [1]. King Soopers does not publish a fee: "Fees and limits vary by state" [2]. Neither publishes separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Other King Soopers stores

King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks [2], and the other Denver King Soopers store pages we checked list a Money Services desk. Those pages do not list check cashing, so we have not put them on this list. If one is closer, call the store and ask before you go. They are on the Denver hub list.

What to bring

  • Walmart: the endorsed check and valid identification (ID types not listed) [1].
  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [2].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. See Denver Post Offices.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I cash a check in Denver at night?

All four listed locations publish store hours past 5 p.m. every day. Walmart and King Soopers do not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [2].

Does King Soopers cash checks?

Yes, at Money Services desks in general: King Soopers' check-cashing page says it cashes payroll, government, tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person, up to $5,000, with no bank account required [2]. On this Denver list, four locations list check cashing on their official store pages: three Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers Virginia Village. Twelve other Denver King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk (or money orders) without naming check cashing, so call ahead. King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee [2].

Can I cash a personal check at these Denver locations?

Four Denver locations on this list tag check cashing on their official store pages: three Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers Virginia Village. Walmart's official check-cashing page lists two-party personal checks up to $200 with a $6 maximum fee at some locations [1]. King Soopers' official check-cashing page lists payroll, government, income tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person (not checks payable to a business), and says fees and limits vary by state; it does not state a personal-check fee or cap on that page, so call the desk ahead [2].

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Denver?

No location on this Denver list publishes 24-hour hours. All listed locations (three Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers Virginia Village) publish store hours past 5 p.m. every day, closing at 10 p.m. or 11 p.m. Walmart and King Soopers do not publish separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Does this page show check-cashing places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not track live open or closed status. Published store hours were last verified Nov 10, 2026, and money-desk hours are not published separately from store hours. Confirm with the location before you go.

When is the King Soopers Money Services desk open?

King Soopers does not publish separate Money Services desk hours on its Denver store pages. King Soopers Virginia Village publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, and the chain's check-cashing page notes only that hours vary by location [2], so call ahead.
