Where to cash a check in Denver

Denver locations list check cashing on their official store page: three Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers Virginia Village. All four publish store hours seven days a week.

Short version. Walmart publishes a $4 maximum for pre-printed checks up to $1,000 and an $8 maximum above $1,000; two-party personal checks are limited to $200 with a $6 maximum [1]. King Soopers does not publish a fee: "Fees and limits vary by state" [2]. Neither publishes separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Other King Soopers stores

King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks [2], and the other Denver King Soopers store pages we checked list a Money Services desk. Those pages do not list check cashing, so we have not put them on this list. If one is closer, call the store and ask before you go. They are on the Denver hub list.

What to bring

  • Walmart: the endorsed check and valid identification (ID types not listed) [1].
  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [2].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. See Denver Post Offices.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

More detail on check types and limits: check-cashing fee guide. Payday and title-loan storefronts, including ACE Cash Express, are not listed; see ACE's check-cashing policy.

FAQ

Can I cash a check in Denver at night?

All four listed locations publish store hours past 5 p.m. every day. Walmart and King Soopers do not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [2].
