What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Denver time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official store page or official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier than the store. Call ahead.

Holiday note. Walmart's official store directory says its Denver Supercenters are closed Thursday, November 26, 2026 [1].

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Which Denver Post Offices are open on Saturday?

Every Denver Post Office on our list publishes Saturday retail hours, most of them 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. See the table for each office.

Is a store open late the same as the money desk being open late?

Not necessarily. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.
