How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

What is not on this list yet

  • Payday and title-loan storefronts, including ACE Cash Express, are not listed, even when they also cash checks. See ACE's check-cashing policy.
  • Stand-alone Western Union and MoneyGram agents (for example, convenience stores) have not been checked for Denver yet.
  • Some Post Offices may be missing. Our USPS list came from the official location pages we opened; it is not yet a ZIP-by-ZIP sweep of the whole city.
  • Nearby cities (Aurora, Lakewood, Commerce City, Englewood, Wheat Ridge, and Federal Heights) will get their own pages after their locations are checked.

FAQ

Where can I cash a check in Denver on a Sunday?

The three Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers Virginia Village list check cashing on their official store pages and publish Sunday store hours. Money-desk hours are not published, so call ahead. See check cashing in Denver.

What is the cheapest published money-order fee in Denver?

Walmart publishes a $1 maximum fee for money orders; USPS charges $2.65 for up to $500 [2][4]. King Soopers does not publish a fee [6].

Do Denver Post Offices cash checks?

The official Denver Post Office pages we checked list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [4].
