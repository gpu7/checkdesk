USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. King Soopers says the cost "will vary by state" and does not publish a fee [3]. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. Walmart and King Soopers publish store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I buy a money order in Denver on a Sunday?

The Walmart Supercenters and King Soopers stores on this list publish Sunday store hours. Denver Post Offices on this list are closed Sunday. Money-desk hours at Walmart and King Soopers are not published, so call ahead.

Is a Post Office money order the same price at every Denver Post Office?

Yes. USPS publishes one national fee: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1].
