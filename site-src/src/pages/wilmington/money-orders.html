Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Wilmington Post Offices below list money orders on their official pages, print Wilmington, and are inside Wilmington city limits: Rodney Sq and Lancaster Ave. One publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m.: Rodney Sq, until 5:30 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours. Neither publishes Sunday hours. Offices that print Wilmington but sit outside the city, including Talleyville, Newport, Greenville, Marshallton, and Edgemoor, are not listed here. Elsmere, New Castle, and Newark are separate municipalities and are not listed here. Walmart Supercenters in Wilmington have not been checked yet.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Wilmington city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Wilmington. Wilmington is an incorporated city in New Castle County. Many nearby addresses use a Wilmington mailing city but sit in another municipality or in unincorporated New Castle County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.
