How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Middletown town limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Middletown. Middletown is an incorporated town in New Castle County. This page covers Middletown, Delaware, not a Middletown in another state. Many nearby addresses use a nearby mailing city or sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Middletown Post Offices sell money orders?

The one Middletown location on this page lists money orders on its official USPS location page: Middletown Post Office at 298 E Main St. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Middletown town limits and print those other cities, including offices in Odessa, Townsend, Smyrna, Clayton, Delaware City, Bear, and Newark.

Which Middletown Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

The one listed office publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: Middletown Post Office at 298 E Main St, until 5:30 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Middletown Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

The one listed office publishes Saturday retail hours and is labeled Open weekends: Middletown Post Office, 8:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Are Odessa, Townsend, Smyrna, Clayton, or Newark included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities or sit outside Middletown town, so they are not listed under Middletown. This page also does not cover a Middletown in another state.
