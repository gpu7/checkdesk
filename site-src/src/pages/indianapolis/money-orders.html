For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All 17 listed Indianapolis Post Offices sell money orders. Nine are labeled Open evenings: Bacon until 5:30 p.m.; Eastgate until 6:00 p.m.; the Indianapolis Post Office and Wanamaker until 6:30 p.m.; Castleton, New Augusta, Nora, and Southport until 7:00 p.m.; and Park Fletcher until 8:00 p.m. 16 publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. The Indianapolis Post Office is closed on Saturday and Sunday. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
