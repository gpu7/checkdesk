Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. 3 Portland Post Offices list money orders on their official pages and geocode inside Portland city limits (the Census place Portland city, GEOID 2360545, in Cumberland County). Two print Portland, Maine: Downtown Station at 400 Congress St Ste 9998 and Station A at 622 Congress St Ste 9998. One community-named office is also listed, because both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Portland city and the official page lists money orders: Cliff Island at 11 Sunset Rd Unit 1, which prints Cliff Island. Displayed addresses keep the official printed USPS city. Cliff Island is a community inside Portland city, not a separately incorporated place. Weekday retail there closes at 4:45 p.m. after a 1:00–2:00 p.m. midday closure, so it is not labeled Open evenings. This is Portland, Maine, in Cumberland County, not Portland, Oregon, and not South Portland, Westbrook, Falmouth, or Cape Elizabeth. Downtown Station is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail there runs until 5:30 p.m. Station A weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Downtown Station 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., Station A 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and Cliff Island 8:30 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Portland city.
