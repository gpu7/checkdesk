Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six Las Vegas Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Las Vegas, Nevada, and are inside Las Vegas city limits (the Census place Las Vegas city, GEOID 3240000, in Clark County): Crossroads at 6210 N Jones Blvd, Garside at 1801 S Decatur Blvd, King at 1801 N Martin L King Blvd, Red Rock Vista at 2449 N Tenaya Way, Summerlin at 1611 Spring Gate Ln, and Topaz at 2675 N Decatur Blvd. This is Las Vegas, Nevada, in Clark County, not Henderson, North Las Vegas, Mesquite, Boulder City, or a Clark County census designated place such as Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Sunrise Manor, or Winchester, and not Las Vegas, New Mexico. Henderson, North Las Vegas, Reno, and Sparks stay on separate lists. None is labeled Open evenings. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Crossroads, Garside, King, Red Rock Vista, Summerlin, and Topaz, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Five listed offices match the current and 2020 Census address files in Las Vegas city, and all six official map points fall there. Garside is listed under a documented map-point fallback: the official page already prints Las Vegas, Nevada; the official map point is in Las Vegas city on both Census files; and the address 1801 S Decatur Blvd did not match on either file after retries. Matches that dropped the ZIP and snapped to 1801 N Decatur Blvd in ZIP 89108 were rejected, because that is a different address. Fifteen offices that print Las Vegas are held because they geocode in unincorporated Clark County census designated places, not in Las Vegas city: Airport, East Las Vegas, James C Brown Jr, Las Vegas at 1001 E Sunset Rd Rm 1030, Paradise Valley, Poe 683, and Silverado, in the Paradise CDP; Desert Inn, in the Winchester CDP; Emerald, Poe Flamingo, Spring Valley, and Westridge, in the Spring Valley CDP; and Huntridge, Sunrise, and Winterwood, in the Sunrise Manor CDP. Two offices that print Las Vegas and geocode in Las Vegas city are held because their official pages do not list money orders: Downtown Las Vegas at 201 Las Vegas Blvd S Ste 100, and Poe 695 at 8350 W Cheyenne Ave. No other office in the Nevada locator sweep geocodes into Las Vegas city. No Las Vegas location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built.
