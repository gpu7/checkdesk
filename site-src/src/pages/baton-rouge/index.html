Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six Baton Rouge Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and are inside Baton Rouge city limits (the Census place Baton Rouge city, GEOID 2205000, in East Baton Rouge Parish): Audubon at 4500 Government St, Broadview at 235 Marilyn Dr, Istrouma at 5200 Longfellow Dr, Old Hammond at 10936 Old Hammond Hwy, Scotlandville at 7980 Plank Rd, and Southeast at 4535 Bennington Ave. This is the municipal city of Baton Rouge, not the East Baton Rouge Parish total, and not St. George, Central, Zachary, or Baker. None is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 4:30 p.m. or earlier. All six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Istrouma, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Audubon, Broadview, Old Hammond, Scotlandville, and Southeast, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Audubon, Broadview, Istrouma, and Old Hammond match the current and 2020 Census address files in Baton Rouge city, and both official map points fall there. Scotlandville and Southeast are listed on a documented map-point fallback: both official map points are in Baton Rouge city on the current and 2020 files, and both official pages already print Baton Rouge.
