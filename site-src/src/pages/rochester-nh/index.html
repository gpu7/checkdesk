Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. One Rochester Post Office lists money orders on its official page, prints Rochester, New Hampshire, and is inside Rochester city limits (the Census place Rochester city, GEOID 3365140, in Strafford County): Rochester at 20 Allen St. This is Rochester, New Hampshire, in Strafford County, not Dover, not Somersworth, and not Rochester, Minnesota.
