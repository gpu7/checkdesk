The nearby-city lists now include Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters checked on their official pages on Nov 11, 2026. "None listed" under check cashing means no checked location in that city names check cashing on its official page. The new Walmart Supercenter pages in Aurora, Commerce City, Englewood, and Lakewood show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hubs without services and are not counted for money orders or check cashing; call the store. Wheat Ridge and Federal Heights have no Walmart Supercenter inside city limits, and Walmart Neighborhood Markets are not listed. City pages list a location under the city whose limits it is in, even when its mailing address names a different city, and places outside these city limits (such as unincorporated Adams County) are not listed.

Colorado Springs, added Nov 11, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters inside Colorado Springs city limits. Its Walmart Supercenter pages also show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. Stores and Post Offices with a Colorado Springs mailing address that are outside city limits (for example in Monument, Fountain, Cimarron Hills, or Security-Widefield) are not listed.

Fort Collins, added Nov 11, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters inside Fort Collins city limits. Its two Walmart Supercenter pages also show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. Stores in nearby towns such as Timnath, Windsor, and Loveland are not listed.

Thornton, added Nov 11, 2026, lists King Soopers stores and a Walmart Supercenter inside Thornton city limits. Its Walmart Supercenter page also shows only a general "Money Services" label, so that store is on the city hub without services. No Thornton store page names check cashing, and no Post Office is listed yet. Stores in Northglenn, Westminster, Brighton, and other nearby cities are not listed under Thornton.

Arvada, added Nov 11, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and a Walmart Supercenter inside Arvada city limits. Its Walmart Supercenter page and one King Soopers page (Candelas) show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. No Arvada store page names check cashing. Stores in Westminster, Wheat Ridge, Lakeside, Golden, and other nearby places are not listed under Arvada.

Westminster, added Nov 11, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters inside Westminster city limits. Its three Walmart Supercenter pages also show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. No Westminster store page names check cashing. Stores in Arvada, Broomfield, Federal Heights, Northglenn, Thornton, and other nearby places are not listed under Westminster.

Greeley, added Nov 11, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters inside Greeley city limits. Its two Walmart Supercenter pages also show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. No Greeley store page names check cashing. Stores and Post Offices in Evans, Windsor, Eaton, Loveland, and other nearby places are not listed under Greeley.

Pueblo, added Nov 12, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and Walmart Supercenters inside Pueblo city limits. Its two Walmart Supercenter pages also show only a general "Money Services" label, so those stores are on the city hub without services. No Pueblo store page names check cashing. Pueblo West, an unincorporated community, and stores in Fountain, Cañon City, and other nearby places are not listed under Pueblo.

Centennial, added Nov 12, 2026, lists Post Offices, King Soopers stores, and a Walmart Supercenter inside Centennial city limits. Its Walmart Supercenter page also shows only a general "Money Services" label, so that store is on the city hub without services. No Centennial store page names check cashing.
