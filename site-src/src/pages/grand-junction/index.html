How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Grand Junction Post Office sells money orders?

The Grand Junction Post Office (241 N 4th St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Grand Junction Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Grand Junction Post Office (241 N 4th St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Grand Junction Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes, briefly. The Grand Junction Post Office (241 N 4th St) publishes weekday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 5:15 p.m.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Grand Junction?

The official pages for the two Grand Junction Walmart Supercenters (2545 Rimrock Ave and 2881 North Ave) show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so we do not list them for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Where can I cash a check in Grand Junction?

No Grand Junction store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. The two Walmart Supercenters list a general Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Why are Fruita, Palisade, and Clifton locations not listed?

Fruita and Palisade are separate towns, and Clifton, Fruitvale, Orchard Mesa, and Redlands are unincorporated communities, even though many addresses there use a Grand Junction mailing address. This page lists only locations inside the City of Grand Junction limits.
