Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Four Topeka Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Topeka, and geocode inside Topeka city limits (the Census place Topeka city, GEOID 2071000, in Shawnee County): Gage Center at 1430 SW Woodhull St, Hicrest at 2921 SE Adams St, North Topeka at 220 NW Lyman Rd, and the Topeka Post Office at 424 S Kansas Ave Ste 1. This is Topeka, Kansas, in Shawnee County, not Lawrence, not Silver Lake, and not a Topeka in another state. None is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. All four publish Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All four are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Topeka city. No map-point fallback was used. The Pauline Post Office at 5826 SW Topeka Blvd prints Topeka, but the Census geocoder does not place the address or the official map point in an incorporated place or CDP on the current or 2020 files (both legs are in Shawnee County), so it is held out of this list; its official page also does not list money orders. Two other offices print Topeka and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: the Topeka Post Office and the Topeka KS S&DC, both at 1410 NW Gage Blvd, Rm 111. No other office in the Kansas locator sweep geocodes into Topeka city. No Topeka location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Topeka pages are Central Time.
