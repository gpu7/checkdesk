Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Four Augusta Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Augusta, and are inside the Census place Augusta-Richmond County consolidated government (balance): Augusta, Forest Hills, Hill Station, and Peach Orchard. This is Augusta, Georgia, not Hephzibah, Blythe, Martinez, Fort Gordon, Gracewood, or North Augusta, and not an Augusta in another state. City-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 balance (GEOID 1304204), not the whole of Richmond County. Three are labeled Open evenings: the Augusta Post Office until 6:00 p.m., and Forest Hills and Peach Orchard until 5:30 p.m. Two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Forest Hills and Peach Orchard. None of the listed offices publishes Sunday hours. Hill Station closes at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays and is closed on weekends, so it is not on the evenings-and-weekends list. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. The Martinez Post Office prints Augusta but sits in the Martinez CDP in Columbia County, so it is held out of this list. Gracewood and Fort Gordon have a geocode leg in the balance but print Gracewood and Fort Gordon, so they are held out of this list. Fort Gordon's official address files did not match after 384 street-name, city, and ZIP variants; both official map points fall in the balance. Map-point fallback is only used when the printed city matches. No Augusta location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Augusta pages are local Eastern Time.

FAQ

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Augusta?

None of the checked Augusta locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. locations publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. (Open evenings) and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). No Augusta location on this list names check cashing on its official page yet. See evenings and weekends in Augusta.

Does this list show places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. The Open evenings and Open weekends labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page, last verified Nov 15, 2026. Confirm hours with the official source or the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in Augusta?

No location on this Augusta list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Augusta Post Office pages here list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
