How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Montrose city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Montrose line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Montrose Post Office sells money orders?

The Montrose Post Office (321 S 1st St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Montrose Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Montrose Post Office (321 S 1st St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Montrose Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Montrose Post Office (321 S 1st St) publishes weekday retail hours from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Does the Montrose Walmart sell money orders?

Its official store page does not say. The Montrose Supercenter (16750 S Townsend Ave) shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name money orders, so we list it on this hub without services. The store publishes hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Are there King Soopers money desks in Montrose?

Not on this list. No King Soopers store prints a Montrose address.

Where can I cash a check in Montrose?

No Montrose location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. The Montrose Supercenter page shows only a general "Money Services" label; call the store before you go.

Why is the Montrose Annex not listed?

The Montrose Annex (3025 Woodgate Rd) is inside Montrose city limits, but its official USPS record lists no retail hours and no money orders, so there is no checked service to list.

Why are Olathe, Delta, and Ridgway locations not listed?

Olathe and Ridgway are separate towns, and Delta and Ouray are separate cities. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside Montrose city limits, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside Montrose city limits.
