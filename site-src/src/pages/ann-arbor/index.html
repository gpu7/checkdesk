Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Ann Arbor Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Ann Arbor, Michigan, and are inside Ann Arbor city limits (the Census place Ann Arbor city, GEOID 2603000, in Washtenaw County): Ann Arbor at 2075 W Stadium Blvd, Green Road at 3000 Green Rd, and Liberty at 200 E Liberty St Lbby.
