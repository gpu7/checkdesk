Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Evans city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Evans line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Evans Post Office sells money orders?

The Evans Post Office (3344 11th Ave) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Evans Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Evans Post Office (3344 11th Ave) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Evans Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Evans Post Office (3344 11th Ave) publishes weekday retail hours from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Are there King Soopers or Walmart money desks in Evans?

Not on this list. No King Soopers store and no Walmart Supercenter prints an Evans address. The nearest official store pages print Greeley, and the Census geocoder places those stores in Greeley city.

Where can I cash a check in Evans?

No Evans location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet.

Why are the Walmart Supercenter on 23rd Avenue and the King Soopers University Square store not listed?

Those stores, Walmart Supercenter 980 (3103 S 23rd Ave) and King Soopers University Square (2712 11th Ave), print Greeley. The U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places both addresses inside Greeley city limits, so they are not listed under Evans.

Why are Greeley and La Salle locations not listed?

Greeley is a separate city, and La Salle is a separate town. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside Evans city limits, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside Evans city limits.
