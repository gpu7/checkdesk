How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Phoenix city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and either the official printed city is Phoenix, or both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Phoenix city and money-order tags are present. The displayed city is the official printed USPS city. Phoenix is an incorporated city inside Maricopa County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Phoenix mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here. The Laveen Post Office is listed under the neighborhood-name rule; its official map points return Maricopa County only, with no place, and that caveat is documented on the Phoenix pages.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Which Phoenix Post Offices sell money orders?

All 17 Phoenix locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Phoenix city limits, print a different city, or do not list money orders on the official page.

Which Phoenix Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

The Phoenix Post Office at 4949 E Van Buren St publishes weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m. (Arizona Time), so it is labeled Open evenings. The other listed offices close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays. A 5 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.

Which Phoenix Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Fifteen of the 17 listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends, including Laveen (10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.). None of them publishes Sunday retail hours. The Downtown Phoenix and Capitol Post Offices are closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Why is the Laveen Post Office listed if it prints Laveen?

Its official page prints Laveen, not Phoenix. Both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Phoenix city (GEOID 0455000), and the official page lists money orders, so it is listed under the approved neighborhood-name rule. The displayed city stays the official printed USPS city. Official map points return Maricopa County only, with no incorporated place, just off the city boundary; that caveat does not fail the listing standard, which uses the two address-file legs, and is documented here. Laveen is a community inside Phoenix city, not a separately incorporated place.

Why is the Laveen Annex not listed?

It also prints Laveen, and both Census address files place the address inside Phoenix city, but the official page lists no retail hours and no money orders, so it is not listed. Official map points also return Maricopa County only, with no place.

Why are the Northwest and Sunnyslope Post Offices not listed?

Both print Phoenix and sit inside Phoenix city limits, and both publish retail hours, including Saturday. Their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not on this money-order directory. The Northwest Annex and two processing plants (the North Valley SDC and the Rio Salado S&DC) are left off for the same reason.

Is the Daisy Mountain Post Office in Phoenix?

Yes. Its official page prints Phoenix (ZIP 85087, which is also listed as the official New River ZIP). The Census geocoder places the address and official map point inside Phoenix city limits on both the current and 2020 files, not in the New River or Anthem census-designated places, so it is listed here.
