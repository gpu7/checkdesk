Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Lincoln Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Lincoln, Nebraska, and are inside Lincoln city limits (the Census place Lincoln city, GEOID 3128000, in Lancaster County): College View at 5100 Van Dorn St, Indian Village at 1201 Calvert St, and Lincoln at 700 R St. This is Lincoln, Nebraska, in Lancaster County, not Waverly, Hickman, or a Lincoln township.
