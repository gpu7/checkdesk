How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Huntsville city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Huntsville line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Huntsville Post Offices sell money orders?

Six Huntsville Post Offices have official USPS location pages that list money orders: Downtown Huntsville (615 Clinton Ave W), West (3605 Governors Dr SW), Mastin Lake (2501 Mastin Lake Rd NW), Wynn Drive (645 Wynn Dr NW), Haysland (8401 Whitesburg Dr SE), and the Huntsville Post Office (3408 Wall Triana Hwy). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Which Huntsville Post Office is open after 5 p.m.?

The Wynn Drive Post Office (645 Wynn Dr NW) publishes weekday retail hours from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., so it is labeled Open evenings. The other five close at 5 p.m. on weekdays.

Which Huntsville Post Offices are open on Saturday?

Four publish Saturday retail hours: West (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Mastin Lake (10 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Wynn Drive (10 a.m. to 2 p.m.), and Haysland (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.). Downtown Huntsville and the Huntsville Post Office on Wall Triana Hwy are closed on Saturday. None publishes Sunday hours.

Do the Huntsville Walmart stores sell money orders?

Their official store pages do not say. The five Huntsville Supercenters show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name money orders, so we list them on this hub without services. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Huntsville?

No Huntsville location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. The Huntsville Walmart Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label; call the store before you go.

Why is the Redstone Arsenal Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints a Huntsville address, but the Census geocoder has no match for the address, and the official USPS map point falls in the Redstone Arsenal area, outside Huntsville city limits. It is held for review and not listed. It is also on a military installation, which may limit public access.

Why are Madison and Owens Cross Roads locations not listed?

Madison and Owens Cross Roads are separate cities, so stores and Post Offices that sit in those cities are not listed here. Two Walmart Supercenters that print Madison or Owens Cross Roads addresses fall inside Huntsville city limits on the Census map; they are held for review and not listed yet.
