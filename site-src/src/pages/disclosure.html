Disclosure: how this site makes money

Last updated Nov 10, 2026

Check Desk is a small, independent site. Here is how it is set up, so you can judge the pages with that in mind.

No ads, no paid rankings, no affiliates

At this time the site has no ads, no popups, no paid placements, and no affiliate programs. We have not signed up for any affiliate program, and commissions do not influence what we publish because there are no commissions.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated first, and any commercial link will be labeled on the page where it appears.

What money does not change

  • Facts and prices still come from official or clearly cited public pages, with a last-verified date.
  • We do not rank locations by what they pay us.
  • We do not publish reviews or testimonials we have not verified.
