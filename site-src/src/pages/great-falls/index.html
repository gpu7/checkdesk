Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Great Falls Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Great Falls, Montana, and are inside Great Falls city limits (the Census place Great Falls city, GEOID 3032800, in Cascade County): Charles M Russell at 3400 9th Ave S and Great Falls at 215 1st Ave N. This is Great Falls, Montana, in Cascade County, not Cascade, Belt, or Neihart. The Balance of Cascade County stays off this list. Billings, Missoula, Bozeman, and Butte stay on separate lists.
