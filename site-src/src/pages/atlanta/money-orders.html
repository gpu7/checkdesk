For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All eighteen listed Atlanta Post Offices sell money orders. Two are labeled Open evenings: the Greenbriar Mall Post Office and the Pharr Road Post Office, both until 6:00 p.m. Nine publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Ben Hill, Broadview, Cascade Heights, Central City, Civic Center, East Atlanta, Greenbriar Mall, Howell Mill, and Pharr Road. None of the listed offices publishes Sunday hours.
