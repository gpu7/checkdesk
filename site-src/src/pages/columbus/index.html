Columbus check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Columbus Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Columbus, and are inside Columbus city limits: Beallwood, Downtown Columbus, and Lindsey Creek. This is Columbus, Georgia, the Census place Columbus city, which is coextensive with Muscogee County, not a Columbus in another state, and not Fort Benning, Midland, or Fortson. All three close at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays and are closed on Saturday and Sunday, so none is labeled Open evenings or Open weekends, and Columbus has no evenings-and-weekends page (Check Desk does not publish an empty list). A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Two offices print a different city but have a Columbus city geocode leg, so they are held out of this list: Midland, which prints Midland, with current and 2020 address files and both official map points in Columbus city; and Fortson, which prints Fortson; the official city and ZIP (Fortson, 31808) did not match after street-name, city, and ZIP retries, but a documented ZIP retry using 31909 matched 2511 Double Churches Rd, Columbus, GA, 31909 in Columbus city on both address files, and both official map points fall there. Map-point fallback is only used when the printed city matches. Four in-city records are not listed, because their official pages do not list money orders: Baker Village, Columbus, Columbus GA S&DC, and Wynnton. No Columbus location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Columbus pages are local Eastern Time.

What we checked
