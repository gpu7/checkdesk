Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Idaho Falls Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Idaho Falls, and geocode inside Idaho Falls city limits (the Census place Idaho Falls city, GEOID 1639700, in Bonneville County): Eagle Rock at 875 N Capital Ave and the Idaho Falls Post Office at 605 4th St. This is Idaho Falls, Idaho, in Bonneville County, not Idaho County, and not an Idaho Falls in another state, and not Ammon, Iona, or Ucon. Both close at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays and are closed on Saturday and Sunday, so neither is labeled Open evenings or Open weekends, and Idaho Falls has no evenings-and-weekends page (Check Desk does not publish an empty list). A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Idaho Falls city. No other office in the Idaho locator sweep geocodes into Idaho Falls city. No Idaho Falls location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Idaho Falls pages are Mountain Time.
