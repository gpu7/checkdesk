Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Newport News Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Newport News, Virginia, and are inside Newport News city limits (the Census place Newport News city, GEOID 5156000, an independent city (county-equivalent GEOID 51700)): Denbigh at 14104 Warwick Blvd, Lee Hall at 17415 Warwick Blvd, Newport News at 101 25th St, Oyster Point at 739 Thimble Shoals Blvd Ste 501, Parkview at 636 79th St, Patrick Henry at 685 Turnberry Blvd, and Warwick at 10830 Warwick Blvd. This is Newport News, Virginia, an independent city, at /newport-news/. This is not Hampton, not Norfolk, not Newport, Rhode Island, and not Newport, Kentucky. Fort Eustis stays off this list because its official page prints Fort Eustis, not Newport News. The listed offices are in the independent city of Newport News (county-equivalent GEOID 51700). Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole-place GEOID 5156000. None is labeled Open evenings. Four publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Denbigh and Patrick Henry, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Parkview and Warwick, 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Three other offices are not listed.
