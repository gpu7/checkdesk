Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. One Waipahu Post Office lists money orders on its official page, prints Waipahu, and geocodes inside the Waipahu CDP: the Waipahu Post Office at 94-245 Leoku St. This is Waipahu, Hawaii, a Census community in Honolulu County, not a Waipahu in another state, and not Urban Honolulu, East Honolulu, Pearl City, or Hilo. It is not labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 4:00 p.m. It publishes Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and is labeled Open weekends. It does not publish Sunday hours.
