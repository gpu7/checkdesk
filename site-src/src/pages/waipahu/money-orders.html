For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. The listed Waipahu Post Office sells money orders. It is not labeled Open evenings. It publishes Saturday hours and is labeled Open weekends. It does not publish Sunday hours.
