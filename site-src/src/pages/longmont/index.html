How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Is the Longmont Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Longmont Post Office (201 Coffman St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Longmont Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes. The Longmont Post Office (201 Coffman St) publishes weekday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Longmont sell money orders?

All three King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: Pace Street (1611 Pace St), Hover Street (995 S Hover St), and North Main (2255 Main St). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Longmont?

No Longmont store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and all three Longmont King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Longmont?

The official pages for the two Longmont Walmart Supercenters (2514 Main St and 2285 E Ken Pratt Blvd) show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so we do not list them for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Why are Niwot and Firestone locations not listed?

Niwot is an unincorporated community, and Firestone, Frederick, and Mead are separate towns. Their store pages and Post Offices print those places, not Longmont. This page lists only locations inside Longmont city limits.
