How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Hartford city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Hartford. Hartford is an incorporated city, coextensive with Hartford town, in the Capitol Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Hartford Post Offices sell money orders?

The seven Hartford locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Hartford, Ann Street, Old State House, Washington Street, Blue Hills, Barry Square, and Unity Plaza. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Murphy Rd Carrier Annex prints Hartford and sits in the city, but it does not list money orders, so it is not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Hartford city limits or print a different city, including offices in West Hartford, East Hartford, Wethersfield, and Newington.

Which Hartford Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

One of the seven listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: the Hartford Post Office at 141 Weston St, until 6:00 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Washington Street, Blue Hills, Barry Square, and Old State House close at 5 p.m. on weekdays. Ann Street and Unity Plaza close earlier, and both publish a midday break in weekday hours.

Which Hartford Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Five of the seven listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Hartford (8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.), Washington Street (9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.), Blue Hills and Barry Square (9:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.), and Unity Plaza (9:00 a.m. to noon). Ann Street and Old State House do not publish Saturday or Sunday retail hours. None of the seven publishes Sunday hours.

Why are Ann Street, Barry Square, Blue Hills, Old State House, Unity Plaza, and Washington Street listed?

Each official page prints Hartford, not those station names, as the city. For five of them, the Census Geocoder matches the address, and the official USPS map point, inside Hartford city on both the current and 2020 files. The Old State House office is listed on the map-point fallback described below. An office whose printed city is Hartford and whose address or official map point is inside the city is listed, even when the official name is a neighborhood or station name.

Why is the Old State House Post Office listed on a map-point fallback?

Its official page prints Hartford and lists money orders. The Census address file did not match 80 State House Sq on either the current or 2020 file, including street-name retries. Shortening the street to State St snapped to a different street, so that match was rejected. The official USPS map point falls inside Hartford city on both Census files, so the office is listed under the documented map-point fallback.

Why is Murphy Rd Carrier Annex not listed?

Its official page prints Hartford, and the Census geocoder places 70 Murphy Rd inside Hartford city on both files. The official page does not list money orders and does not publish a BUSINESS retail-hours block. An office is listed only when its own official page lists money orders.

Are West Hartford, East Hartford, Wethersfield, or Newington included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities and sit outside Hartford city, so they are not listed under Hartford.
