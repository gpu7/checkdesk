How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Miami city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Miami. Miami is an incorporated city in Miami-Dade County. This page covers Miami, Florida, not a Miami in another state. Many nearby addresses use a Miami mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality, in a census-designated place, or in unincorporated Miami-Dade County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Where can I get a money order near me in Miami?

This page lists checked Miami locations whose official USPS page lists money orders: seven Post Offices. None is labeled Open evenings. USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1]. See the money-order guide.

Does this list show money-order desks that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. Published hours were last verified Nov 15, 2026. Confirm with the location before you go.

Are there 24-hour money-order desks in Miami?

None of the listed Miami money-order locations publishes 24-hour hours. Miami Post Offices on this list close at 5 p.m. on weekdays (with no Sunday hours). Confirm hours with the Post Office before you go.

How much is a money order at the Post Office?

USPS publishes one national fee, the same at every Post Office: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Domestic USPS money orders are limited to $1,000 each. Pay with cash or a debit card; USPS says you cannot pay with a credit card [1].

Can I buy a money order in Miami on a Sunday?

No location on this Miami money-orders list publishes Sunday hours. All seven Miami Post Offices on this list are closed Sunday; six publish Saturday retail hours while Metro is closed Saturday [1].

Where can I cash a money order in Miami?

You can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free. USPS says to bring a primary photo ID and not to sign the money order until you are at the counter [1]. All seven Miami Post Offices on this list are official USPS locations that list money orders. For Western Union or MoneyGram money orders, see the published policies on the money-order guide and the check-cashing fee guide; Check Desk does not invent a store's till policy.

Is a cashier's check the same as a money order?

No. A cashier's check is a bank-issued product, and Check Desk does not list banks or places that sell cashier's checks. This page lists Miami locations whose official pages list money orders. USPS domestic money orders are limited to $1,000 each ($2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00; $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00) [1]. See the money-order guide.
