How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Miami city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Miami. Miami is an incorporated city in Miami-Dade County. This page covers Miami, Florida, not a Miami in another state. Many nearby addresses use a Miami mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality, in a census-designated place, or in unincorporated Miami-Dade County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Miami Post Offices sell money orders?

All seven Miami locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Allapattah, Coconut Grove, Flagler Miami, Jose Marti, Little River, Metro, and Shenandoah. USPS publishes the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Twenty-seven offices that also list money orders print Miami but sit outside the city, so they are held for review. Brickell, Buena Vista, Buena Vista Carrier Annex, and Hibiscus Carrier Annex do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders print a different city, such as Miami Beach or Coral Gables, and are outside Miami city limits.

Which Miami locations are open evenings or weekends?

None of the seven listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so none is labeled Open evenings. Six of the seven listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Allapattah, Coconut Grove, Flagler Miami, Jose Marti, Little River, and Shenandoah. Metro is weekday-only. None publishes Sunday hours.

Is Miami Beach included?

No. Miami Beach, Miami Springs, Miami Shores, Miami Gardens, Coral Gables, North Miami, and other nearby municipalities are separate incorporated places. Their Post Offices print those cities, not Miami, and they are not listed under Miami. Offices that print Miami but sit in those places, or in a census-designated place or unincorporated Miami-Dade County, are held out of this list.

Where can I cash a check in Miami?

No Miami location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Miami have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Miami?
