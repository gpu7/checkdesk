Where to get a money order and what official pages say it costs

You can buy a money order at any Post Office, at Walmart, at King Soopers Money Services desks, and at stores that sell Western Union or MoneyGram money orders. Only some of them publish a price. This page shows the published prices first and says plainly when a price is not published.

Short version. Walmart publishes a $1 maximum money-order fee and says exact charges vary by location [2]. USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, cash or debit only [1]. King Soopers, Western Union, 7-Eleven, and MoneyGram do not publish a national purchase fee, so the answer there is Not published, call ahead [3][4][5][7]. USPS and MoneyGram both state a $1,000 limit per money order [1][7].

Published fees first, lower first. "Not published, call ahead" means the official page does not give a dollar figure. "Not stated" means the official page does not say; it does not mean there is no rule. A chain maximum is not a promise of a store's till price.

What to bring

  • Cash or a debit card. USPS, Walmart, and King Soopers all name cash or debit [1][2][3]. USPS says you cannot use a credit card [1].
  • Enough for the amount plus the fee. USPS says you pay the dollar value of the money order plus the issuing fee [1].
  • ID for larger amounts. Walmart asks for government photo ID for purchases over $1,000 [2]. MoneyGram says to bring identification if needed [7].
  • Need more than $1,000? USPS money orders are limited to $1,000 each [1]. MoneyGram gives the same US limit [7].

Cashing a money order you received

  • USPS money orders: cashing one at a Post Office is free. Do not sign it until you are at the counter, and bring a primary photo ID. USPS says you can also cash them at most banks and some stores [1].
  • Western Union money orders: you may be able to cash them at banks and other retailers, which may charge a fee. Not every Western Union location can cash them [4], and 7-Eleven does not cash Western Union money orders [5].
  • Walmart lists Western Union and MoneyGram money orders among the items it cashes [9]; see the check-cashing fee guide for its published maximums.

Lost money orders, refunds, and expiration

  • USPS: you cannot stop payment, but a lost or stolen postal money order can be replaced for a $23.00 processing fee. Start a Money Order Inquiry at any Post Office with your receipt. Confirming a loss can take up to 30 days and investigating up to 60 days [1]. Domestic postal money orders never expire [1].
  • Western Union: refunds are requested online, and the official FAQ lists refund processing fees of $0 for money orders of $5 or less, $5 for $5.01 to $99.99, and $15 for $100.00 and above [6]. Western Union money orders do not expire, but depending on the state of purchase, a non-refundable service charge may be taken if one is not used within one to three years [4].
  • Keep your receipt. USPS and Western Union both use it to check status or start a refund [1][4].

What the official pages do not say

  • Walmart does not publish a maximum money-order amount [2].
  • King Soopers, Western Union, 7-Eleven, and MoneyGram do not publish a national purchase fee. We do not estimate one.
  • Bank and credit union money-order fees are set by each institution. We have not collected them.

FAQ

How much is a money order at the Post Office?

USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1].

How much is a money order at Walmart?

Walmart publishes a $1 maximum fee and says exact charges vary by location but won't be over $1 [2].

How much is a money order at King Soopers?

King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee. Its official page says the cost will vary by state, so call the store's Money Services desk [3].

Can I buy a money order with a credit card?

Not at USPS: it takes cash or a debit card and says you cannot pay with a credit card. Walmart and King Soopers list cash or debit [1][2][3].

What is the most a money order can be for?

USPS domestic money orders are limited to $1,000 each. MoneyGram also states a $1,000 US limit that may vary slightly by state and location [1][7].
