Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Chandler Post Offices list money orders on their official pages and have addresses and official map points inside Chandler city limits: Andersen Springs, Chandler, and Ocotillo. All three publish Saturday hours. None of the three is open after 5 p.m. on weekdays. No Chandler-printed office was held, and no in-city office without money-order tags was found. The Ocotillo office is in ZIP 85248, which is also an official Sun Lakes ZIP, but its address and official map point are inside Chandler city, not the Sun Lakes census-designated place. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Chandler city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Chandler. Chandler is an incorporated city inside Maricopa County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Chandler mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Which Chandler Post Offices sell money orders?

All three Chandler locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Andersen Springs, Chandler, and Ocotillo. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Chandler city limits or print a different city.

Which Chandler Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the three listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so none is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Chandler Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

All three listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Andersen Springs, Chandler, and Ocotillo. None of the three publishes Sunday retail hours.

Is the Ocotillo Post Office in Sun Lakes?

No. Its official page prints Chandler, and the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in Chandler city. ZIP 85248 also appears as an official Sun Lakes ZIP, but this office is not in the Sun Lakes census-designated place.

Where can I cash a check in Chandler?

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Chandler?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Chandler?

Does this list show places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. The Open evenings and Open weekends labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page, last verified Nov 14, 2026. Confirm hours with the official source or the location before you go.
