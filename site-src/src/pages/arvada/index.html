How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses outside the city also use an Arvada mailing address.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Arvada Post Offices are open on Saturday?

Both Post Offices listed here publish Saturday retail hours from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.: the Arvada Post Office (5885 Allison St) and Indian Tree (7765 Wadsworth Blvd). Neither publishes Sunday hours.

Which Arvada Post Office is open after 5 p.m.?

Both publish weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Arvada sell money orders?

Six of the seven King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: 58th and Ralston, Lloyd King Center, Lake Arbor, 80th and Sheridan, 64th and Ward, and Westwoods. The Candelas page shows only a general "Money Services" label. King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Arvada?

No Arvada store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and all seven Arvada King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Arvada?

The official Arvada Walmart Supercenter page shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name check cashing, so we do not list it for it. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.
