How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Jacksonville city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Jacksonville. Jacksonville is a consolidated city-county, but the Census place used here is the SUMLEV 162 incorporated place “Jacksonville city” (GEOID 1235000), the Duval County balance, not the whole county. Jacksonville Beach, Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Baldwin are separate municipalities and are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Jacksonville Post Offices sell money orders?

All fifteen Jacksonville locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Murray Hill, Springfield, South Jacksonville, Lake Forest, Carver, Jacksonville NAS, Southpoint, North Jacksonville, Monument, Mayport Naval Station, Lake Shore, Westland, Pottsburg, Mandarin, and Arlington. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The Baldwin Post Office also lists money orders, but it is held for review because the address and official map point fall in Baldwin town, not inside Jacksonville city. The Kings Rd office, Jacksonville FL SDC, Gateway Carrier Annex, and Baymeadows DDC do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Jacksonville city limits or print a different city, including the offices in Jacksonville Beach, Atlantic Beach, Orange Park, and Saint Johns.

Which Jacksonville Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Five of the fifteen listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Murray Hill and Mandarin (until 6:00 p.m.), Westland (until 6:30 p.m.), and Southpoint and Pottsburg (until 5:30 p.m.). A 5 p.m. close does not count. South Jacksonville, Lake Forest, Carver, North Jacksonville, Monument, Lake Shore, and Arlington close at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays. Springfield, Jacksonville NAS, and Mayport Naval Station close earlier than 5:00 p.m.

Which Jacksonville Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Eleven of the fifteen listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Murray Hill, South Jacksonville, Lake Forest, Southpoint, North Jacksonville, Monument, Lake Shore, Westland, Pottsburg, Mandarin, and Arlington. Springfield, Carver, Jacksonville NAS, and Mayport Naval Station do not publish Saturday or Sunday retail hours. None of the fifteen publishes Sunday hours.

Why are the Jacksonville NAS and Mayport Naval Station Post Offices listed if the address files did not match?

Both official pages already print Jacksonville, and both official USPS map points fall inside Jacksonville city on the current and 2020 Census files. The current and 2020 address files did not match 569 Childs St or 460 Massey Ave after street-name, city, and ZIP retries, and no retry snapped to a different street. Under the documented map-point fallback, an office is listed when the printed city already matches and the official map point is inside the city. OpenStreetMap was not used.

Why is the Baldwin Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints Jacksonville, but the Census Geocoder places the address and the official USPS map point in Baldwin town (GEOID 1203250), not inside Jacksonville city, on both the current and 2020 files. Baldwin is a separate incorporated town in Duval County. An office whose address and official map point are not in Jacksonville city is held for review and not listed. The map-point fallback does not apply when the official point is not in the city.

Is Jacksonville Beach included?

No. Jacksonville Beach, Atlantic Beach, and Neptune Beach are separate incorporated cities in Duval County. Their Post Offices print those cities, not Jacksonville, and they are not listed under Jacksonville. Baldwin town is also a separate municipality.
