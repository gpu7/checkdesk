Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Four Macon Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Macon, and are inside the Census place Macon-Bibb County, which is coextensive with Bibb County: Macon, Mulberry, Pio Nono, and Zebulon Branch. This is Macon, Georgia, not a Macon in another state, and not Lizella or Dry Branch. Two are labeled Open evenings: the Macon Post Office until 6:00 p.m., and the Zebulon Branch Post Office until 5:30 p.m. Both of those also publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None of the listed offices publishes Sunday hours. Mulberry and Pio Nono close at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays and are closed on weekends, so they are not on the evenings-and-weekends list. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Dry Branch and Lizella have addresses and official map points inside Macon-Bibb County but print Dry Branch and Lizella, so they are held out of this list. Two in-city records are not listed, because their official pages do not list money orders: Macon GA S&DC and South Macon, both at 1740 Rocky Creek Rd. Map-point fallback is only used when the printed city matches. No Macon location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Macon pages are local Eastern Time.
