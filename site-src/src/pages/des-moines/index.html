Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six Des Moines Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Des Moines, and geocode inside Des Moines city limits (the Census place Des Moines city, GEOID 1921000, in Polk and Warren counties; all six listed offices are in Polk County): Beaverdale, Capital Square, Des Moines, East Side, South Des Moines, and University Station. This is Des Moines, Iowa, in Polk County, not West Des Moines, not Urbandale, not Pleasant Hill, not Ankeny, and not a Des Moines in another state. One is labeled Open evenings: the Des Moines Post Office, until 5:30 p.m. South Des Moines publishes Saturday hours, 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and is labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours.
