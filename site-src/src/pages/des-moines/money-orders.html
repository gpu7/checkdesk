For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All six listed Des Moines Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: the Des Moines Post Office, until 5:30 p.m. South Des Moines publishes Saturday hours, 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and is labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. Beaverdale, Capital Square, East Side, and University Station are closed on Saturday and Sunday and close at 5:00 p.m. or earlier on weekdays, so they are not on the evenings-and-weekends list. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
