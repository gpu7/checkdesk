Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Frederick Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Frederick, Maryland, and are inside Frederick city limits (the Census place Frederick city, GEOID 2430325, in Frederick County): College Estates at 1301 W 7th St and Frederick at 201 E Patrick St. This is Frederick, Maryland, in Frederick County, not Frederick, Colorado, and not Brunswick, Walkersville, Thurmont, or Middletown. One is labeled Open evenings: the Frederick Post Office at 201 E Patrick St, until 6:00 p.m. College Estates weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Both publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: College Estates 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and Frederick 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both match the current and 2020 Census address files in Frederick city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Frederick MD SDC at 201 E Patrick St, the same address as the listed Frederick Post Office, prints Frederick and geocodes in the city, but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not listed. No other office in the Maryland locator sweep geocodes into Frederick city. No Frederick location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Frederick pages are Eastern Time.
