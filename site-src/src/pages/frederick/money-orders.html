For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Both listed Frederick Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: the Frederick Post Office at 201 E Patrick St, until 6:00 p.m. College Estates weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Both publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: College Estates 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and Frederick 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
