What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Pacific Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Pacific Time. Of the 30 listed San Diego Post Offices, only Carmel Mountain Postal Store publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. (6 p.m.) and is labeled Open evenings, and 26 publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5 p.m. weekday close does not count as Open evenings. Four listed offices have no evening and no weekend retail hours, so they are not on this page: Southeastern, Naval Hospital, Paradise Hills, and La Jolla Village.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Diego city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). San Diego is an incorporated city inside San Diego County, not a consolidated city-county. Neighborhood-named offices whose official pages print a community name such as La Jolla or San Ysidro, rather than San Diego, are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Diego city and money-order tags are present; the displayed city is the official printed USPS city. Chula Vista, National City, Coronado, and other separately incorporated places stay off this list.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Does this page show locations that are open now?

No. This page does not have live open or closed status. Labels come from hours published on official USPS location pages, last verified Nov 19, 2026. Check the published hours in the table and confirm with the location before you go.

No location on this San Diego list names check cashing on its official page yet, so none is listed for it at night. The Post Offices here list money orders, not general check cashing. See the San Diego hub for every checked location.
