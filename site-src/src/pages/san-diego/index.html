How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside San Diego city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). San Diego is an incorporated city inside San Diego County, not a consolidated city-county. Neighborhood-named offices whose official pages print a community name such as La Jolla or San Ysidro, rather than San Diego, are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Diego city and money-order tags are present; the displayed city is the official printed USPS city. Chula Vista, National City, Coronado, and other separately incorporated places stay off this list.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Which San Diego Post Offices sell money orders?

All 30 San Diego locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. 27 print San Diego; La Jolla, La Jolla Village, and San Ysidro are also listed because both Census address files place the address inside San Diego city and the official pages list money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. NAS North Island also lists money orders, but it is held because the official map point falls in Coronado city. Eleven San Diego-printed records inside the city do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside San Diego city limits or print a different city.

Which San Diego Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Only Carmel Mountain Postal Store publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. (6 p.m.) and is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count. The other 29 listed offices close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays.

Which San Diego Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

26 of the 30 listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. None of the 30 publishes Sunday hours. The four listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Southeastern, Naval Hospital, Paradise Hills, and La Jolla Village.

Are the La Jolla and San Ysidro Post Offices listed?

Yes. La Jolla, La Jolla Village, and San Ysidro are listed because both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside San Diego city and the official pages list money orders. Displayed addresses keep the official printed USPS city. The Ocean Beach and Pacific Beach offices print San Diego and were already listed. La Jolla Annex UC also prints San Diego, but it is not listed because its official page does not list money orders.

Why is the NAS North Island Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints San Diego, but the Census Geocoder did not match the address after street-name retries, and the official USPS map point falls in Coronado city on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose official map point is not in San Diego city is held for review and not listed.

Where can I cash a check in San Diego?

No San Diego location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in San Diego have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in San Diego?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in San Diego?

None of the checked San Diego locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. location publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m. (Open evenings) and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). No San Diego location on this list names check cashing on its official page yet. See evenings and weekends in San Diego.

Does this list show places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. The Open evenings and Open weekends labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page, last verified Nov 19, 2026. Confirm hours with the official source or the location before you go.
