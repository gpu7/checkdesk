FAQ

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Durham?

None of the checked Durham locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. None publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m., and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). No Durham location on this list names check cashing on its official page yet. See evenings and weekends in Durham.

Can I cash a check in Durham?

No location on this Durham list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Durham Post Office pages here list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
