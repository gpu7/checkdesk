Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Bowling Green Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Bowling Green, and geocode inside Bowling Green city limits (the Census place Bowling Green city, GEOID 2108902, in Warren County): the Bowling Green Post Office at 5300 Scottsville Rd and the Bowling Green KY SDC Post Office, also at 5300 Scottsville Rd. The two listed offices are distinct USPS location IDs that print the same street address, the same retail hours, and the same phone number; both official pages list money orders, so both are listed. This is Bowling Green, Kentucky, in Warren County, not Bowling Green, Ohio. Both are labeled Open evenings, until 5:30 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours, 9:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Bowling Green city. No map-point fallback was used.
