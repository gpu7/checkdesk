How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Fountain city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a Colorado Springs ZIP code, even when they sit in Fountain or in another place.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Fountain Post Office sells money orders?

The Fountain Post Office (101 S Santa Fe Ave) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Fountain Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Fountain Post Office (101 S Santa Fe Ave) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Fountain Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Fountain Post Office (101 S Santa Fe Ave) publishes weekday retail hours from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Do the Fountain Walmarts sell money orders?

Their official store pages do not say. The Fountain S South Us Hwy 85 87 Supercenter (6310 S Us Highway 85-87) and the Fountain Venetucci Blvd Supercenter (4425 Venetucci Blvd) each show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name money orders, so we list them on this hub without services. Both stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Why is the Venetucci Boulevard Walmart listed if it has a Colorado Springs ZIP code?

The official store page for the Fountain Venetucci Blvd Supercenter (4425 Venetucci Blvd) prints Fountain, CO 80906. The U.S. Census Bureau address geocoder found no match for that address, and the official store-page map point falls inside Fountain city limits, so the store is listed here. It is not listed under Colorado Springs.

Why is the Fountain King Soopers not listed?

The Fountain King Soopers (6255 Sawcut Pt) prints a Fountain address, and its official store-page map point falls inside Fountain city limits, but its official page lists no Money Services and no money orders, so there is no checked service to list.

Where can I cash a check in Fountain?

No Fountain location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. The two Fountain Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label; call the store before you go.

Why are Colorado Springs, Security, and Fort Carson locations not listed?

Colorado Springs is a separate city, and Security-Widefield and Fort Carson are census designated places outside Fountain city limits. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside Fountain city limits, even when they are a short drive away or share a ZIP code. This page lists only locations inside Fountain city limits.
