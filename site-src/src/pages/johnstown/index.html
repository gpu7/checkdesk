Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. The Johnstown Post Office is the only Johnstown location whose official page lists money orders. No King Soopers store and no Walmart Supercenter prints a Johnstown address. Walmart publishes a chain-wide $1 maximum money-order fee [2] at stores that sell them. King Soopers says its money-order cost "will vary by state" and does not publish a fee [3].

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the Town of Johnstown limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Johnstown line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

What is not on this list yet

  • Check cashing: no Johnstown location we checked names check cashing, so this town has no check-cashing list yet. See the check-cashing fee guide.
  • King Soopers: no King Soopers store prints a Johnstown address. The nearest official King Soopers pages print Windsor, Firestone, or Loveland, and the Census geocoder places those stores outside the Town of Johnstown limits.
  • Walmart Supercenters: no Walmart Supercenter prints a Johnstown address. The nearest official Supercenter pages print Loveland, and the Census geocoder places those stores outside the Town of Johnstown limits.
  • Nearby towns and cities: Milliken, Gilcrest, Mead, Berthoud, Windsor, Firestone, and Platteville are separate towns, and Evans, Greeley, and Loveland are separate cities, so their stores and Post Offices are not listed here, even when they are close to Johnstown.
  • Post Office contract units (postal counters inside other businesses) have not been checked.
  • Bill pay and prepaid reload: no Johnstown location we checked names bill pay or prepaid reload on its official page, so this town has no bill-pay list yet.
  • Payday and title-loan storefronts, including ACE Cash Express, are not listed, even when they also cash checks. See ACE's check-cashing policy.
  • Stand-alone Western Union and MoneyGram agents and other grocery chains have not been checked.

FAQ

Which Johnstown Post Office sells money orders?

The Johnstown Post Office (121 Castle Pines Ave) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Johnstown Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Johnstown Post Office (121 Castle Pines Ave) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9:30 a.m. to 12 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Johnstown Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Johnstown Post Office (121 Castle Pines Ave) publishes weekday retail hours from 8:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 2 p.m. to 4:30 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Are there King Soopers or Walmart money desks in Johnstown?

Not on this list. No King Soopers store and no Walmart Supercenter prints a Johnstown address. The nearest official store pages print Windsor, Firestone, or Loveland, and the Census geocoder places those stores outside the Town of Johnstown.

Where can I cash a check in Johnstown?

No Johnstown location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet.

Why are the Loveland Walmart Supercenters and the Windsor, Firestone, and Loveland King Soopers stores not listed?

Those stores print Loveland, Windsor, or Firestone. The U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places Walmart Supercenter 1008 (250 W 65th St), Walmart Supercenter 953 (1325 Denver Ave), and King Soopers Eagle Drive (1275 Eagle Dr) inside Loveland city limits, King Soopers Windsor (1520 Main St) inside the Town of Windsor, and King Soopers Firestone (6110 Firestone Blvd) inside the Town of Firestone, so they are not listed under Johnstown.

Why are Milliken, Windsor, and Loveland locations not listed?

Milliken, Gilcrest, Mead, Berthoud, Windsor, Firestone, and Platteville are separate towns, and Evans, Greeley, and Loveland are separate cities. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside the Town of Johnstown, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside the Town of Johnstown limits.
