How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Tucson city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Tucson. Tucson is an incorporated city inside Pima County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Tucson mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Which Tucson Post Offices sell money orders?

All eight Tucson locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Tucson city limits, print a different city, or do not list money orders on the official page.

Which Tucson Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the eight listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so none is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count. The Tucson Post Office at 1501 S Cherrybell Stra publishes weekday hours until 6:30 p.m., but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not on this directory.

Which Tucson Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Four of the eight listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Coronado, Fort Lowell, Mission, and Rincon. None of the eight publishes Sunday retail hours.

Why are the Casas Adobes and Mountain View Post Offices not listed?

Their official pages print Tucson, but the Census geocoder places both addresses and official map points in the Casas Adobes census-designated place, outside Tucson city limits. An office printed Tucson whose address or map point falls outside the city is held for review and not listed.

Why is the Oro Valley Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints Tucson, but the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in Oro Valley town, a separate incorporated place, so it is held for review and not listed.

Why is the South Tucson Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints Tucson, but the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in South Tucson city, a separate incorporated city surrounded by Tucson. It is held for review and not listed. The official page also does not list money orders.

Why is the Tucson Post Office on Cherrybell Stravenue not listed?

It prints Tucson and sits inside Tucson city limits, and it publishes weekday hours until 6:30 p.m. and Saturday hours. Its official page does not list money orders, so it is not on this money-order directory.

Is the Davis Monthan AFB Post Office in Tucson?

Yes. Its official page prints Tucson. The Census Geocoder had no address match after street-name retries, so the listing uses the official USPS map point, which the geocoder places inside Tucson city limits on both the current and 2020 files.
