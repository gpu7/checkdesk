Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Twenty-one Baltimore Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Baltimore, Maryland, and are inside Baltimore city limits (the Census place Baltimore city, GEOID 2404000, an independent city, not Baltimore County): Arlington, Baltimore, Calvert, Carroll, Clifton East End, Druid, Fells Point, Franklin, Govans, Hamilton, Hampden Roland Park, Highlandtown, Market Center, Mondawmin Mall, Mount Washington, Northwood, Raspeburg, Roland Park, Southeast, Walbrook, and Waverly. This is Baltimore, Maryland, an independent city, not a Baltimore in another state, and not Towson, Catonsville, Dundalk, Essex, or Brooklyn. None publishes Sunday hours. The two listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Calvert and Market Center. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All twenty-one match the current and 2020 Census address files in Baltimore city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Two other offices print Baltimore and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: the Baltimore MD E Lexington SDC at 1000 E Lexington St, and the South Post Office at 1200 S Hanover St Ste 100.
