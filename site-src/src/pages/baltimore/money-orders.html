For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All twenty-one listed Baltimore Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: the Baltimore Post Office at 900 E Fayette St Rm 118, until 7:00 p.m. Nineteen of the twenty-one listed offices publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. The two listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Calvert and Market Center. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
