East Honolulu check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two East Honolulu Post Offices list money orders on their official pages and geocode inside the East Honolulu CDP: Aina Haina and Hawaii Kai. Official USPS pages print Honolulu, not East Honolulu, because East Honolulu is a Census community and is not a USPS printed city. They are listed here by geocode-in-CDP, the rule approved for this Hawaii exception, and they are held off the Urban Honolulu list. This is East Honolulu, Hawaii, a Census community in Honolulu County, not a Honolulu in another state, and not Urban Honolulu, Pearl City, or Waipahu. Neither office is labeled Open evenings; Aina Haina closes at 4:00 p.m. on weekdays and Hawaii Kai at 4:30 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. Neither publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Waialae Kahala prints Honolulu but geocodes in Urban Honolulu CDP, so it is listed on the Urban Honolulu page, not here. No other office in the Hawaii locator sweep geocodes into East Honolulu CDP. No East Honolulu location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the East Honolulu pages are Hawaii Time (HST).
