Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. One Bozeman Post Office lists money orders on its official page, prints Bozeman, Montana, and is inside Bozeman city limits (the Census place Bozeman city, GEOID 3008950, in Gallatin County): Babcock at 32 E Babcock St. This is Bozeman, Montana, in Gallatin County, not Belgrade, Manhattan, Three Forks, or West Yellowstone. The Balance of Gallatin County stays off this list. Billings, Missoula, Great Falls, and Butte stay on separate lists. None is labeled Open evenings or Open weekends, and Bozeman has no evenings-and-weekends page (Check Desk does not publish an empty list). A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. The listed office matches the current and 2020 Census address files in Bozeman city, and the official map point falls there on both files. No map-point fallback was used. One office that prints Bozeman is held because the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point fully inside Bozeman city: the Bozeman Post Office at 2201 Baxter Ln. The current address file did not match; the 2020 address file and both official map-point legs place it in unincorporated Gallatin County, with no incorporated place and no CDP. Street-name retries did not place it in Bozeman city. Map-point fallback is not used, because the official point is not inside Bozeman city. No other office in the Montana locator sweep geocodes into Bozeman city. No Bozeman location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Bozeman pages are Mountain Time.
