How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Orlando city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Orlando. Orlando is an incorporated city in Orange County. This page covers Orlando, Florida, not an Orlando in another state. Many nearby addresses use an Orlando mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality, in a census-designated place, or in unincorporated Orange County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Orlando Post Offices sell money orders?

All seven Orlando locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: College Park, Dixie Village, Downtown Orlando, Herndon, Orange Blossom, Orlando MOWU, and Orlo Vista. USPS publishes the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Eleven offices that print Orlando sit outside the city, so they are held for review. Gore Street Carrier Annex, Lee Vista, Mall Carrier Annex, and the Orlando record at 10401 Post Office Blvd W Fl 2 do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders print a different city, such as Winter Park or Ocoee, and are outside Orlando city limits.

Which Orlando locations are open evenings or weekends?

One listed office publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: the Orlando MOWU Post Office, until 8:00 p.m. Four of the seven listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: College Park, Dixie Village, Orlando MOWU, and Orlo Vista. The Orlando MOWU Post Office also publishes Sunday hours, from 12:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. Downtown Orlando, Herndon, and Orange Blossom are weekday-only. Downtown Orlando opens at 7:00 a.m. and closes at 5:00 p.m.; a 5 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings, so it is not on the evenings-and-weekends page.

Is Winter Park included?

No. Winter Park, Maitland, Eatonville, Edgewood, Belle Isle, Ocoee, Winter Garden, Apopka, and other nearby municipalities are separate incorporated places. Their Post Offices print those cities, not Orlando, and they are not listed under Orlando. Offices that print Orlando but sit in a census-designated place or in unincorporated Orange County are held out of this list.
