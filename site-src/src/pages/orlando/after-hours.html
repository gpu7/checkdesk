How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Orlando city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Orlando. Orlando is an incorporated city in Orange County. This page covers Orlando, Florida, not an Orlando in another state. Many nearby addresses use an Orlando mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality, in a census-designated place, or in unincorporated Orange County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Does this page show locations that are open now?

No. This page does not have live open or closed status. Labels come from hours published on official USPS location pages, last verified Nov 15, 2026. Check the published hours in the table and confirm with the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in Orlando at night?

No location on this Orlando list names check cashing on its official page yet, so none is listed for it at night. The Post Offices here list money orders, not general check cashing. See the Orlando hub for every checked location.

Can I buy a money order in Orlando on a weekend or at night?

Orlando money-order locations on this list publish weekend hours, and publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m. Four of the seven listed Post Offices publish Saturday retail hours, one stays open past 5 p.m. on weekdays, and the Orlando MOWU Post Office publishes Sunday retail hours (12 p.m. to 6 p.m.) [1].
