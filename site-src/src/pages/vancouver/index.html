Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. FAQ

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Vancouver?

None of the checked Vancouver locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. locations publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. (Open evenings) and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). No Vancouver location on this list names check cashing on its official page yet. See evenings and weekends in Vancouver.

Can I cash a check in Vancouver?

No location on this Vancouver list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Vancouver Post Office pages here list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
