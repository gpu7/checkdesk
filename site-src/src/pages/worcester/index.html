Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six Worcester Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Worcester, Massachusetts, and are inside Worcester city limits (the Census place Worcester city, GEOID 2582000, in Worcester County): Greendale at 290 W Boylston St, Lincoln Mall at 560 Lincoln St Ste 8, Quinsigamond Village at 347 Greenwood St Ste 1, Webster Square at 1049 Main St Ste 5, West Side at 381 Chandler St, and Worcester at 4 E Central St. This is Worcester, Massachusetts, in Worcester County, not a Worcester in another state, and not Leominster, Fitchburg, Gardner, Southbridge, Paxton, Shrewsbury, or Auburn. `/auburn/` remains Auburn, Maine. One is labeled Open evenings: the Worcester Post Office at 4 E Central St, until 6:00 p.m. Greendale and West Side weekday retail close at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Lincoln Mall, Quinsigamond Village, and Webster Square close earlier. All six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Greendale and Worcester, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; Lincoln Mall, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Quinsigamond Village, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Webster Square, 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; and West Side, 8:45 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All six match the current and 2020 Census address files in Worcester city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Worcester MA SDC at 4 E Central St, the same address as the listed Worcester Post Office, prints Worcester and geocodes in the city, but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not listed. No other office in the Massachusetts locator sweep geocodes into Worcester city. No Worcester location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Worcester pages are Eastern Time.
