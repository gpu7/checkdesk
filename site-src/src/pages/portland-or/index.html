Portland check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

This page lists locations inside Portland city limits, each checked on an official USPS location page. So far that is seventeen Post Offices. It shows which services each page lists, the published hours, and what the brand publishes about fees.

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1].
