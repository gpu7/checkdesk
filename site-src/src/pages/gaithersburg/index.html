Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Gaithersburg Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Gaithersburg, Maryland, and are inside Gaithersburg city limits (the Census place Gaithersburg city, GEOID 2431175, in Montgomery County): Diamond Farms at 23 Firstfield Rd, Gaithersburg at 21 S Summit Ave, and Kentlands at 618 Center Point Way. This is Gaithersburg, Maryland, in Montgomery County, not Rockville, and not Montgomery Village, Germantown, or Redland.
