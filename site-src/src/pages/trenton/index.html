Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Trenton Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Trenton, New Jersey, and are inside Trenton city limits (the Census place Trenton city, GEOID 3474000, in Mercer County): Downtown at 20 S Montgomery St and Villa Park at 1137 Hamilton Ave. This is Trenton, New Jersey, in Mercer County, not a Mercer County census designated place such as White Horse, Mercerville, or Yardville, and not a township in the Balance of Mercer County. Newark, Jersey City, Paterson, and Elizabeth stay on separate lists. None is labeled Open evenings. All two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Downtown and Villa Park, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Five offices that print Trenton are held because the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point fully inside Trenton city: Independence at 2465 S Broad St Ste C2, whose official map point is in the White Horse CDP; Mercerville at 339 Highway 33 Ste 2, in the Mercerville CDP; Trenton Carrier Annex at 435 US Highway 130 Unit B, in the Yardville CDP; Circle at 2601 Brunswick Ave, whose current and 2020 lookups find no incorporated place or census designated place (the current address and both official map-point lookups are in Mercer County); and Trenton at 680 US Highway 130, whose current and 2020 address and official map-point lookups find no incorporated place or census designated place (they are in Mercer County). No map-point fallback. No other office in the New Jersey locator sweep geocodes into Trenton city. No Trenton location names check cashing.
