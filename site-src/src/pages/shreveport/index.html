Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Shreveport Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Shreveport, Louisiana, and are inside Shreveport city limits (the Census place Shreveport city, GEOID 2270000): Downtown Shreveport at 333 Milam St, Huntington Station at 6719 Pines Rd, Industrial at 1446 Hawn Ave, Lynbrook at 241 Lynbrook Blvd, Meriwether at 8340 Mansfield Rd, Shreveport Main Office at 2400 Texas Ave, and Southfield at 1925 E 70th St. This is Shreveport city, which sits mostly in Caddo Parish and partly in Bossier Parish, not Bossier City. Six of the listed offices geocode in Caddo Parish. The Industrial Post Office at 1446 Hawn Ave geocodes in Shreveport city in Bossier Parish, not in Bossier City. Four are labeled Open evenings: Huntington Station and Southfield, until 5:15 p.m.; Meriwether, until 5:30 p.m.; and Shreveport Main Office, until 6:00 p.m. None publishes Saturday or Sunday hours, so none is labeled Open weekends. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
