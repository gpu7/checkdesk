Short version. USPS charges $2.65 for a domestic money order of $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1]. Pay with cash or a debit card; credit cards are not accepted. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free. Dover Post Office at 445 Bank Ln lists money orders and is inside Dover city. It is labeled Open evenings and Open weekends. The Dover AFB Post Office also lists money orders, but it prints Dover AFB, not Dover, and is not listed here.

Hours are local time in Dover (Eastern Time).
