How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Dover city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Dover. Dover is an incorporated city in Kent County and is the state capital. Many nearby addresses use a Dover mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Dover Post Offices sell money orders?

The one Dover location on this page lists money orders on its official USPS location page: Dover Post Office at 445 Bank Ln. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The Dover AFB Post Office also lists money orders, but it is not listed here because its official page prints Dover AFB, not Dover. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Dover city limits and print those other cities, including offices in Camden Wyoming, Cheswold, Little Creek, Magnolia, Smyrna, and Clayton.

Which Dover Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

The one listed office publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: Dover Post Office at 445 Bank Ln, until 6:00 p.m. Monday retail hours start at 7:30 a.m.; Tuesday through Friday start at 8:00 a.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Dover Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

The one listed office publishes Saturday retail hours and is labeled Open weekends: Dover Post Office, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Why isn't the Dover AFB Post Office listed?

Its official page prints Dover AFB, not Dover. A location is listed under Dover only when it prints Dover and sits inside Dover city. The Census Geocoder did not match 442 13th St on the current or 2020 address file after street-name retries, but the official map point falls inside Dover city, so the row is held rather than listed.

Are Camden, Wyoming, Cheswold, or Smyrna included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities or sit outside Dover city, so they are not listed under Dover.
