How much is a money order at the Post Office?

USPS publishes one national fee, the same at every Post Office: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Domestic USPS money orders are limited to $1,000 each. Pay with cash or a debit card; USPS says you cannot pay with a credit card [1].

Can I buy a money order in Raleigh on a Sunday?

No location on this Raleigh money-orders list publishes Sunday hours. All seven Raleigh Post Offices on this list are closed Sunday; five publish Saturday retail hours while Capitol and Century are closed Saturday [1].

Where can I cash a money order in Raleigh?

You can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free. USPS says to bring a primary photo ID and not to sign the money order until you are at the counter [1].
