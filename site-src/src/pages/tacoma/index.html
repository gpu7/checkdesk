Tacoma check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Five Tacoma Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Tacoma, Washington, and are inside Tacoma city limits (the Census place Tacoma city, GEOID 5370000, in Pierce County (GEOID 53053)): Downtown Tacoma at 1102 A St, Evergreen at 4001 S Pine St, Lincoln at 3705 S G St, Proctor at 3801 N 27th St, and South Tacoma at 3503 S 56th St. This is Tacoma, Washington, at /tacoma/. This is not University Place, not Parkland, not Lakewood, and not a Tacoma in another state. The listed offices are in Pierce County (GEOID 53053). Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole-place GEOID 5370000. Two are labeled Open evenings: Evergreen, until 7:00 p.m. and Proctor, until 6:00 p.m. All two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Evergreen and Proctor, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Three other offices are not listed. Two print Tacoma, Washington, but the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point inside Tacoma city: Parkland (all four legs are in Parkland CDP) and University Place (all four legs are in University Place city; the official page prints Tacoma). One prints Tacoma and geocodes in Tacoma city but does not list money orders: TCCF East. No Tacoma location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Tacoma pages are Pacific Time.
