How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Fairbanks city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Fairbanks. Fairbanks is an incorporated city inside Fairbanks North Star Borough. Many nearby addresses use a Fairbanks mailing city but sit in a census-designated place, another city, or unincorporated borough land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Fairbanks Post Offices sell money orders?

The Downtown Station Post Office at 315 Barnette St is the only Fairbanks location on this page, and its official USPS location page lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Fairbanks city limits or print a different city.

Which Fairbanks Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Downtown Station publishes weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m. (Alaska Time), so it is labeled Open evenings.

Which Fairbanks Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Downtown Station is closed on Saturday and Sunday, so it is not labeled Open weekends. No Fairbanks city location on this page publishes weekend retail hours.

Why is the Fairbanks Main Office not listed?

Its official page prints Fairbanks, but the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in the College census-designated place, outside Fairbanks city limits. An office printed Fairbanks whose address or map point falls outside the city is held for review and not listed.

Why is the College Post Office not listed?

The official locator name is College, and the official page prints a Fairbanks address (755 Fairbanks St). The Census geocoder places that address and the official map point in the College census-designated place, outside Fairbanks city limits, so it is held for review and not listed.

Why is the Fort Wainwright Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints Fort Wainwright, not Fairbanks. The Census geocoder places the address and official map point inside Fairbanks city, but the printed city and the geocode disagree, so it is held for review and not listed.

Is the North Pole Post Office in Fairbanks?

No. Its official page prints North Pole, and the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in North Pole city, a separate incorporated city in Fairbanks North Star Borough. It is not listed under Fairbanks.

Where can I cash a check in Fairbanks?

No Fairbanks location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Fairbanks have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Fairbanks?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
