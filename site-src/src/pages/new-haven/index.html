How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside New Haven city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is New Haven. New Haven is an incorporated city, coextensive with New Haven town, in the South Central Connecticut Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which New Haven Post Offices sell money orders?

The six New Haven locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: New Haven, Yale, Fair Haven, Westville, Amity Retail Store, and Kilby. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside New Haven city limits or print a different city, including offices in West Haven, East Haven, Hamden, and North Haven.

Which New Haven Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Two of the six listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: the New Haven Post Office at 50 Brewery St and the Yale Post Office at 206 Elm St, both until 5:30 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Amity Retail Store, Fair Haven, and Westville close at 5 p.m. on weekdays. Kilby publishes weekday retail hours only from 10:00 a.m. to noon.

Which New Haven Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

All six listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. None of the six publishes Sunday hours. The main New Haven office is open Saturday 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Westville is open Saturday 8:30 a.m. to noon. The other four are open Saturday 8:00 a.m. to noon.

Why are the Fair Haven, Westville, Amity Retail Store, and Kilby Post Offices listed?

Each official page prints New Haven, not Fair Haven, Westville, Amity, or Kilby as the city. The Census Geocoder matches the address, and the official USPS map point, inside New Haven city on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose printed city is New Haven and whose address and map point are inside the city is listed, even when the official name is a neighborhood or station name.

Are West Haven, East Haven, Hamden, or North Haven included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities and sit outside New Haven city, so they are not listed under New Haven.

Where can I cash a check in New Haven?

No New Haven location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in New Haven have not been checked yet.

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
