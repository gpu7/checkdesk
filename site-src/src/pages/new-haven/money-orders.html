Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six New Haven Post Offices below list money orders on their official pages, print New Haven, and have addresses and official map points inside New Haven city limits: New Haven, Yale, Fair Haven, Westville, Amity Retail Store, and Kilby. Two publish a weekday close after 5 p.m.: the New Haven Post Office and Yale, both until 5:30 p.m. All six publish Saturday hours. None publishes Sunday hours. West Haven, East Haven, Hamden, and North Haven are separate municipalities and are not listed here. Walmart Supercenters in New Haven have not been checked yet.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside New Haven city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is New Haven. New Haven is an incorporated city, coextensive with New Haven town, in the South Central Connecticut Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

Where can I get a money order near me in New Haven?

Are there 24-hour money-order desks in New Haven?

None of the listed New Haven money-order locations publishes 24-hour hours. New Haven Post Offices on this list close between 12 p.m. and 5:30 p.m. on weekdays (with no Sunday hours). Confirm hours with the Post Office before you go.
