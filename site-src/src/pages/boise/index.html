Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Boise Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Boise, and geocode inside Boise city limits (the Census place Boise City city, GEOID 1608830, in Ada County): Boise Airport Station at 2625 W Apollo St, Borah at 750 W Bannock St, Cole Village at 3485 N Cole Rd, Collister at 4650 W State St, Five Mile at 1350 S Five Mile Rd, Oregon Trail at 2208 E Summersweet Dr, and Overland at 1650 W Targee St. This is Boise, Idaho, in Ada County, not Boise County, and not a Boise in another state, and not Meridian, Garden City, Eagle, Kuna, or Star.
