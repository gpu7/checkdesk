Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Covington Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Covington, and geocode inside Covington city limits (the Census place Covington city, GEOID 2117848, in Kenton County): the Covington Post Office at 700 Scott St and the Latonia Post Office at 111 E Southern Ave. This is Covington, Kentucky, in Kenton County, not a Covington in another state, and not Erlanger, Fort Mitchell, or Florence.
