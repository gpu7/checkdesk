Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Biloxi Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Biloxi, Mississippi, and are inside Biloxi city limits (the Census place Biloxi city, GEOID 2806220, in Harrison County): Biloxi at 135 Main St, Keesler A F B at 201 Meadows Dr, and West Biloxi at 2110 Pass Rd.
