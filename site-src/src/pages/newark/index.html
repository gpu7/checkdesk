How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Newark city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Newark. Newark is an incorporated city in New Castle County. This page covers Newark, Delaware, not Newark, New Jersey. Many nearby addresses use a Newark mailing ZIP but sit in another municipality or in unincorporated New Castle County, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Newark Post Offices sell money orders?

The two Newark locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Federal Post Office at 110 E Main St and Newark Post Office at 401 Ogletown Rd. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The Newark Carrier Annex is not listed here because its official page prints Bear, not Newark, and the address sits in the Glasgow census-designated place. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Newark city limits and print those other cities, including offices in Bear, Wilmington, New Castle, Elkton, and Hockessin.

Which Newark Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

One of the two listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: Newark Post Office at 401 Ogletown Rd, until 6:00 p.m. Weekday retail hours there start at 7:30 a.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Federal Post Office closes at 4:00 p.m. on weekdays.

Which Newark Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Both listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Federal Post Office (8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.) and Newark Post Office (8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.). Neither publishes Sunday hours.

Why isn't the Newark Carrier Annex listed?

Its official page prints Bear, not Newark. A location is listed under Newark only when it prints Newark and sits inside Newark city. The Census Geocoder places 125 Rickey Blvd in the Glasgow census-designated place, outside Newark city.

Are Bear, Brookside, Glasgow, Elkton, or Wilmington included?

No. Those are separate municipalities or census-designated places. Their Post Offices print those cities or sit outside Newark city, so they are not listed under Newark. This page also does not cover Newark, New Jersey or Newark, Maryland.

Where can I cash a check in Newark?

No Newark location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Newark have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Newark?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
