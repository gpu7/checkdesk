How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Brighton Post Office sells money orders?

The Brighton Post Office (90 N 4th Ave) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Brighton Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Brighton Post Office (90 N 4th Ave) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Brighton Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes. The Brighton Post Office (90 N 4th Ave) publishes weekday retail hours from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Brighton sell money orders?

Two King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: 4th and Bromley Lane (500 E Bromley Ln) and 50th and Bridge Street (100 N 50th Ave). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Brighton?

No Brighton store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and the two Brighton King Soopers pages listed here show a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Brighton?

The official page for the Brighton W Bromley Lane Walmart Supercenter (60 W Bromley Ln) shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name check cashing, so we do not list it for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Why is the Walmart on E 128th Ave not listed?

Its official store page prints a Brighton address (7101 E 128th Ave), but the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places that address inside Thornton city limits.
