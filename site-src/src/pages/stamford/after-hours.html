What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Eastern Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Eastern Time. Of the four listed Stamford Post Offices, none publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so none is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. weekday close does not count as Open evenings. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Summer Atlantic, West Ave, and Bulls Head. None publishes Sunday hours. Glenbrook has no evening and no weekend retail hours, so it is not on this page.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Stamford city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Stamford. Stamford is an incorporated city, coextensive with Stamford town, in the Western Connecticut Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

Are there 24-hour check cashing or money-order desks in Stamford?

No location on this Stamford evenings-and-weekends list publishes 24-hour hours. None publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m., and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). The latest published weekday closing time among these Post Offices is 5 p.m.
