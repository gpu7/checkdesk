How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Stamford city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Stamford. Stamford is an incorporated city, coextensive with Stamford town, in the Western Connecticut Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Stamford Post Offices sell money orders?

The four Stamford locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Summer Atlantic, West Ave, Bulls Head, and Glenbrook. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The two records at 427 West Ave and Stamford CT SDC print Stamford and sit in the city, but they do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Stamford city limits or print a different city, including offices in Greenwich, Darien, New Canaan, and Norwalk.

Which Stamford Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the four listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Summer Atlantic, West Ave, and Glenbrook close at 5 p.m. on weekdays. Bulls Head also closes at 5 p.m. Stamford CT SDC publishes a weekday close at 5:30 p.m., but it is not listed here because its official page does not list money orders.

Which Stamford Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Three of the four listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Summer Atlantic, West Ave, and Bulls Head. Glenbrook does not publish Saturday or Sunday retail hours. None of the four publishes Sunday hours.

Why is the Glenbrook Post Office listed?

Its official page prints Stamford, not Glenbrook as the city. The Census Geocoder matches the address, and the official USPS map point, inside Stamford city on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose printed city is Stamford and whose address and map point are inside the city is listed, even when the official name is a neighborhood name.

Why are the offices at 427 West Ave and Stamford CT SDC not listed?

All three official pages print Stamford, and the official USPS map points fall inside Stamford city on both Census files. The Stamford Post Office and West Ave Carrier Unit do not list money orders and do not publish a BUSINESS retail-hours block. Stamford CT SDC publishes weekday and Saturday retail hours, including a weekday close after 5 p.m., but it also does not list money orders. An office is listed only when its own official page lists money orders.

Are Greenwich, Darien, New Canaan, or Norwalk included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities and sit outside Stamford city, so they are not listed under Stamford.

Where can I cash a check in Stamford?

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Stamford?
