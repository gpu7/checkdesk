For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All 6 listed Charleston Post Offices sell money orders. None is labeled Open evenings. Johns Island weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., with a midday closure from 1:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Five publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Ashley River, East Bay, James Island, and Saint Andrews, 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m.; and Johns Island, 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. The Charleston Post Office is closed on Saturday and Sunday, so it is not on the evenings-and-weekends list. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
