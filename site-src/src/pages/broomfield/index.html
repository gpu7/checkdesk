How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Broomfield Post Offices sell money orders?

Both Post Offices listed here have official USPS location pages that list money orders: the Broomfield Post Office (280 E 1st Ave) and the Eagle View Post Office (8800 W 116th Cir). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is a Broomfield Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Broomfield Post Office (280 E 1st Ave) and the Eagle View Post Office (8800 W 116th Cir) both publish Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Neither publishes Sunday hours.

Is a Broomfield Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes. The Broomfield Post Office (280 E 1st Ave) publishes weekday retail hours from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., and the Eagle View Post Office (8800 W 116th Cir) from 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Broomfield sell money orders?

All three King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: Miramonte and 287 (1150 US Highway 287), Broomfield on Sheridan (12167 Sheridan Blvd), and 136th and Zuni (2355 W 136th Ave). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Broomfield?

No Broomfield store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and all three Broomfield King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Broomfield?

The official pages for the two Broomfield Walmart Supercenters (4651 W 121st Ave and 500 Summit Blvd) show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so we do not list them for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Why are Westminster and Louisville locations not listed?

Westminster, Thornton, Northglenn, Federal Heights, Arvada, Louisville, Superior, Lafayette, and Erie are separate cities and towns. Their store pages and Post Offices sit outside the City and County of Broomfield, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside Broomfield.
