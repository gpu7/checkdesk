For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All ten listed Tulsa Post Offices sell money orders. Five are labeled Open evenings: Eastside Tulsa, until 6:00 p.m., Robert W Jenkins, until 6:00 p.m., Sheridan Tulsa, until 6:00 p.m., Tulsa AMF Retail, until 9:00 p.m., and Utica Square, until 6:00 p.m. Five publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Robert W Jenkins, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Southeast Tulsa, 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Tulsa AMF Retail, 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.; Utica Square, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; West Tulsa, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.
