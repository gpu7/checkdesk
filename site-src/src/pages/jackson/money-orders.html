For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All eight listed Jackson Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Jackson, until 7:00 p.m. on weekdays. Lefleur weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Candlestick Park, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Jackson, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and North Jackson, 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Delta Station, Fondren, Lefleur, Southwest, and Westland do not publish Saturday or Sunday hours.
