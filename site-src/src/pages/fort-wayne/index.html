Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Five Fort Wayne Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Fort Wayne, and geocode inside Fort Wayne city limits (the Census place Fort Wayne city, GEOID 1825000, in Allen County): Centennial, Diplomat Plaza, Hazelwood, Northwood, and Waynedale. This is Fort Wayne, Indiana, in Allen County, not a Fort Wayne in another state, and not New Haven, Huntertown, or Leo-Cedarville. Three are labeled Open evenings: Centennial and Hazelwood until 5:30 p.m.; and Northwood until 6:30 p.m. All five publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Three are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Fort Wayne city. Two are listed under a documented fallback (printed city already Fort Wayne; official map point in Fort Wayne city on both files): Diplomat Plaza (neither address file matched 4171 Diplomat Plaza Ctr after 100 street and ZIP retries; snaps to 4171 Plaza Dr were discarded as a different street) and Northwood (the current address file matched 6041 Stellhorn Rd in Fort Wayne city; the 2020 file did not match after 35 retries). Four other offices print Fort Wayne and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: Fort Wayne, Gabriel Carrier Annex, Northbrook, and Saint Marys Station. No other office in the Indiana locator sweep geocodes into Fort Wayne city. No Fort Wayne location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Fort Wayne pages are Eastern Time.
