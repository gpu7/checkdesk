How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses outside the city also use a Pueblo mailing address.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Where can I get a money order near me in Pueblo?

This page lists checked Pueblo locations whose official store or USPS page lists money orders: two King Soopers stores and four Post Offices. are labeled Open evenings. USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1]; King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee [3]. Two other Pueblo locations (Walmart Pueblo Dillon Drive Supercenter and Walmart Pueblo W Northern Ave Supercenter) do not name money orders on their official page and appear on the Pueblo hub list instead. See the money-order guide.

Does this list show money-order desks that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. Published hours were last verified Nov 12, 2026, and King Soopers publishes store hours rather than separate money-desk hours. Confirm with the location before you go.

Are there 24-hour money-order desks in Pueblo?

None of the listed Pueblo money-order locations publishes 24-hour hours. The listed King Soopers stores publish store hours closing at 11 p.m. every day. Pueblo Post Offices on this list close between 5 p.m. and 6 p.m. on weekdays (with no Sunday hours). Money-desk hours at King Soopers are not published, so call ahead.
