Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. 10 Louisville, KY Post Offices list money orders on their official pages and are inside Louisville, Kentucky, city limits (the Census place Louisville/Jefferson County metro government (balance), GEOID 2148006, in Jefferson County, including the former Louisville city extent). Eight print Louisville: the Downtown Louisville Post Office at 835 S 7th St, the Dr Martin Luther King Jr Post Office at 2727 W Broadway, Hikes Point at 3050 Hunsinger Ln, Iroquois at 4772 Allmond Ave, Okolona at 7400 Jefferson Blvd, Pleasure Ridge Park at 6121 Greenwood Rd, Shelby at 2312 S Preston St, and the Shively Post Office at 3131 Crums Ln. Two community-named offices are also listed, because both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside the PEP/ACS balance and the official pages list money orders: Fairdale at 411 Mount Holly Rd, which prints Fairdale; and Fisherville at 15125 Old Taylorsville Rd, which prints Fisherville. Displayed addresses keep the official printed USPS city. Fairdale and Fisherville are communities inside the PEP/ACS balance, not separate incorporated places. This is Louisville, Kentucky, in Jefferson County, at /louisville-ky/, not Louisville, Colorado (already on Check Desk at /louisville/), and not Jeffersontown, St. Matthews, Shively city, Lyndon, Middletown, Anchorage, or the other remaining Jefferson County municipalities. City-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 balance, not the Jefferson County or SUMLEV 170 consolidated-city total. The Census geocoder still splits that balance into a non-functioning former-city polygon (GEOID 2148000) and the remainder (GEOID 2148006); ACS and the Population Estimates Program include the former city in the balance, so offices in either polygon are listed here. The Shively Post Office at 3131 Crums Ln prints Louisville and geocodes in the balance, not in Shively city, so it is listed here. All 10 listed offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Okolona, until 6:00 p.m. Eight publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: the Dr Martin Luther King Jr, Hikes Point, Pleasure Ridge Park, and Shively Post Offices, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Fairdale and Shelby, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Fisherville, 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.; and Okolona, 9:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. The Downtown Louisville and Iroquois Post Offices are closed on Saturday and Sunday. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All 10 match the current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside the PEP/ACS balance. No map-point fallback was used. Six offices print Louisville but sit in other Jefferson County municipalities: Anchorage, Annshire Annex (in Watterson Park), Jeffersontown, Lyndon, Middletown, and Saint Matthews. Three offices remain held because they print another city and both Census address files did not place the address inside the balance (map-point fallback is not used when the printed city disagrees): Eastwood, Harrods Creek, and Masonic Home. Eight other offices print Louisville and geocode in the balance, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed. No other office in the Kentucky locator sweep geocodes into this Louisville place and remains unlisted, except those holds. No Louisville, KY location names check cashing. What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-16. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. City limits are the Census place Louisville/Jefferson County metro government (balance), GEOID 2148006, in Jefferson County, as defined by the Population Estimates Program and ACS, checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files on 2026-11-16; community-named rows were added Nov 19, 2026 from those same pages and geocodes. Offices the geocoder places in GEOID 2148006 or in the non-functioning former Louisville city extent (GEOID 2148000) are treated as inside that place; offices in the 83 other Jefferson County municipalities are not. All 10 listed offices are in Jefferson County (GEOID 21111). Community-named offices whose official pages print a name such as Fairdale or Fisherville, rather than Louisville, are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside the PEP/ACS balance and money-order tags are present; the displayed city is the official printed USPS city.
