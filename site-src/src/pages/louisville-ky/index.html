Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Eight Louisville Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Louisville, and geocode inside Louisville, Kentucky, city limits (the Census place Louisville/Jefferson County metro government (balance), GEOID 2148006, in Jefferson County, including the former Louisville city extent): the Downtown Louisville Post Office at 835 S 7th St, the Dr Martin Luther King Jr Post Office at 2727 W Broadway, Hikes Point at 3050 Hunsinger Ln, Iroquois at 4772 Allmond Ave, Okolona at 7400 Jefferson Blvd, Pleasure Ridge Park at 6121 Greenwood Rd, Shelby at 2312 S Preston St, and the Shively Post Office at 3131 Crums Ln. This is Louisville, Kentucky, in Jefferson County, not Louisville, Colorado (already on Check Desk at /louisville/), and not Jeffersontown, St. Matthews, Shively city, Lyndon, Middletown, Anchorage, or the other remaining Jefferson County municipalities. City-limits checks use the SUMLEV 162 balance, not the Jefferson County or SUMLEV 170 consolidated-city total. The Census geocoder still splits that balance into a non-functioning former-city polygon (GEOID 2148000) and the remainder (GEOID 2148006); ACS and the Population Estimates Program include the former city in the balance, so offices in either polygon are listed here. The Shively Post Office at 3131 Crums Ln prints Louisville and geocodes in the balance, not in Shively city, so it is listed here. One is labeled Open evenings: Okolona, until 6:00 p.m. Six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: the Dr Martin Luther King Jr, Hikes Point, Pleasure Ridge Park, and Shively Post Offices, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Shelby, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and Okolona, 9:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. The Downtown Louisville and Iroquois Post Offices are closed on Saturday and Sunday. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All eight are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside the PEP/ACS balance. No map-point fallback was used. Six offices print Louisville but sit in other Jefferson County municipalities: Anchorage, Annshire Annex (in Watterson Park), Jeffersontown, Lyndon, Middletown, and Saint Matthews. Five offices geocode in the balance but print another city, so they are held out of this list: Eastwood, Fairdale, Fisherville, Harrods Creek, and Masonic Home. Eight other offices print Louisville and geocode in the balance, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed. No other office in the Kentucky locator sweep geocodes into this Louisville place except those printed-city holds. No Louisville, KY location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Louisville, KY pages are Eastern Time.

What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-16. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. City limits are the Census place Louisville/Jefferson County metro government (balance), GEOID 2148006, in Jefferson County, as defined by the Population Estimates Program and ACS, checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files. Offices the geocoder places in GEOID 2148006 or in the non-functioning former Louisville city extent (GEOID 2148000) are treated as inside that place; offices in the 83 other Jefferson County municipalities are not. All eight listed offices are in Jefferson County (GEOID 21111). OpenStreetMap was not used. Kentucky observes Eastern Time in Jefferson County.
