For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All eight listed Louisville, KY Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Okolona, until 6:00 p.m. Six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: the Dr Martin Luther King Jr, Hikes Point, Pleasure Ridge Park, and Shively Post Offices, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; Shelby, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and Okolona, 9:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. The Downtown Louisville and Iroquois Post Offices are closed on Saturday and Sunday. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
