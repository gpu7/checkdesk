How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Many addresses outside the city also use a Colorado Springs mailing address.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Colorado Springs Post Offices are open on Saturday?

All eight Post Offices listed here publish Saturday retail hours, mostly ending at 1 p.m.; Briargate is open until 3 p.m. and Templeton until 2 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. See the table for each office.

Which Colorado Springs Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

The downtown Colorado Springs Post Office (201 E Pikes Peak Ave), Briargate, Cheyenne Mountain, and Templeton publish weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m. The other four close at 5 p.m.
