Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Aurora, Illinois Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Aurora, and geocode inside Aurora city limits (the Census place Aurora city, GEOID 1703012): Aurora East at 3900 Gabrielle Ln, in DuPage County, and Aurora IL SDC at 525 N Broadway, in Kane County. This is Aurora, Illinois, not Aurora, Colorado, and not North Aurora, Batavia, or Naperville. Both are labeled Open evenings: Aurora East until 6:30 p.m., and Aurora IL SDC until 7:00 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours, 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. Neither publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Aurora city. One other office prints Aurora and geocodes in the city, but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not listed: the Aurora Post Office at 525 N Broadway. Naper West at 3900 Gabrielle Ln prints Fox Valley, not Aurora, so it is not listed even though both official map points fall in Aurora city. No Aurora, Illinois location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Aurora, Illinois pages are Central Time.
