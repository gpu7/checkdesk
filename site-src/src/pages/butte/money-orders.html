For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All three listed Butte Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Butte, until 5:30 p.m. Copper Hill weekday retail closes at 4:30 p.m., with a midday closure from 1:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. One publishes Saturday hours and is labeled Open weekends: Butte, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.
