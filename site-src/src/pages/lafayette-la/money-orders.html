For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All four listed Lafayette, Louisiana, Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Lafayette, until 5:30 p.m. Two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Lafayette, 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and Bertrand, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.
