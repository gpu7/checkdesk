How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Do King Soopers stores in Castle Rock sell money orders?

Both King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: Ridge Road (750 N Ridge Rd) and Promenade Parkway (5544 Promenade Pkwy). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Can I buy a money order at the Castle Rock Post Office?

The official USPS location page for the Castle Rock Post Office (220 S Wilcox St), read Nov 12, 2026, does not list money orders, so we do not list it. Call the Post Office before you go.

Where can I cash a check in Castle Rock?

No Castle Rock store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and both Castle Rock King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Castle Rock?

The official page for the Castle Rock Walmart Supercenter (133 Sam Walton Lane) shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name check cashing, so we do not list it for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Why are Castle Pines and Parker locations not listed?

Castle Pines and Parker are separate municipalities, and Sedalia and Franktown are unincorporated communities. Their store pages and Post Offices print those places, not Castle Rock. This page lists only locations inside the Town of Castle Rock limits.
