How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Fresno city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Fresno. Fresno is an incorporated city inside Fresno County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Fresno mailing city, print a neighborhood postal city, or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Which Fresno Post Offices sell money orders?

All eight Fresno locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Pinedale, Clinter, Cardwell, Hughes, Fresno, Woodward Park, East Fresno, and Tower. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. The Barton and Calwa Post Offices also list money orders, but they are held for review because the address and official map point fall in Calwa CDP, not inside Fresno city. Civic Center Fresno does not list money orders, so it is not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Fresno city limits or print a different city, including the offices in Clovis.

Which Fresno Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the eight listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Cardwell, Hughes, Woodward Park, East Fresno, and the Fresno Post Office close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays. Pinedale, Clinter, Tower, and the others in this list also close at 5 p.m. or earlier.

Which Fresno Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Five of the eight listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Clinter, Cardwell, Hughes, Woodward Park, and East Fresno. Pinedale, the Fresno Post Office, and Tower do not publish Saturday or Sunday retail hours. None of the eight publishes Sunday hours.

Why is the Pinedale Post Office listed?

Its official page prints Fresno, not Pinedale. The Census Geocoder matches the address, and the official USPS map point, inside Fresno city on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose printed city is Fresno and whose address and map point are inside the city is listed, even when the official name is a neighborhood name.

Why are the Barton and Calwa Post Offices not listed?

Both official pages print Fresno, but the Census Geocoder places the address and the official USPS map point in Calwa CDP, not inside Fresno city, on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose address and official map point are not in Fresno city is held for review and not listed. The map-point fallback does not apply when the official point is not in the city.

Why is the Clinter Post Office listed if the current address file did not match?

Its official page already prints Fresno, and the official USPS map point falls inside Fresno city on both the current and 2020 Census files. The 2020 address file matches 2601 E Olive Ave in Fresno city. The current address file did not match after street-name retries. Under the documented map-point fallback, an office is listed when the printed city already matches and the official map point is inside the city.

Is Clovis included?

No. Clovis is a separate incorporated city. The two records at 655 Minnewawa Ave print Clovis and sit in Clovis city, so they are not listed under Fresno.

Where can I cash a check in Fresno?
