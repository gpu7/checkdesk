Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Sioux City Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Sioux City, and geocode inside Sioux City city limits (the Census place Sioux City city, GEOID 1973335, in Woodbury and Plymouth counties; both listed offices are in Woodbury County): Sioux City and Sioux City IA SDC. Both official pages are at 214 Jackson St: the Sioux City Post Office and the Sioux City IA SDC. This is Sioux City, Iowa, in Woodbury County, not South Sioux City, Nebraska, not North Sioux City, South Dakota, not Sergeant Bluff, and not a Sioux City in another state. Neither is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours, 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. Neither publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Sioux City city.
