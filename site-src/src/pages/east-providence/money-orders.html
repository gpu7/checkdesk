For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All three listed East Providence Post Offices sell money orders. None is labeled Open evenings. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: East Providence Finance Unit, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Riverside, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; and Rumford, 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.
