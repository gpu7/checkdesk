How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Port St. Lucie city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Port St. Lucie (USPS prints Port Saint Lucie, the same place). Port St. Lucie is an incorporated city in St. Lucie County. This page covers Port St. Lucie, Florida, not a Port St. Lucie in another state. Fort Pierce, Stuart, and other nearby municipalities are separate incorporated places and are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Port St. Lucie Post Offices sell money orders?

Both Port St. Lucie locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: the Port St. Lucie Post Office and Port St. Lucie Midport. USPS publishes the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Nearby Post Offices that sell money orders print a different city, such as Fort Pierce or Stuart, and are outside Port St. Lucie city limits.

Which Port St. Lucie locations are open evenings or weekends?

One listed office publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: the Port St. Lucie Post Office, until 6:00 p.m. Both listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. Neither publishes Sunday hours. Midport closes at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays; a 5 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.

Is Fort Pierce included?

No. Fort Pierce, Stuart, St. Lucie Village, and other nearby municipalities are separate incorporated places. Their Post Offices print those cities, not Port St. Lucie, and they are not listed under Port St. Lucie.

Where can I cash a check in Port St. Lucie?

No Port St. Lucie location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Port St. Lucie have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Port St. Lucie?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
