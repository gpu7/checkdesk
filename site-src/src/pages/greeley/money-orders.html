How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses outside the city also use a Greeley mailing address.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.

FAQ

Where can I get a money order near me in Greeley?

This page lists checked Greeley locations whose official store or USPS page lists money orders: three King Soopers stores and two Post Offices. are labeled Open evenings. USPS charges $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00 [1]; King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee [3]. Two other Greeley locations (Walmart Greeley 47th Ave Supercenter and Walmart Greeley S 23rd Ave Supercenter) do not name money orders on their official page and appear on the Greeley hub list instead. See the money-order guide.

Does this list show money-order desks that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. Published hours were last verified Nov 11, 2026, and King Soopers publishes store hours rather than separate money-desk hours. Confirm with the location before you go.

Are there 24-hour money-order desks in Greeley?

None of the listed Greeley money-order locations publishes 24-hour hours. The listed King Soopers stores publish store hours closing at 11 p.m. every day. Greeley Post Offices on this list close at 5:30 p.m. on weekdays (with no Sunday hours). Money-desk hours at King Soopers are not published, so call ahead.

How much is a money order at the Post Office?

USPS publishes one national fee, the same at every Post Office: $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00. Domestic USPS money orders are limited to $1,000 each. Pay with cash or a debit card; USPS says you cannot pay with a credit card [1].

Can I buy a money order in Greeley on a Sunday?

The three King Soopers stores on this list publish Sunday store hours (6 a.m. to 11 p.m.). Both Greeley Post Offices on this list are closed Sunday and publish Saturday retail hours (9 a.m. to 1 p.m.). Money-desk hours at King Soopers are not published separately from store hours, so call ahead [3].

Where can I cash a money order in Greeley?

You can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free. USPS says to bring a primary photo ID and not to sign the money order until you are at the counter [1]. Both Greeley Post Offices on this list are official USPS locations that list money orders. For Western Union or MoneyGram money orders, see the published policies on the money-order guide and the check-cashing fee guide; Check Desk does not invent a store's till policy.

Is a cashier's check the same as a money order?

No. A cashier's check is a bank-issued product, and Check Desk does not list banks or places that sell cashier's checks. This page lists Greeley locations whose official pages list money orders. USPS domestic money orders are limited to $1,000 each ($2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00; $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00) [1]. See the money-order guide.
