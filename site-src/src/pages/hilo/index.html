Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Hilo Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Hilo, and geocode inside the Hilo CDP: the Downtown Hilo Post Office at 154 Waianuenue Ave Ste 1, and the Hilo Post Office at 1299 Kekuanaoa St. This is Hilo, Hawaii, a Census community in Hawaii County on the Island of Hawaii, not a Hilo in another state, and not Urban Honolulu, East Honolulu, Pearl City, or Waipahu. Neither office is labeled Open evenings; Downtown Hilo weekday retail closes at 4:00 p.m. and the Hilo Post Office at 4:30 p.m. Both publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Downtown Hilo 12:30 p.m. to 2:00 p.m., and the Hilo Post Office 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Neither publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Hilo CDP. No other office in the Hawaii locator sweep geocodes into Hilo CDP. No Hilo location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Hilo pages are Hawaii Time (HST).

None of the checked Hilo locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. None publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m., and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). Can I cash a check in Hilo?

No location on this Hilo list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Hilo Post Office pages here list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
