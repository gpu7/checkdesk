How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Northglenn city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they share a Northglenn ZIP code.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

What is not on this list yet

  • Check cashing: no Northglenn location we checked names check cashing, so this city has no check-cashing list yet. See the check-cashing fee guide.
  • King Soopers: no King Soopers store prints a Northglenn address. The nearest official King Soopers pages print Thornton, Westminster, Federal Heights (a Denver mailing address), Broomfield, Commerce City, or Arvada, and the Census geocoder places those stores outside Northglenn city limits.
  • Walmart Supercenters: no Walmart Supercenter prints a Northglenn address. The nearest official Supercenter pages print Thornton, Westminster, Brighton, Broomfield, or Commerce City, and the Census geocoder places those stores outside Northglenn city limits.
  • Walmart Neighborhood Markets and fuel centers are not listed in any city. The Walmart Neighborhood Market at 10755 Washington St prints Northglenn and sits inside the city, but Neighborhood Markets are omitted by policy and its official page does not show a Money Services label.
  • Nearby cities: Thornton, Westminster, Federal Heights, Denver, Broomfield, Commerce City, and Arvada are separate cities, so their stores and Post Offices are not listed here, even when they are close to Northglenn or share a ZIP code such as 80233.
  • The Northview Post Office (850 W 112th Ave) is inside the city, but its official USPS record lists no retail hours and no money orders, so it is not listed.
  • Post Office contract units (postal counters inside other businesses) have not been checked.
  • Bill pay and prepaid reload: no Northglenn location we checked names bill pay or prepaid reload on its official page, so this city has no bill-pay list yet.
  • Payday and title-loan storefronts, including ACE Cash Express, are not listed, even when they also cash checks. See ACE's check-cashing policy.
  • Stand-alone Western Union and MoneyGram agents and other grocery chains have not been checked.

FAQ

Which Northglenn Post Office sells money orders?

The Northglenn Post Office (11887 Washington St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Northglenn Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Northglenn Post Office (11887 Washington St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Northglenn Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes. The Northglenn Post Office (11887 Washington St) publishes weekday retail hours from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., so it is labeled Open evenings.

Are there King Soopers or Walmart money desks in Northglenn?

Not on this list. No King Soopers store and no Walmart Supercenter prints a Northglenn address. The only Walmart store that prints Northglenn is a Neighborhood Market, and Neighborhood Markets are not listed.

Where can I cash a check in Northglenn?

No Northglenn location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet.

Why are the King Soopers stores on 104th Avenue and 120th Avenue not listed?

Those stores, King Soopers 104th and Washington (750 E 104th Ave) and King Soopers Mission Trace (3801 E 120th Ave), print Thornton and use the 80233 ZIP code, which Northglenn also uses. The U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places both addresses inside Thornton city limits, so they are not listed under Northglenn.

Why is the Northview Post Office not listed?

The Northview Post Office (850 W 112th Ave) is inside Northglenn city limits, but its official USPS record lists no retail hours and no money orders, so there is no checked service to list.

Why are Thornton and Westminster locations not listed?

Thornton, Westminster, Federal Heights, Denver, Broomfield, Commerce City, and Arvada are separate cities. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside Northglenn city limits, even when they are a short drive away or share a ZIP code. This page lists only locations inside Northglenn city limits.
