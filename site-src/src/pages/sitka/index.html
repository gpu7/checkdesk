How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City and borough limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Sitka city and borough limits (the consolidated city-borough, checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Sitka.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Sitka Post Offices sell money orders?

The Sitka Post Office at 1207 Sawmill Creek Rd is the only Sitka location on this page, and its official USPS location page lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Sitka city and borough or print a different city.

Which Sitka Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None on this page. The Sitka Post Office publishes weekday retail hours until 5 p.m. (Alaska Time). A 5 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.

Which Sitka Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

None on this page. The Sitka Post Office is closed on Saturday and Sunday, so it is not labeled Open weekends.

Why is there no evenings-and-weekends list for Sitka?

The only checked Sitka location closes at 5 p.m. on weekdays and is closed on weekends, so it earns neither label. Check Desk does not publish an empty evenings-and-weekends list. If a later checked location qualifies, that page can be added then.

Is the Port Alexander Post Office in Sitka?

No. Its official page prints Port Alexander, and the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in Port Alexander city, a separate incorporated city in Prince of Wales-Hyder Census Area. It is not listed under Sitka.

Where can I cash a check in Sitka?

No Sitka location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Sitka have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Sitka?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
