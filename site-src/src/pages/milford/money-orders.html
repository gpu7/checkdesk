Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. One Milford Post Office below lists money orders on its official page, prints Milford, and is inside Milford city limits: Milford Post Office at 110 Causey Ave. It is labeled Open weekends. It is not labeled Open evenings, because weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours. Nearby Post Offices in Houston, Harrington, Frederica, Lincoln, Greenwood, Ellendale, and Milton also sell money orders; they sit outside Milford city and are not listed here. How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Milford city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Milford. Milford is one incorporated city that spans Sussex and Kent counties. This page covers the whole Census place, not a county part, and it covers Milford, Delaware, not a Milford in another state. Many nearby addresses use a nearby mailing city or sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.
