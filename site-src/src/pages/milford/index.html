How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Milford city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Milford. Milford is one incorporated city that spans Sussex and Kent counties. This page covers the whole Census place, not a county part, and it covers Milford, Delaware, not a Milford in another state. Many nearby addresses use a nearby mailing city or sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Milford Post Offices sell money orders?

The one Milford location on this page lists money orders on its official USPS location page: Milford Post Office at 110 Causey Ave. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Milford city limits and print those other cities, including offices in Houston, Harrington, Frederica, Lincoln, Greenwood, Ellendale, and Milton.

Which Milford Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the listed offices. Milford Post Office at 110 Causey Ave closes at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays, and a 5 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings.

Which Milford Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

The one listed office publishes Saturday retail hours and is labeled Open weekends: Milford Post Office, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is this the whole city, or only one county?

The whole city. Milford is one Census place that spans Sussex and Kent counties. The listed office sits in the Sussex County part, and it is listed because it is inside the whole-place city limits.

Are Slaughter Beach, Houston, Harrington, or Lincoln included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities or sit outside Milford city, so they are not listed under Milford. Slaughter Beach shares ZIP 19963 as an alias on the Milford ZIP page, but it is a separate town. This page also does not cover a Milford in another state.

Where can I cash a check in Milford?

No Milford location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Milford have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Milford?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
