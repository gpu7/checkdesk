Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-18. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. The eastern zone under 49 CFR 71.4 is the part of the United States east of the boundary in 49 CFR 71.5. The Tennessee segment of that boundary is 49 CFR 71.5(d). Knox County lies east of the Scott-Morgan-Roane segment, so Knoxville is in the Eastern zone. Hamilton County lies east of its own west line, which is the 71.5(d) boundary with Marion County and the Georgia line; Chattanooga is not treated as a 49 CFR 71.5(h) boundary municipality, so Chattanooga is in the Eastern zone.
