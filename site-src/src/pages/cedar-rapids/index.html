Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Cedar Rapids Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Cedar Rapids, and geocode inside Cedar Rapids city limits (the Census place Cedar Rapids city, GEOID 1912000, in Linn County): Cedar Rapids, Northeast Station, and West Station. This is Cedar Rapids, Iowa, in Linn County, not Marion, not Hiawatha, not Robins, not Fairfax, and not a Cedar Rapids in another state. Two are labeled Open evenings: Northeast Station and West Station, both until 5:30 p.m. All three publish Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. The Cedar Rapids Post Office closes at 5:00 p.m. on weekdays, so it is not labeled Open evenings. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All three are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Cedar Rapids city. No map-point fallback was used.
