How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Boulder Post Offices are open on Saturday?

All three Post Offices listed here publish Saturday retail hours: the Boulder Post Office (1905 15th St) and the Valmont Post Office (2995 55th St) from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., and the High Mar Post Office (4985 Moorhead Ave) from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.

Which Boulder Post Office is open after 5 p.m.?

All three publish weekday retail hours past 5 p.m.: the Valmont Post Office (2995 55th St) is open until 6 p.m., and the Boulder Post Office (1905 15th St) and the High Mar Post Office (4985 Moorhead Ave) are open until 5:30 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Boulder sell money orders?

All three King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: 30th Street Boulder (1650 30th St), Table Mesa (3600 Table Mesa Dr), and Gun Barrel (6550 Lookout Rd). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Boulder?

No Boulder store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and all three Boulder King Soopers pages list a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Is there a Walmart in Boulder?

Walmart's official store directory lists no Walmart store in Boulder, so none is listed here. Walmart Supercenters in nearby cities such as Lafayette, Broomfield, and Longmont are not listed under Boulder.

Is the Gun Barrel King Soopers in Boulder?

Yes. The store at 6550 Lookout Rd is in the Gunbarrel area, but its official page prints Boulder 80301, and the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places the address inside Boulder city limits.
