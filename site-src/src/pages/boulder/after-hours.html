What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Mountain Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official store page or official USPS location page. Store hours are not money desk hours, which King Soopers does not publish; call ahead. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Does this page show locations that are open now?

No. This page does not have live open or closed status. Labels come from hours published on official store and USPS pages, last verified Nov 12, 2026. Check the published hours in the table and confirm with the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in Boulder at night?

No location on this Boulder list names check cashing on its official page yet, so none is listed for it at night. See the Boulder hub for every checked location.

When is the King Soopers Money Services desk open in Boulder?

The Boulder King Soopers stores on this list publish store hours until 11 p.m. every day, not separate Money Services desk hours. Call the store before you go.

Can I buy a money order in Boulder on a weekend or at night?

Boulder money-order locations on this list publish weekend hours, and publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. The three listed King Soopers stores publish Saturday and Sunday store hours until 11 p.m. (money-desk hours are not published separately from store hours, so call ahead) [2]. All three listed Post Offices publish Saturday retail hours, stay open past 5 p.m. on weekdays, and are closed Sunday [1]. See money orders in Boulder.
