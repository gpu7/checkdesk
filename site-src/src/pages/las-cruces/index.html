Las Cruces check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Las Cruces Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Las Cruces, New Mexico, and are inside Las Cruces city limits (the Census place Las Cruces city, GEOID 3539380, in Doña Ana County): Commerce at 1180 Commerce Dr and Las Cruces at 201 E Las Cruces Ave. This is Las Cruces, New Mexico, in Doña Ana County, not Mesilla, not Mesilla Park, not University Park, not Sunland Park, and not Anthony. Albuquerque, Rio Rancho, Santa Fe, and Roswell stay on separate lists. None is labeled Open evenings. Two publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Commerce, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; Las Cruces, 8:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. None publishes Sunday hours. One office that prints Las Cruces is held because the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point fully inside Las Cruces city: University Park at 1600 International Mall, in the University Park CDP. Mesilla Park at 310 E Union Ave is held because its official page prints Mesilla Park, New Mexico, not Las Cruces. The official USPS map point for Mesilla Park is inside Las Cruces city on both Census files, but the printed city does not already match, so it is not listed. One listed office (Commerce) uses a documented map-point fallback: the official page already prints Las Cruces, New Mexico, and the official USPS map point is inside Las Cruces city on both Census files, but the address did not fully match in the city on both files after retries (raw: /workspace/step8nm/retry_nomatch.jsonl). No Las Cruces location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Las Cruces pages are Mountain Time.

What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-17. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). City limits are the Census place Las Cruces city, GEOID 3539380, in Doña Ana County, checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files on 2026-11-17. The listed offices are in Doña Ana County (GEOID 35013). OpenStreetMap was not used. Las Cruces is in the mountain zone under 49 CFR 71.8. The Central–Mountain boundary in 49 CFR 71.7 follows the Texas–New Mexico line and does not put Las Cruces in Central Time. The Mountain–Pacific boundary in 49 CFR 71.9 does not enter New Mexico.
