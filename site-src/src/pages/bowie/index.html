Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Bowie Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Bowie, Maryland, and are inside Bowie city limits (the Census place Bowie city, GEOID 2408775, in Prince George's County): Bowie at 6710 Laurel Bowie Rd, Mitchellville at 1500 Pointer Ridge Pl, and West Bowie at 13030 9th St. This is Bowie, Maryland, in Prince George's County, not Bowie, Texas, and not Laurel, Greenbelt, College Park, or Hyattsville.
