Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Bowie Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Bowie, Maryland, and are inside Bowie city limits (the Census place Bowie city, GEOID 2408775, in Prince George's County): Bowie at 6710 Laurel Bowie Rd, Mitchellville at 1500 Pointer Ridge Pl, and West Bowie at 13030 9th St. This is Bowie, Maryland, in Prince George's County, not Bowie, Texas, and not Laurel, Greenbelt, College Park, or Hyattsville. The Mitchellville Post Office prints Bowie and is inside Bowie city limits; it is not a listing for Mitchellville CDP. None is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. or earlier. Two publish Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends: Bowie and Mitchellville. West Bowie does not publish weekend hours. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All three match the current and 2020 Census address files in Bowie city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Mitchellville Carrier Annex at 6710 Laurel Bowie Rd, the same address as the listed Bowie Post Office, prints Bowie and geocodes in the city, but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not listed. No other office in the Maryland locator sweep geocodes into Bowie city. No Bowie location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Bowie pages are Eastern Time.
