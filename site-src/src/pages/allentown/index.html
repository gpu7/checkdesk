The one checked Allentown location does not publish 24-hour hours, so it is not listed as a 24-hour desk. It does not publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. and Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). Can I cash a check in Allentown?

No location on this Allentown list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Allentown Post Office page here lists money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
