How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City and borough limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Juneau city and borough limits (the consolidated city-borough, checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and either the official printed city is Juneau, or both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside the city and borough and money-order tags are present. The displayed city is the official printed USPS city. Some addresses inside the city and borough use a different mailing city, such as Douglas; the Douglas Post Office is listed under that rule.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Juneau Post Offices sell money orders?

All four Juneau Post Offices on this page have official USPS location pages that list money orders: Auke Bay (11899 Glacier Hwy), Douglas (804 3rd St, which prints Douglas), Federal Station (709 W 9th St), and Mendenhall (9491 Vintage Blvd). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Which Juneau Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Three publish weekday retail hours past 5 p.m. (Alaska Time): Mendenhall closes at 6 p.m., and Auke Bay and Douglas close at 5:30 p.m. Federal Station closes at 5 p.m. on weekdays, so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Which Juneau Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Three publish Saturday retail hours: Mendenhall (11 a.m. to 4 p.m.), Auke Bay (12 p.m. to 3 p.m.), and Douglas (1 p.m. to 3 p.m.). Federal Station is closed on Saturday. None publishes Sunday hours.

Why is the Douglas Post Office listed if it prints Douglas?

Its official page prints Douglas, not Juneau. Both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Juneau city and borough (GEOID 0236400), official map points also fall there, and the official page lists money orders, so it is listed under the approved neighborhood-name rule. The displayed city stays the official printed USPS city. Douglas is a community inside the city and borough, not a separate incorporated place.

Is the Auke Bay Post Office in Juneau?

Yes. The official USPS page prints a Juneau address (11899 Glacier Hwy, Juneau, AK 99801), and the Census geocoder places that address and the official map point inside Juneau city and borough.

Where can I cash a check in Juneau?

No Juneau location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Juneau have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Juneau?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
