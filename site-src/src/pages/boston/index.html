Boston check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

This page lists locations inside Boston city limits, each checked on an official USPS location page. So far that is eleven Post Offices. It shows which services each page lists, the published hours, and what the brand publishes about fees.

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Eleven Boston Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Boston, Massachusetts, and are inside Boston city limits (the Census place Boston city, GEOID 2507000, in Suffolk County): the Astor, Back Bay, Cathedral, Charles Street, Fort Point, Hanover Street, John Fitzgerald Kennedy, Kenmore, Lafayette, Milk Street, and Prudential Center Post Offices. This is Boston, Massachusetts, in Suffolk County, not a Boston in another state, and not Cambridge, Brookline, Somerville, Revere, Chelsea, Quincy, or Newton. Cambridge stays on a separate list. Ten are labeled Open evenings: Astor, until 6:00 p.m., Back Bay, until 5:30 p.m., Charles Street, until 5:30 p.m., Fort Point, until 11:59 p.m., Hanover Street, until 6:00 p.m., John Fitzgerald Kennedy, until 5:30 p.m., Kenmore, until 5:30 p.m., Lafayette, until 6:00 p.m., Milk Street, until 6:00 p.m., and Prudential Center, until 5:30 p.m. Cathedral weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m., which does not count as Open evenings. Nine publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Astor, Hanover Street, and Prudential Center, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; Back Bay, 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.; Cathedral, 8:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.; Charles Street, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Fort Point, 6:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m.; Kenmore, 8:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.; and Lafayette, 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Sunday hours: Fort Point, 6:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. The two listed offices with no weekend retail hours are John Fitzgerald Kennedy and Milk Street. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All eleven match the current and 2020 Census address files in Boston city, and both official map points fall there. No map-point fallback was used. Three other offices print Boston and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: the Fenway Carrier Annex at 1313 Boylston St, which has no published retail hours; Soldiers Field at 117 Western Ave Ste 1; and State House at 24 Beacon St Rm 2. Hours on the Boston pages are Eastern Time.

What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-16. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. City limits are the Census place Boston city, GEOID 2507000, in Suffolk County, checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files on 2026-11-17. The listed offices are in Suffolk County (GEOID 25025). OpenStreetMap was not used. Massachusetts observes Eastern Time (49 CFR 71.4). Neighborhood offices that print a name such as Dorchester or Roxbury, rather than Boston, are held even when the geocoder places them inside Boston city.
