How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • Town limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the Town of Windsor limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Windsor Post Office sells money orders?

The Windsor Post Office (215 6th St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Windsor Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Windsor Post Office (215 6th St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to noon. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Windsor Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Windsor Post Office (215 6th St) publishes weekday retail hours from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Does King Soopers in Windsor sell money orders?

Yes. The official page for King Soopers Windsor (1520 Main St) tags money orders and a Money Services desk. King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The store publishes hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Windsor?

No Windsor store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and the King Soopers Windsor page shows a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Is there a Walmart in Windsor?

Not on Walmart's official store directory, which lists no stores in Windsor. The nearest Walmart Supercenters print Timnath, Greeley, Fort Collins, or Loveland addresses, and the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder places each of them outside the Town of Windsor.

Why are Severance and Timnath locations not listed?

Severance, Timnath, Eaton, Johnstown, and Milliken are separate towns, and Greeley, Fort Collins, and Loveland are separate cities. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside the Town of Windsor, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside the Town of Windsor limits.
