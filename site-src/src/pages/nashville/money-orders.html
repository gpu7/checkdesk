For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All twenty-two listed Nashville Post Offices sell money orders. Six are labeled Open evenings: Bellevue, until 6:00 p.m., Brentwood, until 6:30 p.m., Church Street, until 5:30 p.m., Green Hills, until 6:00 p.m., Nashville Main Office Window, until 6:00 p.m., and Woodbine, until 6:00 p.m. Twenty publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Acklen, Antioch, Donelson, East Nashville, Jere Baxter, Madison, Metro, Northeast, Old Hickory, South Nashville, and Woodbine, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Bellevue and West, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; Brentwood, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.; Glenview, 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Green Hills, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.; Hermitage, 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; Joelton, 8:30 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.; Nashville Main Office Window, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; and Whites Creek, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. Sunday hours: Nashville Main Office Window, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The two listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Broadway Retail and Church Street. Joelton weekday retail closes at 4:00 p.m. Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday and at 12:00 p.m. Wednesday, which does not count as Open evenings.
