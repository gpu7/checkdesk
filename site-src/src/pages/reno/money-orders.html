For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All six listed Reno Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Reno, until 5:30 p.m. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Peavine and Reno, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.; Sierra, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours.
