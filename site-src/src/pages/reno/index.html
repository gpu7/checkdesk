Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Six Reno Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Reno, Nevada, and are inside Reno city limits (the Census place Reno city, GEOID 3260600, in Washoe County): Downtown Reno at 135 N Sierra St Ste A1, Peavine at 1580 Grand Point Way, Reno at 2000 Vassar St, Sierra at 1050 N Hills Blvd, University Reno at 1674 N Virginia St, and Washington at 1490 Stardust St. This is Reno, Nevada, in Washoe County, not Sparks, Carson City, or an unincorporated place in the Balance of Washoe County.
