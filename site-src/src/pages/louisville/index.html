How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Louisville city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Louisville line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Where can I buy a money order in Louisville?

Both King Soopers stores listed here tag money orders on their official store pages: King Soopers Louisville (1375 E South Boulder Rd) and King Soopers Louisville Marketplace (1171 W Dillon Rd). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead. See money orders in Louisville.

Where can I cash a check in Louisville?

King Soopers Louisville Marketplace (1171 W Dillon Rd) is the only Louisville store page we checked that names check cashing. King Soopers does not publish a fee. See check cashing in Louisville.

Are the Louisville King Soopers stores open after 5 p.m. or on weekends?

Yes. Both stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, so they are labeled Open evenings and Open weekends. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Why is the Louisville Post Office not listed?

The official USPS record for the office named Louisville (566 S McCaslin Blvd) prints Superior, CO 80027. The U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (current and 2020 files) and the official USPS map point place that address inside Louisville city limits. This page lists a location only when the official printed city and the geocoder or map point agree it is inside Louisville city limits, so we are holding that office off the page. Its official page lists money orders and publishes weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m. and Saturday hours from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m.

Is there a Walmart in Louisville?

Not on this list. No Walmart store prints a Louisville address. The nearest Supercenter we checked (500 Summit Blvd) prints Broomfield and geocodes in Broomfield city.

Why are Lafayette, Broomfield, and Erie locations not listed?

Lafayette, Broomfield, and Erie are separate cities and towns. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside Louisville city limits, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside Louisville city limits.
