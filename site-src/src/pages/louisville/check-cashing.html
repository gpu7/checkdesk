What to bring

  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [1].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. This city's list does not include a Post Office; the office named Louisville prints Superior and is held off the Louisville pages.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Louisville city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder).
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. King Soopers publishes store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I cash a check at night in Louisville?

King Soopers Louisville Marketplace publishes store hours until 11 p.m. every day. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [1].

Does the King Soopers on E South Boulder Rd cash checks?

Its official store page does not say. King Soopers Louisville (1375 E South Boulder Rd) tags a Money Services desk and money orders but does not name check cashing, so it is not on this list. Call the store before you go.

Does King Soopers cash checks?

Yes, at Money Services desks in general: King Soopers' check-cashing page says it cashes payroll, government, tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person, up to $5,000, with no bank account required [1]. On this Louisville list, one location lists check cashing on its official store page: King Soopers Louisville Marketplace. One other Louisville King Soopers page does not name check cashing, so call ahead. King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee [1].

Can I cash a personal check at these Louisville locations?

One Louisville location on this list tags check cashing on its official store page: King Soopers Louisville Marketplace. King Soopers' official check-cashing page lists payroll, government, income tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person (not checks payable to a business), and says fees and limits vary by state; it does not state a personal-check fee or cap on that page, so call the desk ahead [1].

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Louisville?

No location on this Louisville list publishes 24-hour hours. The one listed location (King Soopers Louisville Marketplace) publishes store hours past 5 p.m. every day, closing at 11 p.m. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Does this page show check-cashing places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not track live open or closed status. Published store hours were last verified Nov 12, 2026, and money-desk hours are not published separately from store hours. Confirm with the location before you go.

When is the King Soopers Money Services desk open?

King Soopers does not publish separate Money Services desk hours on its Louisville store pages. King Soopers Louisville Marketplace publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, and the chain's check-cashing page notes only that hours vary by location [1], so call ahead.
