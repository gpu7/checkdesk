Where to cash a check in Louisville

Louisville location lists check cashing on its official store page so far: King Soopers Louisville Marketplace (1171 W Dillon Rd). It publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

What to bring

  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [1].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. This city's list does not include a Post Office; the office named Louisville prints Superior and is held off the Louisville pages.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Louisville city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder).
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. King Soopers publishes store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I cash a check at night in Louisville?

King Soopers Louisville Marketplace publishes store hours until 11 p.m. every day. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [1].

Does the King Soopers on E South Boulder Rd cash checks?

Its official store page does not say. King Soopers Louisville (1375 E South Boulder Rd) tags a Money Services desk and money orders but does not name check cashing, so it is not on this list. Call the store before you go.
