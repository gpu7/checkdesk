Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Racine Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Racine, Wisconsin, and are inside Racine city limits (the Census place Racine city, GEOID 5566000, in Racine County): Racine at 603 Main St and West Racine at 1300 Perry Ave. This is Racine, Wisconsin, at /racine/. This is not Caledonia, and not a Racine in another state. A Racine County geocode is not enough to list an office here. The listed offices are in Racine County (GEOID 55101). Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole-place GEOID 5566000. Caledonia village and other Racine County places stay off this list.
