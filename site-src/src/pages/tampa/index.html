How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Tampa city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Tampa. Tampa is an incorporated city in Hillsborough County. This page covers Tampa, Florida, not a Tampa in another state. Temple Terrace is a separate municipality. Many nearby addresses use a Tampa mailing ZIP but sit in a census-designated place or another city, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. A 5 p.m. close does not count. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Tampa Post Offices sell money orders?

All eleven Tampa locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Commerce, Hilldale, Interbay, MacDill AFB, New Tampa, Peninsula Tampa, Produce, Sulphur Springs, Tampa, West Tampa, and Ybor City. USPS publishes the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Seven offices that also list money orders, or that print Tampa, sit outside the city in census-designated places and are held for review. Tampa Carrier Annex does not list money orders, so it is not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders print a different city, such as Temple Terrace or Plant City, and are outside Tampa city limits.

Which Tampa locations are open evenings or weekends?

Six of the eleven listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Commerce, New Tampa, and West Tampa (until 5:30 p.m.), Hilldale and Peninsula Tampa (until 6:00 p.m.), and the Tampa Post Office (until 10:00 p.m.). Nine publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. The Tampa Post Office also publishes Sunday hours, 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. MacDill AFB is not labeled Open evenings or Open weekends. Commerce is evenings only.

Is Temple Terrace included?

No. Temple Terrace is a separate incorporated city in Hillsborough County. Its Post Office prints Temple Terrace, not Tampa, and it is not listed under Tampa. Offices that print Tampa but sit in nearby census-designated places are held out of this list.
