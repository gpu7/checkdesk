What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Pacific Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Pacific Time. Of the 78 listed Los Angeles Post Offices, 23 publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings, and 57 publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5 p.m. weekday close does not count as Open evenings. 19 listed offices have no evening and no weekend retail hours, so they are not on this page.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Los Angeles city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Los Angeles is an incorporated city inside Los Angeles County, not a consolidated city-county. Offices whose official page prints a neighborhood or community name are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Los Angeles city and the official page lists money orders; the displayed address keeps the official printed USPS city. Nearby addresses that sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Does this page show locations that are open now?

No. This page does not have live open or closed status. Labels come from hours published on official USPS location pages, last verified Nov 19, 2026. Check the published hours in the table and confirm with the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in Los Angeles at night?

No location on this Los Angeles list names check cashing on its official page yet, so none is listed for it at night. The Post Offices here list money orders, not general check cashing. See the Los Angeles hub for every checked location.

Can I buy a money order in Los Angeles on a weekend or at night?

Los Angeles money-order locations on this list publish weekend hours, and publish weekday hours past 5 p.m. 57 of the 78 listed Post Offices publish Saturday retail hours, 23 stay open past 5 p.m. on weekdays, and all 78 are closed Sunday [1]. See money orders in Los Angeles.
