How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Los Angeles city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Los Angeles. Los Angeles is an incorporated city inside Los Angeles County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Los Angeles mailing city, print a neighborhood postal city, or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.

FAQ

Which Los Angeles Post Offices sell money orders?

All 43 Los Angeles locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Several neighborhood-printed offices also list money orders, but they are held for review because they do not print Los Angeles. Ten Los Angeles-printed records inside the city do not list money orders, so they are not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Los Angeles city limits or print a different city.

Which Los Angeles Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

13 listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Main Office Los Angeles, Oakwood, Dockweiler, Del Valle, Macys Retail Store, West Los Angeles Finance, Edendale, Los Feliz, Hollywood, Palms, Westchester, Foy, and Mar Vista. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Los Angeles Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

31 of the 43 listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends. None of the 43 publishes Sunday hours. The 12 listed offices with no weekend retail hours are Augustus F Hawkins, Federal Los Angeles, Arcade, Del Valle, Macys Retail Store, Market, Miracle Mile, Ramirez, Highland Park, Westvern, Arco, and Bunker Hill.

Why are Van Nuys, San Pedro, and other neighborhood Post Offices not listed?

Their official pages print a neighborhood or community name, not Los Angeles. An office whose printed city is not Los Angeles is held for review and not listed, even when the Census address match and the official USPS map point fall inside Los Angeles city. The map-point fallback does not apply when the printed city already disagrees. The Hollywood Post Office at 1615 Wilcox Ave is listed because it prints Los Angeles.

Why are the East Los Angeles, Hazard, Wagner Branch, Airport Los Angeles, and Barrington offices not listed?

All five print Los Angeles, but the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point fully inside Los Angeles city. Hazard and East Los Angeles geocode in East Los Angeles CDP. Wagner Branch geocodes in Westmont CDP with an official point in Inglewood city. Airport Los Angeles has no address match after retries and an official point in Inglewood city. Barrington's address is in Los Angeles city, but the official map point is not in any incorporated place.
