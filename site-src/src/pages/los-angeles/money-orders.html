Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. 78 Los Angeles Post Offices below list money orders on their official pages and are inside Los Angeles city limits. 43 print Los Angeles, CA; 35 print a neighborhood or community name and were added Nov 19, 2026 under the approved rule. 23 publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. 57 publish Saturday hours. None publishes Sunday hours. Walmart Supercenters in Los Angeles have not been checked yet.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Los Angeles city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Los Angeles is an incorporated city inside Los Angeles County, not a consolidated city-county. Offices whose official page prints a neighborhood or community name are listed when both the current and 2020 Census address files place the address inside Los Angeles city and the official page lists money orders; the displayed address keeps the official printed USPS city. Nearby addresses that sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Pacific Time.
