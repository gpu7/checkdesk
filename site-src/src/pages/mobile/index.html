How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Mobile city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some nearby addresses use a different mailing city, even when they are close to the Mobile line.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Mobile Post Offices sell money orders?

Twelve Mobile Post Offices have official USPS location pages that list money orders: the Mobile Post Office (250 Saint Joseph St), Midtown (907 Spring Hill Ave), Loop (415 Dauphin Island Pkwy), Crichton (168 Bay Shore Ave), Bel Air (3410 Bel Air Mall), Bayside (2000 Rosedale Rd), Cottage Hill (705 Oak Circle Dr W), Spring Hill (5551 Old Shell Rd), Towne West (5701M Moffett Rd), Plaza de Malaga (6157 Airport Blvd Ste 310), Tillmans Corner (5441 Highway 90 W Ste 13), and Airport (8400 Airport Blvd). USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Which Mobile Post Office is open after 5 p.m.?

The Bel Air Post Office (3410 Bel Air Mall) publishes weekday retail hours from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., so it is labeled Open evenings. The other eleven close at 5 p.m. or earlier on weekdays.

Which Mobile Post Offices are open on Saturday?

Seven publish Saturday retail hours: the Mobile Post Office (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Bel Air (12 p.m. to 5 p.m.), Cottage Hill (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Spring Hill (9:30 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Towne West (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.), Tillmans Corner (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.), and Airport (9 a.m. to 12 p.m.). Midtown, Loop, Crichton, Bayside, and Plaza de Malaga are closed on Saturday. None publishes Sunday hours.

Do the Mobile Walmart stores sell money orders?

Their official store pages do not say. The three listed Mobile Supercenters show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name money orders, so we list them on this hub without services. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Mobile?

No Mobile location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. The Mobile Walmart Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label; call the store before you go.

Why is the Prichard Post Office not listed?

Its official page prints a Mobile mailing address, but the Census geocoder places both the address and the official USPS map point inside the City of Prichard, a separate city. It is held for review and not listed under Mobile.

Why are Saraland, Semmes, and Prichard locations not listed?

Saraland, Semmes, and Prichard are separate cities, so stores and Post Offices inside them are not listed here, even when they are a short drive away. This page lists only locations inside Mobile city limits.
