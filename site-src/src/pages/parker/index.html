How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Parker Post Office sells money orders?

The Parker Post Office (18695 Pony Express Dr) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Parker Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Parker Post Office (18695 Pony Express Dr) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to noon. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Parker Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

Yes. The Parker Post Office (18695 Pony Express Dr) publishes weekday retail hours from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Parker sell money orders?

Three King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders: Stroh Ranch (12959 S Parker Rd), Crossing at Stonegate (17031 Lincoln Ave), and Cottonwood Drive (17761 Cottonwood Dr). King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead. The King Soopers Parker store at 6755 Prospector Ln lists no money services on its official page.

Where can I cash a check in Parker?

No Parker store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and the three Parker King Soopers pages listed here show a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Parker?

The official page for the Parker Walmart Supercenter (11101 S Parker Rd) shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name check cashing, so we do not list it for that. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.

Why are Castle Rock and Highlands Ranch locations not listed?

Castle Rock, Castle Pines, Lone Tree, Centennial, and Aurora are separate cities and towns, and Highlands Ranch, The Pinery, and Stonegate are unincorporated communities. Their stores and Post Offices sit outside the Town of Parker, even when they are a short drive away or use a Parker mailing address. This page lists only locations inside the Town of Parker limits.
