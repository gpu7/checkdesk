Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Newark Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Newark, New Jersey, and are inside Newark city limits (the Census place Newark city, GEOID 3451000, in Essex County): Ironbound at 279 Ferry St Ste 4, Midtown at 40 Clinton St Ste L, North at 243 Broadway, Roseville at 374 7th Ave W, South at 514 Frelinghuysen Ave, Springfield Ave at 290 Springfield Ave, and Vailsburg at 210 Stuyvesant Ave. This is Newark, New Jersey, in Essex County, not East Orange, not a borough or township in the Balance of Essex County, and not Newark, Delaware.
