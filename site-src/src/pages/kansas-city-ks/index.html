Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Five Kansas City, Kansas Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Kansas City, Kansas, and geocode inside Kansas City city limits (the Census place Kansas City city, GEOID 2036000, in Wyandotte County): Argentine at 3400 Strong Ave, Civic Center at 550 Nebraska Ave, Robert L Roberts at 3115 N 51st St, Rosedale at 2200 W 36th Ave, and Wyandotte West at 1310 N 78th Ter. This is Kansas City, Kansas, in Wyandotte County, not Kansas City, Missouri, and not Bonner Springs, Edwardsville, or Lake Quivira. Offices that print Kansas City, Missouri, are not on this list. None is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. or earlier. Four publish Saturday hours, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends: Civic Center, Robert L Roberts, Rosedale, and Wyandotte West. None publishes Sunday hours. Argentine is closed on Saturday and Sunday and closes at 12:00 p.m. on weekdays, so it is not on the evenings-and-weekends list. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. All five are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Kansas City city, Kansas. No map-point fallback was used. Two other offices print Kansas City, Kansas, and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: the Kansas City Post Office at 550 Nebraska Ave (the same printed street address as the listed Civic Center office, with no BUSINESS hours on its official page), and the Muncie Post Office at 6540 Kaw Dr. No other office in the Kansas locator sweep geocodes into Kansas City, Kansas, including the 23 offices that print Kansas City, Missouri. No Kansas City, Kansas location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Kansas City, Kansas pages are Central Time.
