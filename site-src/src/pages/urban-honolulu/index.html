Urban Honolulu check cashing, money orders, and bill pay

This page lists locations inside the Urban Honolulu Census community (CDP) in Honolulu County, each checked on an official USPS location page. So far that is ten Post Offices. It shows which services each page lists, the published hours, and what the brand publishes about fees. Urban Honolulu is a census-designated place, not an incorporated city; Hawaii has no incorporated places recognized by the Census Bureau.

Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Ten Urban Honolulu Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Honolulu, and geocode inside the Urban Honolulu CDP: Ala Moana, Downtown Honolulu, Honolulu, Kaimuki, Kapalama, Makiki, Moiliili, Uptown, Waialae Kahala, and Waikiki. This is Urban Honolulu, Hawaii, a Census community in Honolulu County, not a Honolulu in another state, and not East Honolulu, Pearl City, or Waipahu. One is labeled Open evenings: the Honolulu Post Office, until 8:00 p.m. Six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Ala Moana, Honolulu, Kapalama, Makiki, Waialae Kahala, and Waikiki. None of the listed offices publishes Sunday hours. Downtown Honolulu, Kaimuki, Moiliili, and Uptown close at or before 5:00 p.m. on weekdays and are closed on weekends, so they are not on the evenings-and-weekends list. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Ala Moana is listed on a documented mixed-vintage fallback: the current Census address file matched in Urban Honolulu CDP, the 2020 file did not match after street and ZIP retries, and both official map points fall inside the CDP. Makiki is listed on a documented map-point fallback: neither address file matched 1111 Lunalilo St after street, city, and ZIP retries (no-ZIP hits snapped to a different street, Lunalilo Home Road, in East Honolulu and were discarded), and both official map points fall inside Urban Honolulu CDP. Waialae Kahala geocodes in Urban Honolulu, not East Honolulu. Aina Haina and Hawaii Kai print Honolulu but geocode in the East Honolulu CDP, so they are held out of this list. Fort Shafter and Tripler Army Medical Center sit in Urban Honolulu CDP but print Fort Shafter and Tripler Army Medical Center, so they are held. Sand Island Carrier Annex prints Honolulu and geocodes in the CDP, but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not listed. No Urban Honolulu location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Urban Honolulu pages are Hawaii Time (HST).

FAQ

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Urban Honolulu?

None of the checked Urban Honolulu locations publishes 24-hour hours, so none is listed as a 24-hour desk. location publishes weekday hours past 5 p.m. (Open evenings) and publish Saturday or Sunday hours (Open weekends). No Urban Honolulu location on this list names check cashing on its official page yet. See evenings and weekends in Urban Honolulu.

Does this list show places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not have live open or closed status. The Open evenings and Open weekends labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page, last verified Nov 16, 2026. Confirm hours with the official source or the location before you go.

Can I cash a check in Urban Honolulu?

No location on this Urban Honolulu list names check cashing on its official page yet. The Urban Honolulu Post Office pages here list money orders, not general check cashing. USPS says you can cash a USPS money order at a Post Office for free [1].
