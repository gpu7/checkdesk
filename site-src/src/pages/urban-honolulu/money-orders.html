Money orders in Urban Honolulu

These Urban Honolulu locations list money orders on their official USPS location pages. Hours are local time in Urban Honolulu (Hawaii Time, HST). Urban Honolulu is a Census community (CDP) in Honolulu County, not an incorporated city.

For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All ten listed Urban Honolulu Post Offices sell money orders: Ala Moana, Downtown Honolulu, Honolulu, Kaimuki, Kapalama, Makiki, Moiliili, Uptown, Waialae Kahala, and Waikiki. One is labeled Open evenings: the Honolulu Post Office, until 8:00 p.m. Six publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Ala Moana, Honolulu, Kapalama, Makiki, Waialae Kahala, and Waikiki. None of the listed offices publishes Sunday hours.
