Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Spokane Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Spokane, Washington, and are inside Spokane city limits (the Census place Spokane city, GEOID 5367000, in Spokane County (GEOID 53063)): Hays Park at 3910 N Crestline St, Hillyard at 4401 N Freya St, Liberty Park at 1602 E Sprague Ave, Manito at 3120 S Grand Blvd, Riverside at 904 W Riverside Ave, Shadle Garland at 1903 W Garland Ave, and Sunset Hill at 6801 W Flightline Blvd. This is Spokane, Washington, at /spokane/. This is not Spokane Valley, Washington, and not a Spokane in another state. Offices in Town and Country CDP, in the Spokane County unincorporated balance, and in Idaho stay off this list. The listed offices are in Spokane County (GEOID 53063). Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole-place GEOID 5367000. One listed office (Sunset Hill) uses a documented map-point fallback: the official page already prints Spokane, Washington, and the official USPS map point is inside Spokane city on both Census files, but the current and 2020 address files did not match the official address after retries (raw: /workspace/step8wa/retry_nomatch.jsonl). Three are labeled Open evenings: Hays Park, until 5:30 p.m., Liberty Park, until 5:30 p.m., and Manito, until 5:30 p.m. Three publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Hays Park and Manito, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.; Shadle Garland, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Three other offices are not listed. Two print Spokane, Washington, but the Census geocoder does not place both the address and the official map point inside Spokane city: Rosewood (all four legs are in Town and Country CDP) and Regal (all four legs are in the Spokane County unincorporated balance, with no incorporated place or CDP). One prints Spokane and geocodes in Spokane city but does not list money orders: Northpointe. Offices in Idaho stay off this list. No Spokane location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Spokane pages are Pacific Time.
