For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All eleven listed Kansas City, Missouri, Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Kansas City MO Retail, until 6:00 p.m. on weekdays. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Nine publish Saturday hours and are labeled Open weekends: Antioch, Hickman Mills, James Crews, Longview, South Troost, and Waldo, 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.; Kansas City MO Retail, 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.; Martin City, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.; and Parkway, 8:30 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. None publishes Sunday hours. Country Club and Executive Park do not publish Saturday or Sunday hours.
