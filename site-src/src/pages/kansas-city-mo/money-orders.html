Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. All eleven listed Kansas City, Missouri, Post Offices sell money orders. One is labeled Open evenings: Kansas City MO Retail, until 6:00 p.m. on weekdays.
