Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Three Independence, Missouri, Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Independence, Missouri, and are inside Independence city limits (the Census place Independence city, GEOID 2935000, in Jackson County): Englewood at 1500 S Northern Blvd, Harry S Truman at 14200 E 32nd St S, and Independence at 301 W Lexington Ave Ste 110. This is Independence, Missouri, in Jackson County, not Independence township in Dunklin or Nodaway County, and not Kansas City, Lee's Summit, Blue Springs, or the other Jackson County municipalities.
