How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Is the Lakewood Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The official USPS page lists Saturday retail hours of 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. It is closed Sunday.

Do King Soopers stores in Lakewood sell money orders?

Most do. The official store pages listed here tag money orders, except King Soopers Belmar, which tags only a Money Services desk. King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Lakewood?

The official Lakewood Walmart Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so we do not list them for it. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.
