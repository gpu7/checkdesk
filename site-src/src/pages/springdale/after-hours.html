What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Open eveningsThe published hours are open after 5 p.m. (Central Time) on at least one weekday, Monday to Friday.
Open weekendsThe published hours include Saturday or Sunday.

Both labels come only from hours published on the official USPS location page. If a page publishes no hours, the location gets no label. Hours on this page are Central Time. Of the two listed Springdale Post Offices, only the Springdale Post Office on Springdale Avenue publishes Saturday hours and is labeled Open weekends. Neither office publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so neither is labeled Open evenings. Springdale Station closes at 4:30 p.m. on weekdays and has no weekend retail hours, so it is not on this page.

Locations

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Springdale city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Springdale. Springdale is one incorporated Census place spanning Washington and Benton counties, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Springdale mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Central Time.
