How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Wasilla city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Wasilla. Wasilla is an incorporated city inside Matanuska-Susitna Borough. Many nearby addresses use a Wasilla mailing city but sit in a census-designated place, another city, or unincorporated borough land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Alaska Time.

FAQ

Which Wasilla Post Offices sell money orders?

The Wasilla Post Office at 401 N Main St is the only Wasilla location on this page, and its official USPS location page lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Wasilla city limits or print a different city.

Which Wasilla Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

The Wasilla Post Office publishes weekday retail hours until 5:30 p.m. (Alaska Time), so it is labeled Open evenings.

Which Wasilla Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

The Wasilla Post Office is closed on Saturday and Sunday, so it is not labeled Open weekends. No Wasilla city location on this page publishes weekend retail hours.

Why is the Wasilla Annex not listed?

Its official page prints Wasilla, but the Census geocoder places the official map point in the South Lakes census-designated place, outside Wasilla city limits. An office printed Wasilla whose address or map point falls outside the city is held for review and not listed. That official page also lists no retail hours and no money orders.

Is the Houston Post Office in Wasilla?

No. Its official page prints Houston, and the Census geocoder places the official map point in Houston city, a separate incorporated city in Matanuska-Susitna Borough. It is not listed under Wasilla.

Is the Palmer Post Office in Wasilla?

No. Its official page prints Palmer, and the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in Palmer city, a separate incorporated city in Matanuska-Susitna Borough. It is not listed under Wasilla.

Where can I cash a check in Wasilla?

No Wasilla location we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. Walmart Supercenters in Wasilla have not been checked yet.

Are there Walmart money-order locations in Wasilla?

Not on this page yet. The official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when these listings were collected, so no Walmart rows are included. They may be added later from official store pages.
