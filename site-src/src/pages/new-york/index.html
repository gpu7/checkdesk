What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-17. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. City limits are the Census place New York city, GEOID 3651000, which spans five county parts coextensive with the borough counties (Bronx, Kings, New York, Queens, and Richmond), checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files on 2026-11-17. The fifty-one listed offices are in New York County (GEOID 36061). Only offices whose official page prints New York are listed; offices that print a borough or neighborhood name are held even when they geocode inside New York city. OpenStreetMap was not used. New York is in the Eastern zone under 49 CFR 71.4. The Eastern–Central boundary in 49 CFR 71.5 does not enter New York.
