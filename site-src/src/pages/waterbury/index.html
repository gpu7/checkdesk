How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Waterbury city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Waterbury. Waterbury is an incorporated city, coextensive with Waterbury town, in the Naugatuck Valley Planning Region. Many nearby addresses sit in another municipality, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Eastern Time.

FAQ

Which Waterbury Post Offices sell money orders?

The four Waterbury locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages: Waterbury, East End, Lakewood, and Plaza. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. A second USPS locator record at 135 Grand St prints Waterbury and sits in the city, but it does not list money orders, so it is not listed here. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Waterbury city limits or print a different city, including offices in Prospect, Wolcott, Naugatuck, Watertown, and Middlebury.

Which Waterbury Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

One of the four listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m. and is labeled Open evenings: the Waterbury Post Office at 135 Grand St, until 6:00 p.m. A 5 p.m. close does not count. East End, Lakewood, and Plaza close at 5 p.m. on weekdays.

Which Waterbury Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

All four listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Waterbury (8:00 a.m. to noon), East End and Plaza (8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.), and Lakewood (9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.). None of the four publishes Sunday hours.

Why are East End, Lakewood, and Plaza listed?

Each official page prints Waterbury, not those station names, as the city. The Census Geocoder matches the address, and the official USPS map point, inside Waterbury city on both the current and 2020 files. An office whose printed city is Waterbury and whose address is inside the city is listed, even when the official name is a neighborhood or station name.

Why is there a second Waterbury record at 135 Grand St that is not listed?

The USPS locator has two records at 135 Grand St that both print Waterbury. The Waterbury Post Office record lists money orders and publishes retail hours, so it is listed. The second record does not list money orders and does not publish a BUSINESS retail-hours block, so it is not listed. The Census geocoder places 135 Grand St inside Waterbury city on both files.

Are Prospect, Wolcott, Naugatuck, Watertown, or Middlebury included?

No. Those are separate municipalities. Their Post Offices print those cities and sit outside Waterbury city, so they are not listed under Waterbury.
