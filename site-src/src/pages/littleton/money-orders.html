Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. King Soopers stores in Littleton that tag money orders on their official store page are listed too, but King Soopers says the cost "will vary by state" and does not publish a fee [3], so call ahead. The official page for King Soopers Littleton and Broadway (100 W Littleton Blvd) shows only a general "Money Services" label and does not name check cashing, money orders, or bill pay, so we list that store on the Littleton hub without services. We do not assume a store offers a service its own page does not name, so call the store. Walmart publishes a chain-wide $1 maximum money-order fee [2], but both Walmart Supercenters with a Littleton mailing address are outside Littleton city limits, so neither is listed here.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
