How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses use a different mailing city.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Littleton Post Office sells money orders?

The Littleton Post Office (5753 S Prince St) has an official USPS location page that lists money orders. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office.

Is the Littleton Post Office open on Saturday?

Yes. The Littleton Post Office (5753 S Prince St) publishes Saturday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. It does not publish Sunday hours.

Is the Littleton Post Office open after 5 p.m.?

No. The Littleton Post Office (5753 S Prince St) publishes weekday retail hours from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it is not labeled Open evenings.

Do King Soopers stores in Littleton sell money orders?

One King Soopers store page listed here tags money orders: Mineral and Broadway (7901 S Broadway). King Soopers Littleton and Broadway (100 W Littleton Blvd) tags only a Money Services desk, so it is on this list without services. King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. Both stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Littleton?

No Littleton store page we checked names check cashing, so we do not list any yet. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks, and both Littleton King Soopers pages listed here show a Money Services desk; call the store before you go.

Is there a Walmart in Littleton on this list?

No. The two Walmart Supercenters with a Littleton mailing address (7700 W Quincy Ave and 13420 W Coal Mine Ave) are outside Littleton city limits, according to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder: one is in Denver and the other is in Dakota Ridge, an unincorporated community. This page lists only locations inside the City of Littleton.

Why are Columbine and Ken Caryl locations not listed?

Columbine, Ken Caryl, Dakota Ridge, and Highlands Ranch are unincorporated communities, and Englewood, Denver, Sheridan, Lakewood, and Centennial are separate cities. Many of their stores and Post Offices use a Littleton mailing address, but they sit outside the City of Littleton. This page lists only locations inside Littleton city limits.
