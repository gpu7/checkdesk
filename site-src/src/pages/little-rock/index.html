How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Little Rock city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Little Rock. Little Rock is an incorporated city inside Pulaski County, not a consolidated city-county. North Little Rock is a separate incorporated city in the same county. Many nearby addresses use a Little Rock mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Central Time.

FAQ

Which Little Rock Post Offices sell money orders?

All ten Little Rock locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Little Rock city limits, print a different city, or do not list money orders on the official page.

Which Little Rock Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

Two of the ten listed offices publish a weekday close after 5 p.m. and are labeled Open evenings: Westside (until 6 p.m.) and Chenal (until 5:30 p.m.). A 5 p.m. close does not count. The Little Rock Post Office at 600 E Capitol Ave publishes weekday hours until 5:30 p.m., but its official page does not list money orders, so it is not on this directory.

Which Little Rock Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Seven of the ten listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Asher, Brady, Southside, Forest Park, Industrial, Westside, and Chenal. The State Capitol, Hillcrest, and Otter Creek Post Offices are closed on weekends. None of the ten publishes Sunday retail hours.

Why is the Little Rock Post Office on Capitol Avenue not listed?

It prints Little Rock and sits inside Little Rock city limits, and it publishes weekday hours until 5:30 p.m. Its official page does not list money orders, so it is not on this money-order directory.

Why is the GMF Box Section not listed?

Its official page prints Little Rock, but the Census geocoder places the address and official map point in North Little Rock city, a separate incorporated city. An office printed Little Rock whose address or map point falls outside the city is held for review and not listed. The official page also does not list money orders.

Why are the Mabelvale and Alexander Post Offices not listed?

The Mabelvale office prints Mabelvale, but the Census geocoder places the address and official map point inside Little Rock city. The Alexander office prints Alexander; the address falls inside Little Rock city and the official map point falls in Alexander city. In both cases the printed city is not Little Rock, so they are held for review and not listed.
