Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Burlington Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Burlington, Vermont, and are inside Burlington city limits (the Census place Burlington city, GEOID 5010675, in Chittenden County): Burlington at 11 Elmwood Ave Lbby and North Burlington at 1127 North Ave Ste 40.
