Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Two Lake Charles Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Lake Charles, Louisiana, and geocode inside Lake Charles city limits (the Census place Lake Charles city, GEOID 2241155, in Calcasieu Parish): Drew at 5000 Lake St and Lake Charles at 921 Moss St. This is Lake Charles, Louisiana, in Calcasieu Parish, not Lakewood, Colorado, and not Moss Bluff or Sulphur. None is labeled Open evenings; weekday retail closes at 5:00 p.m. or earlier. Both publish Saturday hours, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and are labeled Open weekends. None publishes Sunday hours. A 5:00 p.m. close does not count as Open evenings. Both are listed on matching current and 2020 Census address files, and both official map points fall inside Lake Charles city. No map-point fallback was used. Two other offices print Lake Charles, LA and geocode in the city, but their official pages do not list money orders, so they are not listed: Eastside at 950 Main St, and the Lake Charles LA S&DC at 921 Moss St (same printed street as the listed Lake Charles Post Office). Moss Bluff at 172 E Telephone Rd prints Lake Charles and lists money orders, but all four Census legs fall in Moss Bluff CDP, not Lake Charles city, so it is not listed. No other office in the Louisiana locator sweep geocodes into Lake Charles city. No Lake Charles location names check cashing. Walmart Supercenters have not been checked yet, because the official walmart.com store locator was unavailable when this page was built. Hours on the Lake Charles pages are Central Time.
