Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Syracuse Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Syracuse, New York, and are inside Syracuse city limits (the Census place Syracuse city, GEOID 3673000, in Onondaga County): Colvin Elmwood at 2200 S Salina St, Downtown Syracuse at 444 S Salina St, Eastwood at 2509 James St, Federal Station at 100 S Clinton St, Franklin Square at 401 W Division St, Syracuse University at 720 University Ave, and Teall at 226 Teall Ave. This is Syracuse, New York, in Onondaga County, not Solvay, not the DeWitt or Mattydale census designated places, and not unincorporated Onondaga County.
