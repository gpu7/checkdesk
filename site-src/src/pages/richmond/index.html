Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. Seven Richmond Post Offices list money orders on their official pages, print Richmond, Virginia, and are inside Richmond city limits (the Census place Richmond city, GEOID 5167000, an independent city (county-equivalent GEOID 51760)): Capitol at 700 E Main St, Central Richmond at 205 N 2nd St, Forest Hill at 1021 Hioaks Rd, Northside at 3004 North Ave, Southside at 2101 Hopkins Rd, Stewart at 3517 Floyd Ave, and Westhampton at 805 Glenburnie Rd.
