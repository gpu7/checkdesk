Short version. For money orders, Post Offices charge $2.65 for $0.01 to $500.00 and $3.75 for $500.01 to $1,000.00, the same at every Post Office [1]. Bring cash or a debit card; USPS does not take credit cards for money orders. Cashing a USPS money order at a Post Office is free [1]. What we checked

Each row comes from that office's official USPS location page, fetched 2026-11-18. Money-order flags are only the tags printed on those pages (Domestic Money Orders and Inquiry Money Orders). Hours are the BUSINESS block on the same page, labeled Eastern Time. City limits are the Census place Columbus city, GEOID 3918000, checked on the Census geocoder current and 2020 files on 2026-11-18. Ranking and city-limits checks use the whole place, which includes parts of Franklin, Delaware, and Fairfield Counties. The listed offices are in Franklin County (GEOID 39049). OpenStreetMap was not used. Columbus is in the Eastern zone under 49 CFR 71.4 and 71.5. The Eastern–Central boundary in 49 CFR 71.5 runs through Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, and Tennessee; it does not mention Ohio and does not enter the state.
