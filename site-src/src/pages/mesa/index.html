How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside Mesa city limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder) and the official printed city is Mesa. Mesa is an incorporated city inside Maricopa County, not a consolidated city-county. Many nearby addresses use a Mesa mailing city or sit in another incorporated city, a census-designated place, or unincorporated county land, and those are not listed here.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours. Hours on this page are Arizona Time (Mountain Standard Time; Arizona does not observe daylight saving).

FAQ

Which Mesa Post Offices sell money orders?

All eight Mesa locations on this page list money orders on their official USPS location pages. USPS charges the same money-order fee at every Post Office. Other nearby Post Offices that sell money orders are not listed here because they sit outside Mesa city limits or print a different city.

Which Mesa Post Offices are open after 5 p.m.?

None of the eight listed offices publishes a weekday close after 5 p.m., so none is labeled Open evenings. A 5 p.m. close does not count.

Which Mesa Post Offices are open on Saturday or Sunday?

Five of the eight listed offices publish Saturday retail hours and are labeled Open weekends: Mesa, Mesa Desert, Mesa Dobson, Mesa Mountain View, and Mesa Superstition Springs. The Mesa Falcon Field, Mesa Four Peaks, and Mesa Sherwood Post Offices are closed on weekends. None of the eight publishes Sunday retail hours.
