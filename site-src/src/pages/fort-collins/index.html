How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder). Some addresses outside the city also use a Fort Collins mailing address.
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.

FAQ

Which Fort Collins Post Offices are open on Saturday?

Both Post Offices listed here publish Saturday retail hours: the Fort Collins Post Office (301 Boardwalk Dr) from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., and Old Town (301 S Howes St) from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Neither publishes Sunday hours.

Which Fort Collins Post Office is open after 5 p.m.?

The Fort Collins Post Office at 301 Boardwalk Dr publishes weekday retail hours until 6 p.m. Old Town closes at 5 p.m.

Do King Soopers stores in Fort Collins sell money orders?

Yes. All five King Soopers store pages listed here tag money orders. King Soopers does not publish a money-order fee; it says the cost will vary by state. The stores publish hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, but money desk hours are not published, so call ahead.

Where can I cash a check in Fort Collins?

King Soopers Midtown Marketplace (2535 S College Ave) is the only Fort Collins store page we checked that names check cashing. King Soopers does not publish a fee. See check cashing in Fort Collins.

Can I cash a check at Walmart in Fort Collins?

The official Fort Collins Walmart Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so we do not list them for it. Walmart's chain check-cashing page publishes a $4 maximum fee for pre-printed checks up to $1,000, but call the store to confirm its desk cashes checks before you go.
