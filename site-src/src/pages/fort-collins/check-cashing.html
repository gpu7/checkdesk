Where to cash a check in Fort Collins

Fort Collins location lists check cashing on its official store page so far: King Soopers Midtown Marketplace (2535 S College Ave). It publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

What to bring

  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [1].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. See Fort Collins Post Offices.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder).
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. King Soopers publishes store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I cash a check at night in Fort Collins?

King Soopers Midtown Marketplace publishes store hours until 11 p.m. every day. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [1].

Does King Soopers cash checks?

Yes, at Money Services desks in general: King Soopers' check-cashing page says it cashes payroll, government, tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person, up to $5,000, with no bank account required [1]. On this Fort Collins list, one location lists check cashing on its official store page: King Soopers Midtown Marketplace. Four other Fort Collins King Soopers pages do not name check cashing, so call ahead. The two Walmart Supercenter pages here do not name check cashing and are not listed here. King Soopers does not publish a dollar fee [1].

Can I cash a personal check at these Fort Collins locations?

One Fort Collins location on this list tags check cashing on its official store page: King Soopers Midtown Marketplace. King Soopers' official check-cashing page lists payroll, government, income tax refund, single-party insurance settlement, and business checks payable to a person (not checks payable to a business), and says fees and limits vary by state; it does not state a personal-check fee or cap on that page, so call the desk ahead [1]. The Fort Collins Walmart Supercenter pages do not name check cashing, so they are not on this list; Walmart's official check-cashing page lists two-party personal checks up to $200 with a $6 maximum fee at some locations, but call the store to confirm [2].

Are there 24-hour check cashing places in Fort Collins?

No location on this Fort Collins list publishes 24-hour hours. The one listed location (King Soopers Midtown Marketplace) publishes store hours past 5 p.m. every day, closing at 11 p.m. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so call ahead.

Does this page show check-cashing places that are open now?

No. Check Desk does not track live open or closed status. Published store hours were last verified Nov 11, 2026, and money-desk hours are not published separately from store hours. Confirm with the location before you go.

When is the King Soopers Money Services desk open?

King Soopers does not publish separate Money Services desk hours on its Fort Collins store pages. King Soopers Midtown Marketplace publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, and the chain's check-cashing page notes only that hours vary by location [1], so call ahead.
