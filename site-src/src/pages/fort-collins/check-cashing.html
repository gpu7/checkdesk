Where to cash a check in Fort Collins

Fort Collins location lists check cashing on its official store page so far: King Soopers Midtown Marketplace (2535 S College Ave). It publishes store hours of 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

Short version. King Soopers does not publish a check-cashing fee: "Fees and limits vary by state" [1]. It does not publish separate money-desk hours, so call ahead. Walmart publishes chain-wide check-cashing maximums [2], but the Fort Collins Walmart Supercenter pages show only a general "Money Services" label and do not name check cashing, so they are not on this list.

Other stores

The other King Soopers and Walmart Supercenter pages we checked in Fort Collins list a Money Services desk but do not name check cashing, so we have not put them on this list. King Soopers says its Money Services desks cash checks [1]; if one is closer, call the store and ask before you go. They are on the Fort Collins hub list.

What to bring

  • King Soopers: your Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number, plus one of: US passport, state ID, driver's license, Resident Alien ID, military ID, Matricula Consular ID, or Tribal Nation or Reservation ID [1].
  • Paid with a USPS money order? Any Post Office cashes it free with a primary photo ID [3]. See Fort Collins Post Offices.

How to read this list

  • Only checked locations. A location appears only if its official store page or official USPS location page was opened and it lists the service. We do not add a service because the chain offers it elsewhere.
  • City limits, not mailing city. A location is on this page only if its address is inside the city's limits (checked with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder).
  • Fees are chain policy. The fee column quotes the brand's official policy page. It is not a store's till price. "Not published, call ahead" means the brand does not publish a dollar figure.
  • Open evenings means the published hours run past 5 p.m. on at least one weekday. Open weekends means published hours on Saturday or Sunday. Both come only from published hours.
  • Store hours are not desk hours. King Soopers publishes store hours, not separate money-desk hours. Call ahead.

FAQ

Can I cash a check at night in Fort Collins?

King Soopers Midtown Marketplace publishes store hours until 11 p.m. every day. King Soopers does not publish separate money-desk hours, so the desk may close earlier. Call ahead.

Do I need a bank account to cash a check at King Soopers?

No. King Soopers says you don't need a bank account to cash a check at a Money Services desk [1].
